## 989 DR

Dwarven miners from Thunderholme accidentally expand their works into the lair of the shadow dragon Aurgloroasa, rousing her from her sleep. She dispatches the dwarves and caves in their tunnel to make it appear as an accident. She becomes interested in Thunderholme's operations and observes the dwarves via divination magic and spies summoned from the Plane of Shadow.

 Eldathyn priests fleeing their home in the Elven Court arrive in the Hullack Forest. These priests, carrying a relic called the Crystrum of Tranquility go on to purify the poisoned waters of the Wyvernwater in a ritual that claims the life of their high priestess.

## 982 DR

Rayuth, the Warlord of Silverymoon, passes away with old age. His son Tulven succeeds him, but he is proud and sadistic.

 Distasteful of Tulven, the famed war captain Aeroth of Silverymoon and his six sons depart Silverymoon and take the sword Taragarth with them. They ultimately settle in the Moonshae Isles.

 Alec Carrathal III, grandson of King Erfle of Callidyrr and his future successor, is born.

 The brothers Oswulf and Eldamar build their castle in the Dragonspine Mountains and the town of Verdigris grows up below it.

## 987 DR

Drow ambush a dwarven mining expedition traveling through tunnels of the Underdark beneath the Trackless Sea. During the fighting, a deep core fissure erupts, flooding a gallery with magma. Many are slain on each side, leaving only a handful of survivors, who each believe the other side has been wiped out. The battle becomes known as "Rockfire".

 Cut off from the route back to mainland Faerûn, the dwarf and drow survivors of the Battle of Rockfire separately journey westward, under the entire ocean. Finally making the surface in Maztica, the dwarves emerge into the harsh desert of the Sands of Itzcala, while the drow arrive to south in the foothills of the Axapoztlan Range.
