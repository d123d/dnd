## -946 DR

Shandaular, the capital city of Ashanath, is destroyed by the armies of the Nentyarch of Tharos.

 Leader of Shandaular, Arkaius dies sealing a portal that allow his people to escape.

 The people of Shandaular flee by means of the two-way portal around which their city is built to the Council Hills. They name themselves the Arkaiuns in honor of their fallen ruler.

 Nentyarch Thargaun unites all the small, conquered Nar kingdoms into the Empire of Narfell.

 The Shield of Weeping Ghosts begins.

## 940 DR

The Second Trollwar breaks out between the humans of Waterdeep and the trolls. It will last until 952 DR. The Waterdhavian defenders battle with troll gangs all over the Dessarin River valley.
 An orc horde from the Sword Mountains led by King Rauragh of Uruth Ukrypt attempts to attack Waterdeep via the Underdark, but it is destroyed by the gold dragon Palarandusk before it can even muster.

 Amphail the Just removes Gurt's greataxe from Morgur's Mound.

 Amphail the Just rules as War Lord of Waterdeep, but reigns only a year before falling in the Troll War. Six more warriors claim the title over the next twelve years, but all die in the war.
 The city of Hulburg is founded on the Moonsea. Cosimar Nanthon becomes its first harmach.

 Ashaba, the water wizard and first human lord of Shadowdale, is dying of old age and chooses to merge with the River Ashaba, so that he might live on as a part of his beloved land.

## 942 DR

__NOTOC__

 Amaara "Goldentresses" Nharimlur, former High Mage of Silverymoon, dies on the island of Evermeet.

 The Eleventh Serôs War comes to an end with the fall of the sea elf kingdom of Keryvyr, but Nyratiis, the would-be-coronal who triggered the war, flees and evades capture. Keryvyr's allies, Naramyr and Selu Maraar, remain intact.
 Drow raid cities all down the Sword Coast, carrying many human inhabitants of the Dessarin Valley off into slavery. The drow pillage and raze the tiny realms of Harpshield and Talmost beside the Ardeep Forest.

 Emurra Scaradath, the young protégé of the Moonseer of Waterdeep, Engalathae, rouses the Selûnite clergy of the city into sending a rescue mission into the Underdark to rescue her family and the Moonseer who were captured by drow slavers.

## 944 DR

The Ffolk are victorious in repelling a giant and firbolg incursion in Myrloch Vale. The Sword of Cymrych Hugh is carried off by surviving firbolgs.

 High King Tanner Cymrych of the Moonshae Isles is killed battling the firbolg chieftain.

 The Ffolk capitulate to the Illuskan invading settlers, ceding the northern Moonshae Isles to "the Northmen". This marks the end of the reign of the High Kings.
 The heir of the last High King Tanner, Niall, changes the family name from Cymrych to Carrathal as he assumes rule only over the realm of Callidyrr. Following suit, the various other noble families of the Moonshaes begin to change the spellings and pronunciations of their surnames to distance themselves from the Cymrych name, giving rise to the distinct Cambridge, Cyric, Kendrick, Kimball, Kincaid, Riker, and Selkirk families.
