## 916 DR

The Harpers and Lathanderites ambush Sammaster and his entourage in southern Cormanthyr during a trip to visit a pair of green dragons. An avatar of Lathander joins the fray and seemingly kills Sammaster, in the process creating a holy relic, the minor artifact known as the Blood of Lathander.

 Alagashon becomes the leader of the Cult of the Dragon, and leads it underground for the first time since its rise in the 800s DR. The Cult adopts a number of revenue-generating schemes to finance its operations.

 The shalarins of the Fourth Passing and previous generations establish the kingdom of Es'roch, continuing to expand As'arem in Serôs.

 The song dragon Essembramaerytha is born in Battledale.

## 913 DR

Xainlaphar, a member of the Red Wizards of Thay.

 A human war-leader named Rauthauvyr, "the Raven", forms a standing army to police the roads between settlements that would later become Sembia. Rauthauvyr pressures the elves into allowing a road to be built through their forests, joining the north-west shore of the Inner Sea with the Moonsea. These actions guarantee the growth and prosperity of these settlements. Finally, Rauthauvyr founds the nation of Sembia under his Raven Banner. However, he declines to govern it, instead instituting and serving "overmasters" elected by a merchant council.
