## 902 DR

In Chondath's bitter civil war, necromantic magic is wielded in battle on the Fields of Nun. This leads to a plague that decimates the country, giving the conflict the name "the Rotting War".
 Raiders from the city of Phlan attack Zhentilar soldiers, causing Zhentil Keep to act to defend itself.
 Melvaunt comes to Phlan's aid against the forces of Zhentil Keep and drives off its forces. Zhentil Keep declares war against both cities.

 The Rotting War causes Chondath to fracture into loosely allied city-states.
 Chondath renounces its claims on the Sembian city-states of Chondathan and Chauncelgaunt, which become independent.
 Under the cover of the Rotting War, Ormpetarr finally manages to secede from Chondath, having failed six times before.
 Tchazzar becomes the Sceptenar of Cimbar, then begins to extend his power along the coast, of what is then northwestern Unther.
 Tanner Cymrych becomes High King of the Moonshae Isles following the death of his father.

 High King Byron Cymrych II of the Moonshae Isles dies of illness.

 Sammaster's research into necromancy results in the Cult of the Dragons' first successful creation of a dracolich, Shargrailar. The rituals and components required are recorded in the Tome of the Dragon, which becomes a holy relic for future Cult cells.

## 906 DR

Phlan and Melvaunt lose a rapid succession of battles against the Zhentilar.
 The drow are forced out of the Twisted Tower by Dalesmen, Cormyrian agents, and a powerful wizard. The Cormyreans seize control of the the Ashaba Valley, which is named Shadowdale.

 Ashaba the water wizard is chosen as ruler over the new state of Shadowdale.
 Clearly having the upper hand, but worried about overstretching its power with the advent of Shadowdale, the first Phlan–Zhentil Keep war ends when Zhentil Keep forces Phlan and Melvaunt to sign the Treaty of the Ride and enter into an alliance known as the Triple Alliance.

## -900 DR

The Raumviran people found the realm of Raumathar. Its capital is Winterkeep. They subjugate the Tuigan tribes living in the northern Endless Waste.
 The fourth age of Calimshan begins with civil unrest, the aftermath of plague and an 8-year-old pasha sitting on the throne, controlled by an expansionist military council who have no care for non-military matters.

 Predicting a coming doom, the star elves of Yuireshanyaar commence construction of Sildëyuir, an extraplanar refuge.

## 90 DR

War breaks out between Najara and Ebenfar. Terpenzi, ruler of Najara, sends an army of ophidians to assault Ebenfar, but the armies of Ebenfar in turn destroy the city of Ss'thar'tiss'ssun. They ward the ruins and drive out the serpentfolk of Najara. Terpenzi rallies the remnants of Najara into battle against the Shadowking Verraketh, but are defeated and slain. Najara is conquered by the Shadowking of Ebenfar.

 The last battlefield is called the Fields of the Dead.

 Kaspler Obarskyr, king of Cormyr, dies.

 A group of yuan-ti from Ss'thar'tiss'ssun uncover a number of Netherese artifacts, including a spell book, from the ruins of Orogoth that date back to the ancient Sarrukh empires.

## 900 DR

King Galaghard III and the Glory of Cormyr army repeatedly defeat the armies of the Witch Lords in eastern Cormyr, at the forgotten temple, Juniril, Manticore's Crossing, and Wheloon. They drive the undead forces into the Vast Swamp. Though exhausted from months of campaigning, the Cormyreans finally achieve victory with the aid of the elf lord Othorion Keove in the nattle with the Witch Lords.
 Chondath enters into civil war.
 In Kara-Tur, the first fight between Lui-hsia Chih of the Many-Hued Peacock Society and Gorath of Royal Vagabonds occurs.

 In Silverymoon, the Vault of the Sages is constructed at its original location in Northbank.

 Over the next century and a half, humans settling in the Yuirwood mingle with the native green elves and birth a nation of half-elves, known as Cha-Tel'Quessir.

## -9000 DR

Vyshaan is thoroughly defeated and Aryvandaar dissolves, ending the Fifth Crown War. Keltormir and Illefarn survive the Crown Wars whole, but Shantel Othreier no longer exists. Illefarn joins Ardeep.
 Telantiwar drow conquer Bhaerynden (later the Great Rift), and occupy the dwarf city as their own.
 The eight subkingdoms of Shanatar try to expand their borders in competition with eachother. Skirmishes break out then erupt into open war, known as the Spawn Wars for the heavy use of deepspawn-bred warriors.

 Dwarven refugees of Bhaerynden flee to surrounding lands. One sizable group migrates to the Chultan Peninsula, then fractures into small tribes that become the wild dwarves.
 Following the end of Fifth Crown War, elves begin returning to the Elven Court and the eastern forests of Cormanthor. However, the High Forest is left unoccupied for an age to regain its peace.

 Circa: The Earthmother summons The Pack to the Moonshae Isles.
