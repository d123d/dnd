## 994 DR

On Midsummer, a minor wizard named Naerlus "Flamespell" Arnarthandyer becomes the first wielder of the Scepter of Mystra after it was given to him by Meldryn Jalensifer, the Magister at the time, under the direct instruction of Mystra herself.
 Naerlus uses the Scepter to establish himself as a petty tyrant along the coast north of Leilon. Meldryn attempts to retrieve the relic but Naerlus accidentally loses it before Meldryn can take it back. Adventurers find the Scepter in Calimshan a few months later and sell it. Winding up in the hands of five wizards, they battle for the relics ownership but lose it the same way Naerlus did.

 A wizard named Taluth casts a meteor swarm spell in Telflamm, tearing apart the surrounding buildings, after he spots a doppelganger in an alleyway.

## 996 DR

The famous ranger hero Yornar "the Tracker" disappears. Although signs of his passing are found for the next three years, he is never seen again and no remains are ever found. Followers of Mielikki believe that he still lives as the personal servant of the goddess.

## 997 DR

The Scepter of Mystra is found by a Calishite mage named Kloroth of the Seven Curses. While using it to build a portal, he is afflicted with an eighth curse that continually polymorphs him into different creatures. He loses the Scepter in Selgaunt and retreats into hiding. By dawn of the next day, it is back in the hands of Naerlus Arnarthandyer, who had fled from the enemies he'd made three years ago.

 Naerlus is killed by a mind flayer when he falls into a portal to Skullport.
 King Nearel of Tethyr dies.

 Nearel is succeed by his son Kortal.

## -990 DR

Plague strikes Calimport and Schamedar. Almost half of Calimport's slave population dies, and the syl-pasha and his family hide in their palace for the next two years until the plague burns out. Calishites suspect the warlords and sorcerers of Jhaamdath are behind the plagues, but this is never proved. Still, plagues periodically attack the Calishites over the next century.

 The dimension door spell is invented by Netherese arcanist Oberon with the name Oberon's extradoor.

## 991 DR

A series of earthquakes strike the kingdom of Sossal in far northeast Faerûn. These open fissures in some regions of the Great Glacier.

 From the fissures in the Great Glacier, powerful demons—trapped under the ice since the time of Narfell—are freed. Sossrim warriors led by Londraeth, their ruler of Sossal, battle and slay the demons.

## 993 DR

Prince Chelimber of the Principality of the Snarling Boar feuds with the Wizard of the Crag, and commissions the mage Taskor the Terrible of Irieabor to kill him. The mages clash in a massive spell-battle, in which summoned water elementals run free and flood the land, causing the ruin of the Principality.

 The flooding by water elementals creates the Marsh of Chelimber.

 Lizardfolk tribes of the northern Serpent Hills migrate east into the Marsh of Chelimber.

 Tarnhart Campion I, the King of Westgate, dies.
