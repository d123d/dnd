## 927 DR

Uktar 30: Red stormclouds engulf the Sword Mountains and rain blood for three days. This is the wrath of the orc god Yurtrus, and results in the Blood Plagues. The orc shaman Wund has prophesied this event.

 Wund unifies the orc tribes of the Sword Mountains under the rule of the chieftain Uruth. They found the orcish realm of Uruth Ukrypt.
 After nearly fifty years of excesses and war from several theocrat rulers, the new king of Westgate, Altarl Campion, bans temples from the city. Not long afterwards, the former temple residents build shrines on several hills just outside the city walls.

 The thieves' guild that will come to be known as the Shadowmasters of Telflamm is founded.

 Gostaraj, the King of Westgate, dies.

## 926 DR

While traveling to marry Prince Rhiigard of Cormyr, Princess Aliia Elethlim of Impiltur is lost when the succubus Soneillon sinks her ship, Nadyra's Glory, on the Sea of Fallen Stars.

 Following the death of Princess Aliia, the Elethlim Dynasty comes to an end and Impiltur is left without a leader. As powerful nobles struggled to win the crown, the kingdom breaks up into feuding city-states in a period known as the Kingless Years.
 Windyn Balindre of Impiltur relocates his company, the Royal Company of Shou Lung, to newly settled Telflamm, and declares himself a Merchant Prince.

## 922 DR

Ythazz Buvaar leads the Red Wizards in rebellion against the rule of Mulhorand. They sack and destroy Delhumide, the provincial capital. At the Battle of Thazalhar, a conjurer named Jorgmacdon summons the demon lord Eltab to the Red Wizards' aid, and the Mulhorandi army is routed.
 Half-drow Crinti from Dambrath raid Ammathlauir. After three days of battle, the hin and their marchwarden leaders, force back the raiders.
 Honda Mototsune battles Kozakuran invaders from Wa.

 Soneillon, Queen of Whispers, infects the Elethlim royal family of Impiltur with the Tortoiscab Plague. Only Princess Aliia survives.

 The Battle of Thazalhar confirms the independence of Thay from Mulhorand. This marks the end of the Second Mulhorand Empire.
 The city of Kensten gains independence from Mulhorand and is led by a mercantile council. Worship of Kossuth blossoms in the city after a millennium of Mulhorandi repression.

 The great wyrm black dragon Chardansearavitriol is transformed into a dracolich.
