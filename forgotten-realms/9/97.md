## 974 DR

Whipmistress Superior Jalrathae is defeated by the High Holy Temptress Lauraera Dlarayna after Jalrathae wrests control of a dozen temples of Loviatar using the Lash of Loviatar.
 King Mhoaran the Tusk-Bearded of Tethyr dies.

 Castle Waterdeep is completed on Higharvestide. It has been over a decade in construction, work having started in 963 DR. Laroun, the first female War Lord of Waterdeep, installs herself in the castle.
 In the Moonsea, the settlement that grew up around the stronghold of former adventurer Sulass Drowsbane is incorporated, becoming the city of Sulasspryn.
 Nearel succeeds his father Mhoaran as King of Tethyr.

## 971 DR

A Cult of the Dragon cell in the city of Surkh trespasses on the territory of a similar cult worshiping Tiamat, the Dragon Queen. The Tiamatans caused an abrupt halt to the Cult's expansion south.

 A Tashlutan priestess of Loviatar, Althatha Ammaeritus, recovers the Lash of Loviatar, which has been lost for forty-two years. Althatha proceeds to attack the Tashalaran 'pleasure houses' run by senior members of the clergy but is overwhelmed while storming the sixth such building.

## -9750 DR

Moon and gold elves from Shantel Othreier flee persecution by the  Vyshaan of Aryvandaar. They settle Riildath (present-day Rawlinswood and Forest of Lethyr), founding the realm of Lethyr. However, they discover Ilythiir's horrid works in Narathmault, and to protect their realm from such things, they determine to clear all non-elves from their forest.

## 972 DR

Derro tribes across the Northdark gather in the Uniting War against the duergar of Gracklstugh. They slay King Barthorn V. His successor, King Tarngardt VII, responds with a crusade to eradicate the derro. The duergar enslave several hundred derro and take them back to Gracklstugh.

 Following its defeat in Surkh by the church of Tiamat in 971 DR, halting its southward expansion, the Cult of the Dragon refocuses attention on the north. Over the next twenty-three years, the cult establishes at least ten new cells, but fails to infiltrate Silverymoon.
 Following their failure in Surkh, disgruntled Cult members begin openly criticizing the leadership of Algashon and the heavy involvement of the church of Bane in Cult activity. The mage Tuelhalva Drakewings emerges as a prominent opponent of Bane's role in the Cult.
