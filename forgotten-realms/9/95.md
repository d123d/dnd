## 955 DR

As an orc horde gathers in the Spine of the World, the mages of the Covenant muster an army from the human realms of the North to face them. However, in an action dubbed the "Orcgates Affair", the Red Wizards of Thay use great portals to magically transport the orcs far to the south. The North is spared, but the Covenant are embarrassed by the orc's failure to show.

 The sudden appearance of a massive horde of orcs spells doom for the town of Undelos, east of the Ankhwood. The Sacred Throne of Skulls, one of the main temples of the Church of Loviatar at the time, and the leader of the church itself, the Highmost Lash Naneethrama Luin, are wiped out.

## 952 DR

The Second Trollwar between the humans of Waterdeep, allied with cities of the North, and the trolls of the Evermoors comes to an end. First, the Waterdhavian defenders drive the trolls back from their walls. The human realms then unite under Aeroth, War Captain of Silverymoon; Ahghairon of Waterdeep; and Samular Caradoon of Tyr, and together they eradicate trolls across the Evermoors. The might of the trolls is ultimately broken during a nine-day fight known as the Long Battle. Renwick Caradoon, the wizard brother of Samular, falls in the final battle but rises as an archlich. Ahghairon the mage used his magical prowess to turn the war against the trolls.

 In Thesk, the settlement of Phsant is established.
 In Waterdeep, House Belabranta is ennobled, likely due to the actions of Ilithrew Belabranta during the Trollwars.
