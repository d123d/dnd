## -9 DR

The Circle of Flames cabal forms in Cormanthor. It comprises many retired ﻿Akh'Faer wizards and their apprentices.
 Tethyr's Clan Fyrson has a civil war, pitting the royalists led by Tavis Fyrson II against the traitors led by his brother Volsun. Volsun wins, killing Tavis, and later in the year captures and ransoms the heirs of Clan Bormul and Clan Karlag, declaring Clan Fyrson rebels against the crown.

 Volsun Fyrson, nephew of Black Alaric, takes up his uncle's moniker and comes to lead the Nelanther pirates, merging his clan's resources with theirs.
