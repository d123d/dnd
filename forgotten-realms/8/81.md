## 812 DR

The rebuilding of the ruined city of Illusk is largely finished, and construction commences on a set of defensive walls. Illusk and the realm of Stornanter prosper off trade with the mines of Mirabar.

 The Mask of Mysteries is found on the face of a corpse buried on the Dragonisle, thought to be that of Arnthas "The Sly", who posed as a friend to the elves of Myth Drannor before its fall only to steal treasure and magical secrets. Sembian adventurers calling themselves the Scarlet Song take it off the corpse and bring it, and a book describing its function, home with them.

## -810 DR

During a firestorm visible across Kara-Tur, red lighting burns 1-meter-high (3.2 feet) letters into the solid granite of the stone bluffs above the mouth of the Hungtse River in Shou Lung. No people or plants by Hungtse are damaged. Revealed in the cliffs are the teachings of the Path of Enlightenment. The emperor's ancestors initially deny these teachings.

 The sahuagin kingdom of Aleaxtis is founded in the Alamber Sea by the sahuagin T'Raam.
