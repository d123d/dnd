## 830 DR

The city of Shantil in Dambrath is besieged by drow, while King Reinhar IX and his forces are trapped on the peninsula south of the city.

 Kartak Spellseer is arrested at Castle Spulzeer and tried and convicted of several foul murders, ruining his family's name. He is sentenced to hang. Kartak manages to escape his fate when a wizard named Rysellan the Dark and several associates break Kartak and fifteen other convicts out of jail with a ring of the ram.

## -836 DR

The Battle of Infinite Darkness is fought at the mouth of Fenghsintzu river between the forces of Shou Lung under General Wo Can and the combined southern tribes under Black Leopard Cult witch doctors. The battle is fought under darkness spells, and the witch doctors summon strange beasts from around the world to fight for them. However, Wo Can is victorious and routs the tribes.

 The famed scholar Po Nih dies in the battle, killed by a spear while writing a report for the temple of Durkon.

## -830 DR

General Wo Can of Shou Lung and his army destroy the Black Leopard Cult temple at the mouth of the Fenghsintzu River in Durkon. This quells resistance in the southern provinces. The temple sinks deep into the earth, and the Cult witch doctors are killed or escape to far-off lands. Legend tells that a high priest traveled unseen along the Fenghsintzu and then out to sea.

## 831 DR

Betrayed by half-elf priestesses of Loviatar, King Reinhar IX and the Arkaiun people are defeated by the drow of T'lindhet in the Battle of Crimar. Cathyr, leader of the Loviatan priestesses, kills Reinhar herself.

 Cathyr submits to the drow, agreeing to rule Dambrath as a client realm of T'lindhet. She renames the capital city after herself and becomes the first queen of Dambrath.
