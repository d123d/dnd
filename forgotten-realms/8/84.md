## 841 DR

Unbeknownst to both of them, Laeral the Witch-Queen of Stornanter feuds with her sister Syluné Silverhand. The goddess Mystra visits both sisters and offers to make them her Chosen. They leave Faerûn and become planewalkers for a while.

 After Laeral departs her realm, the avaricious nobles fight to seize power, quickly leading to the fall of Stornanter.
 Uthaedeol the Blooddrenched, a priest of Bhaal, assassinates King Samyte of Tethyr in Castle Dasaajk, together with his sons, ending the short-lived Mallorhen Dynasty. Alisande, the second daughter of King Strohm V, is crowned queen and starts the Bormul Dynasty, commonly known as the "Queens' Dynasty."
