## 826 DR

Eljak Ferenfal, the Horned Ranger, discovers the body of a Rashemaar berserker while hunting winter wolves across the ice-bound Bluefang Water. Among the corpse's possessions is an impressive greatsword, later identified as the Greatsword of Impiltur. Eljak presents the sword and a cloak made from the pelt of a winter wolf to King Halanter II.

 The great red dragon Mairogra dies. Her mantle of Dragon Queen, along with the artifact known as the Crown of the Mountain, passes on to the benevolent gold dragon Valamaradace.
 High King Byron Cymrych I of the Moonshae Isles dies of natural causes.

 Kevan Cymrych becomes High King of the Moonshae Isles.
