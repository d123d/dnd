## 882 DR

Nimoar the Reaver gathers an army and conquers Ulbaerag Bloodhand's tribe. They settle at Bloodhand Hold and renamed it Nimoar's Hold, on the site that would later become Waterdeep.
 In Ascalhorn, demons defeat the devils and conquer the old elven citadel, which becomes known as Hellgate Keep. The elven kingdoms of Eaerlann and Ammarindar fall to the swarm of demons that emerges.
 The surviving monsters let loose by the Netherese of Ascalhorn overwhelm the dwarf realm of Ammarindar.
 The city of Zhentil Keep launches a military campaign to deal with the growing problem of renegade liches roaming the city at night (also known as the period of the "Night Plague").

 The realm of Ardeep is resettled and rebuilt by moon elf refugees from Eaerlann. They ally with the dwarves of the Forlorn Hills and humans living along the Delimbiyr, in an alliance called the Fallen Kingdom (the same name as Phalorm), but this quickly collapses owing to suspicions about human involvement in the fall of Ascalhorn.
 In Citadel Sundbarr, the dwarves allow the human refugees of the demons to settle in their surface strongholds.
 The king of Maurmurra in the Vilhon Reach is slain by Kalgrathur Daycloaks, leader of the Nightfire Lances mercenary army. Kalgrathur names himself High Lord of Maurmurra.

 Prince Simberuel Astalmé of Ascalhorn was killed by vrocks while protecting the dwarven Forgemaster of Sundbarr in the Turnstone Pass.

 Ghaurin, scion of the adventurous dwarven Stoneheart clan tries to defend his ancestral home from the fiends out of Ascalhorn. After praying to Moradin to save the mansion, an earthquake seals off the underground portion and Ghaurin himself is transformed into a sentient stone golem.
 The bard Maerstar, one of the few humans to have escaped the massacre that occurred at Ascarlhorn, composes a song that rechristens the fortress as Hellgate Keep.

## 889 DR

The Tethyrian Bull Elk tribe attacks and sets Nimoar's Hold (later Waterdeep) ablaze, but ultimately they are driven off by the inhabitants. The settlement is rebuilt in less than a year, before the first snow of winter.

 Duke Tithkar Illehhune joins the Knights of the Shield and convinces his fellows that his Shield of the Hidden Lord is a sign to return the organization to its former power. The even more secretive Shield Council is formed, which comes under the direct influence of Gargauth, who speaks through the Shield of the Hidden Lord.

## 888 DR

The sorcerer Clymph has demons erect a tower named for him in the Great Dale.

 The "Storm Prophet" Tothur is killed in Kormul in a battle known as the Struggle of Storms. Tothur kills forty arch-priests of Bhaelros/Talos before he is brought down and the power of the Talassan church is shattered for the next three centuries as a result. The relic that gave him his powers, the Chanting Chain, disappears into the Underdark.

## 883 DR

Tanisell the Cloaked, a human hailing from the fiend-overrun Ascalhorn (now called Hellgate Keep), is elected as High Mage of Silverymoon.

 Wulgreth escaped Hellgate Keep and seeks sanctuary in the ruins of Karse. There he endeavors to access the immortal power of the momentary god Karsus, but his servant Jhingleshod murders him. Wulgreth's death releases magical forces that turn him into a lich and create the Dire Wood.

 The Hall of Mists and the Grandfather Tree are infested by a colony of giant mutant red ants that were spawned by an eddy of magical chaos emanated from Hellgate Keep.

## 887 DR

Pirates from the South make heavy raids on Nimoar's Hold, which would later become Waterdeep. All the attacks fail as Nimoar's followers beat off three separate attempts.

 The mad Sammaster publishes the first of his error-laden re-translations of many ancient prophecies, including his version of Maglas's The Chronicle of Things to Come. From his flawed translation, Sammaster grows convinced that dead dragons would come to rule the world, a belief he dubbed the "Scaly Way".
