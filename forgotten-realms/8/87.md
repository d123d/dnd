## 875 DR

Algashon persuades Sammaster to assault Alustriel Silverhand, his former lover and fellow Chosen of Mystra. In the initial battle, Alustriel is injured and summons Khelben Arunsun and Laeral Silverhand, also Chosen, for help. Mystra strips Sammaster of his Chosen powers, but Algashon rescues him from death. Sammaster turns entirely mad and evil.

 King Galaghard I (in the guise of his grandson, Draxius) dies in Cormyr at the age of 441.

 Bryntarth, son of Draxius/Galaghard I, becomes king of Cormyr.
