## 852 DR

Bryntarth, future king of Cormyr is born.

 An Illuskan invasion fleet arrives in the Moonshae Isles. The High King's defensive flotilla is destroyed.

 High King Dolan Cymrych of thr Moonshae Isles drowns with his sunken fleet. The Crown of the High King is lost with him in Whitefish Bay.

 Prince Conn Cymrych replaces his father Dolan as High King, but lacking his crown of office and facing down a seemingly inevitable defeat to the Illuskans.

## 850 DR

King Beldred of Impiltur wielding the sword Dornavver, slays and drives the demons out of the Rawlinswood in a year-long campaign known as "the Scouring".

 High King Kevan Cymrych of the Moonshae Isles dies of natural causes.

 Dolan Cymrych becomes High King of the Moonshae Isles.

 The Ilmatari priest Alledec creates Three White Doves, an exceptionally powerful magical mace.

## 851 DR

Foreseeing the death of one of her Chosen, the goddess Mystra appears to the archmage Sammaster and makes him a new Chosen of Mystra. Sammaster later meets Elminster to learn how to handle his new powers, including silver fire.

 Sea elves found the city of Selu'Maraar in the Dragon Reach. It is shielded from persecution from the other states of Serôs by distance and the plentiful fishing boats and sailing ships on the surface. The sea elves make little contact with other nations of Serôs or the surface world, bar Keryvyr.

 Thartryn Ilistar III, a future King of Westgate, is born.
