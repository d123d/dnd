## -800 DR

Twelfth Rysar of Rystall Wood commences.

 Synnorha Durothil becomes a baelnorn and becomes guardian of the Library of the Durothils, beneath their original villa.

 Forty Dukars leave the organization and reform as the Pamasi Order, devoting themselves to calling for war against Aryselmalyr for its increasingly harsh treatment of the Dukars and wizardry in general.
 The Dukars themselves merely aim to embarrass Coronal Vaeqiis II and enhance awareness of the plight of the ordinary people of Serôs, and not just its wizards.

## -8000 DR

In Serôs, by this time all sahuagin communities west of the Sharksbane Wall have been wiped out.

 Circa: Dwarves from Shanatar, cut off from their homes due to the Mindstalker Wars, find Underdark tunnels beneath the Sea of Swords which lead beneath the Moonshae Isles. In the coming years, they will establish the realm of Dwarvenhome in the caverns found under the islands.

 The elves of Cormanthyr found Semberholme, intending it to be a safe haven for their women, children and elders during war and disaster.

## 800 DR

The city of Thentia is founded by the Moonsea.
 In Shou Lung, the last Emperor Chin of the Kao Dynasty, along with his family, dies in a fire that razes the old Imperial City. Ton Bor, the emperor's third cousin, is named the first Emperor Chin of the Yin Dynasty, meaning "Silver". Instead, Shou history records it as the La Dynasty, meaning "Wax" for its malleability. Rival factions and secret societies influence the La emperors, and it is time of rampant corruption and poor management.

 Circa: Impilturan settlers build their homesteads on the eastern side of the Easting Reach. From there, they expand south and inland around the Forest of Lethyr for the next fifty years.

 In Myth Nantar, the shields around the Academy of the Dukars, having slowly expanded since the city's collapse in 720 DR, now cause the city's mythal to become impenetrable to all matter and magic, permitting only water, corals, fish, and sea stars. When it is touched, the mythal appears to emit a haunting song. The legend arises that the city and the Dukars are cursed until they perform for all the races of Serôs. Over the five centuries, fewer and fewer people come to Myth Nantar.

 Sammaster First-Speaker, the future founder of the Cult of the Dragon, is born. However, the whereabouts and exact date are lost to history.

## 803 DR

From drow raids in Dambrath, King Reinhar IX sends an expeditionary force into the mines of the Gnollwatch Mountains and the Underdark beneath, to attack the drow raiders in their own homes. The battle is a catastrophe for the Dambrathans, and the routed humans are swiftly forced back to the surface.

 In Shou Lung, the perception grows that the La Dynasty is unable to govern the empire and that most garrisons are riddled with secret societies like the resurgent Black Leopard Cult. The Yang clan seizes control over the Tyu Ton garrison, replacing the soldiers of Shou Lung with ones loyal to the Yang clan.

## 806 DR

Laeral the Witch-Queen founds the realm of Stornanter in the North, establishing her capital at Port Llast. Understanding the strategic importance of the ruins of Illusk, Laeral commences efforts to rebuild and resettle the city.

 In Illusk, Laeral explores the Host Tower of the Arcane and comes across the surviving liches of the Grand Cabal. She then erects magical barriers around the tower to forbid entry.
