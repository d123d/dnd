## Jysstev

House Jysstev was an Amnian house.

History
The house backed the Jhannivvar pretender for the throne of Tethyr and subsequently lost its riches, living in near poverty. It was forced to sell its assets in Athkatla and Murann, and move to Eshpurta. The house remained well ranked, but only through debts of honour.

Members
 Qar Jysstev, Namarch on the Council of Six and lord of the house.

## Jymahna

Jymahna was a renowned human concubine in Calimport, that later was transformed into a lich by Shangalar the Black and joined his organization the Twisted Rune.

Possessions
In undeath she typically adorned herself in golden gowns and wrappings, as well as a magical silver funerary mask. The exact properties of this mask were unknown to others, as it was a secret coveted by Jymahna and Shangalar, but a wide array of speculation surrounded it (see below).

Relationships
Jumahna had ties with various caravans, making her an important reservoir of news from distant lands. Her long distant contacts, as well as her spies covering much of the same space, was cause for some enmity from her fellow Twisted Rune member Sapphiraktar.

Rumors & Legends
A wide variety of speculation and tales told in taverns surrounded Jymahna and her mysterious mask. Some speculated that the silver mask Jymahna wore gave her the power to cast suggestion at will, charm person three times per day, and that it constantly exuded the friends spell in a  area around her. Other suggested that Jymahna lived on within the mask itself and that she would possess anyone that adorned. Still others believed that Jymahna wore the mask because bearing witness to her face would drive a person to madness, though such people hotly debated whether this madness was out of fear or infatuation with her beauty.

## Jyordhan

Jyordhan was a Zhentarim agent and the Lord of Shadowdale between 1339 DR and his death in 1345 DR.

History
When Aumry was assassinated by Zhentarim agents in 1339 DR, Jyordhan captured and killed these assassins, claimed the Pendant of Ashaba, and presented himself as the new lord, to which the people of Shadowdale agreed. Jyordhan moved the seat of power in Shadowdale from the traditional Twisted Tower of Ashaba to Castle Krag, and the location was soon full of Zhentarim agents. A revolt by the people triggered the dispatch of peacekeeping forces from Zhentil Keep, and although Syluné Silverhand was aware of Jyordhan's true nature, she sought to maintain order in the dale rather than try to overthrow him.

Jyordhan was killed in 1345 DR when he ambushed Khelben "Blackstaff" Arunsun on leaving Shadowdale, although official accounts state that Jyordhan was visiting Khelben in Waterdeep when he passed away from an illness. Jyordhan had nominated Lyran of Melvaunt as a successor to the lordship, but without the Pendant of Ashaba no claim could be made.

## Jym Serpentil

Jym Serpentil was a papermaker inhabiting Waterdeep during the 15 century DR.

Description
Jym worked at Serpentil Books & Folios, a shop specialized in selling maps, ink and paper. He created paper in the back of the store, while his father dealt with customers.
