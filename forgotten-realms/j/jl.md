## Jlacathra Vorn

Jlacathra Vorn was a powerful sorceress in ancient Myth Drannor.

History
Jlacathra was one of the most powerful mages of the city before its fall. However, during the Weeping War, believing it impossible that some fiends could cause the ruination of the city, she disappeared from the war. Instead, she focused on creating a very powerful spell together with the Circle of the Flame cabal. At the moment of the Myth Drannor's fall, Jlacathra was planewalking and nobody saw her again.

However, some time after the city's fall, the Circle of Swords became operative. The magic was kept in the custody of a unique magical construct, the Floating Face.

In the Year of the Tankard, 1370 DR, somehow the magic activated, calling to it some adventurers via their dreams.

## Jlonkin

Jlonkin the Bowyer was one of the few inhabitants of Fallenfalcon, a thorp along the Bowshot road in the wild realm of Owlhold in the Border Kingdoms.

History
By the late 14 century DR, Jlonkin and his family lived in one of the two cottages close to the main pavilion in Fallenfalcon. The bowyer and his family provided travelers who stayed at the camping pavilion with fresh bread and soup, offering bigger meals upon request ahead of time.
