## J. Robert King

J. Robert King is a novelist and game designer who has contributed to a variety of sourcebooks from TSR, Inc.

Career
King has written over twenty novels, including three of the books in the Double Diamond Triangle Saga. He also contributed to Monstrous Compendium Forgotten Realms Appendix II and Aurora's Whole Realms Catalogue in addition to other campaign settings from TSR.

## Jren Prencertal

Sergeant Jren Prencertal was a strongheart halfling warrior of the militia in the village of Shining Falls in the Delimbiyr Vale in the mid-to-late 14 century DR.

Activities
Alongside Nemmet Viccstray and Hethat Deegrell, she served Sheriff Vendal Viccstray.

## Jromin

Jromin was an elf and priest of Curna who inhabited the Shining South during the mid-to-late 14 century DR.

Activities
Jromin lived within the Ajmer Forest in Durpar where he served as a spiritual leader for his people. His fellows credited him for the fact that there were no druids to be found within the forest, believing Jromin would not allow anyone that sloppy within the wooded region. Regardless of if that was true or not, Jromin was highly protective of the forest and secretly monitored anyone who entered.

Possessions
He carried a quarterstaff.

Relationships
Jromin despised the wizard Crimonil.
