## Ohlmer Cormaeril

Ohlmer Cormaeril was the head of the Cormaeril noble house in Cormyr in the mid-to-late 14 century DR.

Activities
Although he appeared to be a respectable nobleman, he engaged in kidnapping for slavery, smuggling, and trade with pirates. He had been known to mistreat young female slaves.

History
He was found slain in his bed in the, after Gaspar Cormaeril assumed control of the house for himself.

## Ohanzee

Ohanzee was a sorcerer living in Unther in 1374 DR.

Activities
Ohanzee lived at the Altar of Scales temple of Tiamat in Unthalass. He helped in Malise's experiments in exchange for funds. Usually, all his familiars died because Ohanzee forgot to care for them.

Personality
Ohanzee cared little about Tiamat or her temple. He was a bitter man with a strong sense of self-importance and a cruel steak. He enjoyed hurting people but always protected himself.

## Ohlo

Ohlo was a human mage who lived in the slums of Phlan circa 1340 DR.

Description
Ohlo made his residence in an elegantly paneled space, which had pieces of furniture in it. He often sat by his table close to the eastern wall, writing things down. He dressed in typical wizard's attire: a purple robe and a pointed hat.

Personality
He only seemed to care for anyone whom he had a use for. Otherwise, he would look sternly at them, or even attack if someone "presumed too much".

Possessions
The mage owned a wand of magic missiles and a large amount of coin.

History
Despite a section of Phlan becoming civilized, Ohlo decided to stay in the eastern part of the slums, where he commanded a large group of orcs and bugbears. In 1340 DR, he ordered a special potion from the Old Rope Guild, but could not fetch it due to other duties. This potion had the power to reduce the age, whilst at the same time increasing the strength, of the imbiber. Thus, Ohlo asked a new group of adventurers to fetch the potion for him from a booth in the southeast of the guild. The confirmation word to the vendor was his own name. If they returned with the potion and handed it over, the mage planned on rewarding the party with platinum, jewelry, and an enchanted piece of equipment, otherwise he planned on attacking them.

## Ohn

Baron Ohn was a ruler of Han Chao in Tabot.

History
When soldiers of Shou Lung invaded Tabot in, the priests and the barbarians of the land were forced to ally against them. Baron Ohn of Han Chao led his warriors and joined with the great sohei Ramara and the monks and together they repelled the Shou in the Battle of Tsagang.

Afterward, Ohn and Ramara negotiated for ten days and established the Peace Land Treaty between the tribes and the monks. It legitimized the monks' hermitages (now mountain keeps) and gave them a role in the defense of the lowland tribes and thereby cemented land and peace. They also founded the city of Tsagang at the site and founded the new nation of Tabot. All present blessed the union and it came to be a holy place and a holy word.

## Ohland Grethgar

Ohland Grethgar was the Magister from  to .

Appearance
He was handsome and well-spoken.

Personality
He had been described as both ambitious and ruthless, the latter trait coming after the death of his second master.

Abilities
Ohland Grethgar had devised a bone hook spell that allowed him to pursue a target wherever they fled, no matter how far they went or whether they were polymorphed. During his time with the Trapmaster, he developed magic to alter locks, and to create doors that were protected by beams of magic to stab intruders. He had gained a reputation in magefairs for the small but useful spells he developed.

History
Ohland was born in Westgate to poor parents; he spent a good portion of his childhoold amongst the logging crofts in the Starmantle area, where he met his first master, Iriskree. He performed minor tasks for both her and his family; his reputation spread, and he was soon offered an apprenticeship by a mage known only as the Trapmaster. The Trapmaster perished in a spell duel with Jaelaera Sundeirnyn; she then turned on him, and he barely lived. He resolved to become stronger then so as to meet every challenger.

He continued the Trapmaster's trade, demonstrating some spells at magefairs, and soon caught the eye of the Simbul. She saw him as a kindred spirit, and offered to turn him into one of the mightiest mages if he would slay the one mage she was forbidden from killing, the Magister Uldinus Lawkland. He agreed, growing in power dramatically under her eye. After slaying several Calishite mages of great cruelty, he set a trap for the Magister, laying a rumor of a crypt full of gorgeous Netherese sorceresses in stasis, surrounded by a hoard of items only they knew how to use. Disguising himself as one of the women, he emerged from hiding and issued a formal challenge as soon as the Magister appeared. Though the Magister fled immediately, Ohland's bone hook spell allowed him to follow precisely, and quickly slay him.

During his tenure as the Magister, Ohland taught the rudiments of magic to people up and down the Sword Coast, blasting challengers whenever they came to him. Unfortunately, he perished over Orlumbor when he was challenged by the black dragon Starlaurynguldar, who was protected with some sort of antimagic amulet; though the device did not survive, Ohland died as well. The dragon perished of its wounds shortly after, and thus, the Mantle of the Magister was offered to Inhil Lauthdryn.

Rumors
There were suspicions that Ohland had also been the Simbul's lover during his time as her disciple.

## Ohlo's Mystic Devices

Ohlo's Mystic Devices was a small shop that dealt in magical miscellany in the city of Phlan of the Moonsea region in the mid-to-late 14 century DR.

History
As of the, Ohlo's Mystic Devices had a signage over its door inviting customers inside. At the time, the proprietor of the establishment was a young, yet distinguished woman. Her inventory consisted of cleric and wizard scrolls, potions of healing, potions of speed, a precious wand of magic missiles, rings and cloaks of protection, and a set of bracers of defense.
