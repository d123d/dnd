## Ozomar Do'Urden

Ozomar Do'Urden, formerly Ozomar Druu'giir, was the house wizard of House Do'Urden.

History
Ozomar was formerly a member of House Druu'giir. He attended Sorcere, the drow academy of magic, and was a "promising graduate". After the death of his rival (and the Do'Urden house wizard) Nalfein Do'Urden, Ozomar happily defected to House Do'Urden to serve as house wizard, presumably until its destruction in 1339 DR.

## Ozzo

Ozzo was a charmed ogre giant who lived in the fortress of Krak al-Niraan in 1367 DR.

Activities
Jamal al-Yindannim used his powers to charm Ozzo into serving the Brotherhood of the True Flame as a brutishly dangerous guard. Ozzo inhabited a large chamber found near the postern of the keep. Anyone entering this area unaccompanied by Jamal, whom Ozzo considered to be his best friend, risked being violently smashed.

Ozzo was carefully monitored by 24 fedayeen at all times, perhaps as a safeguard in case the magic keeping the large brute under control failed. Any intruder lucky or skilled enough to breach Ozzo's chamber was attacked by 23 of the fedayeen while the remaining guard alerted the rest of the keep.

## Ozamata Ku Murawa

Ozamata Ku Murawa was a human thief, one of the Underbarons of Bral, the leader of House Murawa, and the founder of the Yakuza thieves' guild on the Rock of Bral in the mid-to-late 14 century DR. He was also a member of the Council of Captains and was the richest and most powerful Shou on the Rock.Although described as Shou in the Rock of Bral sourcebook, Ozamata's name and culture suggest he is in fact Wanese, from Wa, who are also active in Realmspace but sometimes merged with the Shou.

Personality
Underbaron Murawa was described as a harsh man.

Description
Aged 50, Ozamata was a short, potbellied man who wore his hair in the traditional Shou style.

Possessions
Ozamata wore an obi of protection around his kimono and bracers of defense on his forearms. He carried a magical wakizashi and a war fan.It is possible that this "war fan" was instead a gunsen, a defensive weapon. He also possessed a vial containing oil of slipperiness and three pinches of dust of sneezing and choking.

House Murawa was one of the largest buildings in the Low City. It was located west of Shou-town on Dock Street and overlooked the docks at the leading edge of the Rock.

Abilities
The Underbaron was an accomplished thief.

Activities
Ozamata led the powerful trading family of House Murawa and the thieves guild of the yakuza, both of which he founded. In addition, many of the Shou living on the Rock of Bral considered him the leader of their small community, but Kurishi Otobe, known as the Dragon Lady, was one Shou on Bral who opposed his organizations, while Lord Chan Fu Wi, ambassador of Shou Lung, ignored them both.

Ozamata's yakuza ruled Shou-town and controlled the Docks and much of the Low City. They engaged in extortion, racketeering, and gambling and were marked by their tattoos. Ozamata commanded around 40 yakuza, but could recruit up to 60 thugs if needed.

History
Hailing from Kara-Tur, Ozamata Ku Murawa was among the first Shou to explore wildspace. When he came to the Rock of Bral, he decided to settle in the city there.

Over time, he established both his trading company and his thieves' guild, and he fought to rise to a place of leadership on Bral's Council of Captains, those who owned ships and land on the Rock of Bral. By the 1360s DR, he was the wealthiest and most influential Shou human in Bral.

## Ozama

Ozama was a hot-tempered and magic-hungry wizard responsible for cursing and trapping her fiancé Jarial in a boulder in 1353 DR.

Description
She had beautiful violet eyes that burned with passion.

Personality
Ozama had a sharp tongue and blinding temper.

Abilities
Ozama possessed an immense talent for magic.

Ozama often conjured a magical dagger that had a parasitic effect on its targets, that ice dagger looked uncannily similar to the enchanted dagger called the Borea's Blood with only difference, Borea's Blood was of ice-like blue color.

Possessions
At the time of her death, Ozama was wearing her enchanted snakeskin boots that protected her from poisons and venoms and a gray woolen cloak.

History
In 1353 DR, Ozama met a young sorcerer Jarial and they quickly fell madly in love becoming fiancés. She convinced Jarial to travel with her to the ruins of Myth Drannor in search for a dark naga collector of magic named Preybelish who could lead them to the artifact called the Wizard’s Torc. Eventually the couple had a fiery argument over something trivial as Jarial renounced his love for the sorceress. In heat of the moment, Ozama ensorcered a pebble and threw it to Jarial's feet. The pebble grew and swallowed the sorcerer's lower torso, trapping him in the Dwarven Dungeons of the ruined elven city. Ozama left him with a riddle that was the solution to the curse, and despite Jarial's begging, she stormed off, leaving him alone within a dark and empty underground chamber.

Ozama fully intended to return to Jarial after the man learned his lesson, but she met her doom in the Dungeons overwhelmed by monsters. Sixteen years later, in 1369 DR a group of adventurers fighting to stop the Cult of the Dragon's plans on corrupting Myth Drannor's mythal came across the entombed sorcerer and easily freed him. Jarial joined the group that saved him from the torturous existence, eventually finding Ozama's skeleton surrounded by perished monsters.

## Ozgor Hyelvel

Ozgor Hyelvel was the Magister from  to .

Abilities
Ozgor employed a number of black lightning spells, and could cast spells that brought down towers. He also could cast meteor swarm.

Activities
Ozgor's entire life was akin to a military campaign, where he continuously moved from fight to fight.

History
At the time he was offered the office of the Magister, Ozgor was in the process of attacking a town (destroyed by the late 14 century DR) in the Vilhon Reach, on behalf of its neighboring town's tyrant. By the turn of that year, Ozgor was slaying his employer at the moment, on behalf of one who had outbid him.

He continued to ravage and pillage as the Magister, building up stores of enchanted wares and weapons to which he returned when in need. However, his caches never contained money, for his campaigns always left him short on those.

Ozgor perished when he assaulted the keep of Velgorn Kataclath and his fellow adventurers. Velgorn employed magic to send a piece of the keep, brought down by Ozgor's spell, on a circular trajectory which struck Ozgor from behind while he was busy in a spell duel with him.

## Oznod Brekker

Oznod Brekker was a human fighter in the service of Lord Vorik Aris of Cormyr, who operated in the Stonelands around 1369 DR.

Personality
Oznod was a clever and quick-witted man, though not as charming as he hoped.

Possessions
He carried a +1 longsword and wore chain mail, and had a potion of healing for emergencies. His duties across the Stonelands were aided by a light warhorse.

Activities
In the, he served Lord Aris as leader of a group of six scouts in the region around Wyvernwatch Castle. His mission was to find treasure hunters in Halfhap that intended to explore the area, and kill them before they stumbled upon Wyvernwatch, where Lord Aris was secretly building up his forces.

Relationships
He worked alongside the sorceress Winaneh Marlowson, but his attempts at seduction led to rejection. Although he hated her for this, they still had to perform their work together.
