## Oyn Evenmoor

Oyn Evenmoor was a merchant on the Sword Coast just before the Tyranny of Dragons.

Personality
Oyn was a stubborn, argumentative man with an opinion about everything. He would generously offer drinks to people to listen to his speeches.

History
At the start of the Tyranny of Dragons in 1489 DR Oyn attached his wagon carrying exotic birds to a great caravan that, unbeknownst to him, was full of agents of the Cult of the Dragon transporting treasures for the Hoard of the Dragon Queen.

## Oyaviggaton

Oyaviggaton (meaning "Island of Eternity" in Uluik) was a large iceberg that traveled the Sea of Moving Ice and was never in the same place twice. It was the home and lair of the white dragon Arauthator.

Geography
The iceberg was characterized by several high peaks, well over 200 ft (61 m) tall. The middle of Oyaviggaton formed a plateau, where hundreds of native Ice Hunters lived.

## Oyai

Oyai was a Tabaxi human woman of Katashaka who led her people to Chult and became Negus Negusti (Prince of Princes) and First Bara of Ubtao in the early 3 millennium before Dale Reckoning.

History
At the time, the lands of the Tabaxi were reigned over by a nyama-nummo known as the Sleeper, who supposedly favored them over the other tribes of Katashaka. As a baby, Oyai was named Tarasajok, meaning "She Who Watches over the Sleeper"—and who had the honor of being first to be eaten by the tarrasque upon awakening at the Palace of the Alliz-Dren on the next Night of Feasting.

Shortly after, the couatl Ecatzin came to the Tabaxi to preach of the god Ubtao. Although initially resistant, fearing the feathered serpent to be a minion of Akongo, some of the tribe, including Oyai, gradually came to accept the couatl's words and to learn a little of its language. A chosen few like Oyai even learned a forbidden magic called matumbe. But they still dreaded the Sleeper's imminent awakening.

Finally, on the next Night of Feasting at the end of a millennium, Ubtao blessed Oyai, naming her Negus Negusti and Bara, right before the Sleeper awoke with a roar and came to devour her. But Oyai used the matumbe magic to halt, crush, and slay the Sleeper. That night, many liberated and joyous Tabaxi celebrated Oyai and pledged themselves to Ubtao, as Ecatzin named them Ubtao's blessed children.

Soon after, in, Oyai led the peoples of the Tabaxi, Eshowe, Thinguth, and several others who'd accepted the faith of Ubtao to go with the couatls in a great pilgrimage over the ocean in some 300 canoa (canoes). Landing on the Wild Coast, they journeyed into the Jungles of Chult and met their new god at the Peaks of Flame. Oyai ruled over the first Tabaxi of Chult, and her story of Katashaka would be recorded in the Teachings of Ubtao, later stored in the vaults of the Maze of Life in the later city of Mezro.

## Oyaminartok

Oyaminartok  was a goliath werebear that was skilled at fishing.

Description
Oyaminartok's werebear form represented a humanoid polar bear, though her huge arms almost reached her feet. The werebear's hair was styled into thick braids and decorated with metal beads. She wore furs and a murky green garb that was fastened by a leather belt.

Personality
Though she had a fearsome look to some, Oyaminartok was rather kind, and willing to help adventurers.

Possessions
She carried a fishing rod, as well as a lot of fishing tackle and the fish she had caught (particularly knucklehead trout).

## Oyster

Oysters were a type of mollusk found throughout Toril.

Description
Oysters were small mollusks with a soft body that was protected by a hard shell.

Species

Blacklip oyster, a valuable species of oysters that produced black pearls.
Emerald oyster
Giant oyster

Uses
Oysters were one of the few sources of pearls. There were a number of colors that their pearls could come out as, but no one was certain as to what was the cause of certain colors occurring.

Oysters could be smoked or consumed raw. Oysters were, obviously, a key ingredient in Beef and Oysters Barnabas. In Wa, a type of cake, pink in color, was made with oyster shells mixed with cinnamon.

An oyster shell could be used as the material component for casting the prismatic eye spell.

Ecology
Oysters were found in warm bodies of water.

Locations
 Oysters were found in the High Moor. These freshwater oysters very rarely produced pearls, but that did not stop humans from Secomber from taking the risk in hunting for pearls in the High Moor.
 Oysters were cultivated in the Lake of Steam although few produced pearls. However, a small town on the Lake of Steam, Yhep, was famous for its scarlet pearls.
 Acoval's Cove was home to many pools in which pearl-laden oysters were cultivated.
 Moonever was a small village which supplied oysters to several settlements in Cormyr.
 The Bay of Dancing Dolphins was also known for its riches of oyster beds and their abundance of pearls.
 Pearling season in Zakhara lasted for five months and saw the waters between Gana and Jumlat teeming with pearl boats, their divers hunting for pearl laden oysters on the seabed.
 In Malatra, the fishing village of Buyan cultivated oysters and sold what were considered the best pearls in Malatra.
 In Kozakura, the fishermen of the island of Nakashima cultivated oysters for their pearls. The Senshi Islands were famous for its large and plentiful oyster beds.
 The waters off the coast of Iiso, a city in Wa, were home to oyster beds which were the source of what many considered the best pearls found on Toril.
 Huge reefs of oysters could be found all along the Sword Coast and as far north as the Sea of Moving Ice. These were vital for sustaining local fish populations, and so knowledgeable fishers avoided harvesting too many of them. The fish markets of Waterdeep sold oysters as food during the winter season.

Notable Users
Businesses
 Felzoun's Folly, a tavern in the city of Waterdeep, served its patrons fresh whole oysters.
Races & Sentient Creatures
 In Kara-Tur, oysters were part of the regular diet of both hai nu and takos and were a delicacy among ningyo.

## O-yoroi

The o-yoroi was a type of armor used in Kara-Tur.

Description
The o-yoroi was a comprehensive ensemble of laced armor, comprising various components such as kote, do-maru, sode, haidate, kabuto, and sune-ate. Additionally, it included several smaller pieces, including the mempo (face mask), nodawa (neck guard), wakibiki (armpit guards), sendan-no-ita, and hato-wo-no-ita (guards for the front of the armpit).

Crafted as a matched set, the o-yoroi exhibited harmonious colors and patterns that complemented each other. This not only enhanced the visual appeal but also improved the overall fit of the armor, resulting in a superior armor compared to a collection of mismatched pieces.

## Oyori of the Unknown Warrior

The Oyori of the Unknown Warrior was a unique set of enchanted Kozakuran armor, created sometime before the late 14 century DR.

Description
The Oyori of the Unknown Warrior was a set of heavy armor of Kozakuran design. Before being donned for the first time, this ō-yoroi was bright white. Once it was put on by its owner, its color turned black, and it displayed the wearer's family mon, or crest, in the appropriate colors.

Powers
The Oyori of the Unknown Warrior changed its color from white to black and was decorated differently for each new owner. When the wearer of the ō-yoroi was killed, the image of the family crest and the black color turned back to stark white—the color of mourning. The wearer could tap into the armor's magic to change its appearance in order to aid them with infiltration and disguise, imitating other colors and crests from the wearer's mind. Additionally to that property, the Oyori of the Unknown Warrior possessed a potent defensive +2 enchantment.
