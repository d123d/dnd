## 171 DR

Cymrych Hugh, future first High King of the Moonshae Isles, is born.

 King Deric of Corwell dies of natural causes without an heir.

 Following King Deric's death, the Ffolk of Corwell descend into civil war.
 Halaster Blackcloak begins Halaster's Hunts in Undermountain. They last until 308 DR.
 In northern Arcorar (present-day Yûlash), the last temple of Moander is destroyed and its priests killed following a two-year-long siege and skirmishing with the elves of Cormanthyr.

 The deity Moander is trapped underneath the ruins by the High Magic of the Coronal and sixteen of Cormanthyr's High Mages, and banished from Toril.

## 177 DR

The orcs of the Severed Hand and Argrock are destroyed by the elves of Iliyanbruen. Many elves die in the conflict and Iliyanbruen is vastly weakened.

 Iliyanbruen falls apart less than three years later. The surviving moon elves move to Evermeet and Ardeep, while only scattered wood elf settlements remain.

 Callidyr Hugh, a noble scion of Ebenfar, ends the civil war in Corwell, uniting the Ffolk under his rulership. He founds the Hugh Dynasty of the Moonshae Isles.

 In an event of dire misfortune, Magister Malanthus Stormstaern is struck by a "falling star" and killed. Some believed this errant missile was actually a malfunctioning spelljamming ship.

 Dorgon Heamilolothtar is appointed as the new Magister of Mystra.

## 173 DR

In Halruaa, the worshipers of Mystra and Azuth dominate the Council of Elders. Followers of Leira grow dissatisfied with the situation and leaving the country in skyships.

 Leiran wizard lords of Halruaa and their followers settle on the island of Nimbral, where they are welcomed by the existing elves. The settlers become known as Nimbrans, and develop a distinct culture.

 Qysar Amahl Shoon V's daughter falls from her horse but when an Ilmatari hermit saves her life, the Qysar declares all of Ilmater's faithful immune to persecution and harm from then on, lest the aggressor be cursed to suffer a fate one hundred times worse. This ensures the safety of Ilmater's servants in Calimshan for as long as the proclamation is remembered and sparks a centuries-long debate in the Ilmatari church on whether Amahl V should be canonized.

## -17600 DR

__NOTOC__

 Circa: Elven high mages use High Magic to create Evermeet but cause the First Sundering. The magic stretches both backward and forward in time. The continent of Faerûn is split apart, hundreds of cities are destroyed in floods, and thousands of elves are killed.
 Ongoing seismic activity leads to a thousand years of earthquakes, maelstoms and other deadly oceanic phenomena, making travel and colonization of the waters exceptionally difficult.

 Circa: Angharradh blessed the new island of Evermeet with natural life, and Corellon wards it against Lolth, Malar and other deities the Seldarine oppose.

## -178 DR

The second Emperor Chin of the Kao Dynasty of Shou Lung is crowned and decrees the Spreading of Knowledge, also called the Opening of Wa. This demands that the people of Wa be pacified and instilled with Shou customs. Wa's war-torn and fractious daimyos acquiesce.
 The Shou invasion of Wa is secretly aided by the Order of the Snake, but some members refuse and split off to form the Order of the Mouse. However, they are few and unable to garner support. The Order of the Mouse retreat into the wilderness, and focuses on covertly working against the Order of the Snake.

## -170 DR

__NOTOC__

 The Eye Tyrant Wars break out, as the beholders of the Alimir Hive war against Calimshan. Tethyr and Iltkazar ally with Calimshan against the beholders. The beholders take Almraiven, then Suldolphor, then finally control the Spider Swamp and the southern Forest of Mir. This largely blocks forces from western Calimshan from moving anywhere but directly into beholder-controlled bases.

 Zahyra Bardson-Ithal is appointed the first vizera of Tethyr.

 The Knights of the Shield are formed to gather intelligence for Tethyr during the Eye Tyrant Wars.
