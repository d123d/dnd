## -1 DR

Akkabar the Younger dies in a tavern brawl in Memnon. He is the eldest grandson of Syl-Pasha Akkabar el Shoon of Calimshan and his proclaimed heir, and is also the father of King Amahl II of Tethyr. When the killing is found to be a revenge attack by Tethyrian clansmen, Tethyr suffers badly.

 Construction begins on a new capital for the Shoon Imperium, called Shoonach. The ruins of Ithmong are robbed for building material, and Tethyrian supporters of Clan Ithal are enslaved to transport the stone to Shoonach over a decade.

## 1 DR

The Standing Stone is erected by the elves of Cormanthyr and the Dalefolk. This confirms the Dales Compact and marks the start of the Dalereckoning calendar.
 Cormyr is founded.

 The dwarves of Ammarindar finish constructing Citadel Yaunoroth.
