## 1148 DR

The "Lord of Bones", the necromancer Shradin Mulophor, asks Halaster Blackcloak for permission to occupy the former site of the Sargauth Enclave, forming a new trading post that is later to develop into Skullport. Shradin links the River Sargauth with other subterranean waterways and creates portals to faraway seas, developing trade with other powers of the Underdark.

 The Ice Castle of the Vaasan wizards in the Lugsaas Chain is destroyed by an avalanche after an earthquake strikes the region.

 Sailors that were thought lost at sea begin turning up on shore. They are all now working for the Kraken Society as their first land-bound agents.

 Wai Long Hwa is recognized as the third emperor of the Lui Dynasty of T'u Lung and the Maki Clan becomes more powerful by supporting him. The Three Kingdoms are reunited and the Ho and Tan families are defeated, but remain strong. The Yang family is given control of its lands.

## 1150 DR

Alemander II, future king of Tethyr is born.

 Orc and troll tribes make increased numbers of attacks on the Sword Coast.
 Ibun Rensha of Calimshan and several family members raise an army of mercenaries. They seize Loudwater and claim much of the Delimbiyr Vale.
 Borran Klosk, a mohrg in service to Malar, leads an army of undead in an attack on  the cities of the Vilhon Reach. The Vilhon cities are aided by the dwarves of the Alaoreum and the druids of the Emerald Enclave. The armies clash at the Battle of Morningstar Hollows, where the druids raise the Alaoreum River and flood the undead force. Borran Klosk cannot be destroyed, but clerics of Eldath imprison him beneath the city of Alaghôn.

 Circa: Chondath experiences a resurgence in power. Its mercenaries forces begin to be lent to many foreign wars, particularly the internal fighting in Chessenta.

 Several satraps and shaleiras are found dead in their beds in Calimport. The only clue to the cause of their deaths is a single burn mark. The murderer is eventually caught in the act of killing the Ongalar but whoever the culprit is escapes underground without their identity being revealed despite numerous powerful divinations.

 Khelben "Blackstaff" Arunsun the Elder arrives for the first time in Waterdeep and constructs Arunsun (later Blackstaff) Tower.

 A terrible plague spreads through the Sword Coast area.

 Plague and conflict on the Sword Coast leads to a rise in worship of Talona and Loviatar.

## 1142 DR

The Amcathra family is ennobled in Waterdeep.
 A number of Inner Sea pirates choose to retire to Aglarond, among them the infamous Redsail. They bring their immense booty with them, and in addition to spending big on carousing and introducing crime, they also invest extensively but secretly in legitimate local merchant shipping, businesses, and property. This reduces the southern coastal lands' mercantile dominance on Inner Sea trade.

 The pureblood yuan-ti mages of the Coiled Cabal awake after centuries and attempt to conquer the Cities of the Seabreeze, beginning with Sammaresh. Two dozen archmages of Tashluta and Lapaliiya battle them in a season of savage spell-battles along the Tashtan Coast, in conflict known as the Rage of Wizards. The cities of Lapaliiya and Tashalar suffer severe damage.

 Shell House, a temple to Umberlee in Ilyaport, is destroyed amidst the Rage of Wizards. The Orglara, a relic that the Shell Priests of that temple kept safe, is sucked out to sea by a great wave.
 Anger over the Rage of Wizards in Lapaliiya sees a corresponding rise in respect for priests, leading to the establishment of the civic faiths that dominate each city.
 The Skeletal Finger thieves guild steals the monocle of Bagthalos from the House of the Steadfast, a Helmite temple in Zazesspur.
 2–3 Marpenoth: A group of adventurers chase down a drow slaving expedition near Rothé Valley, and discover the area has long been used as a trap by House Xorlarrin to lure in settlers for eventual enslavement. The adventuring party is lost in the tunnels beneath the valley before they can report their findings to the world.

## 1173 DR

Coram IV of House Tethyr is born.

 Contemplating how to deal with ambitious family members, Emperor Wai Long Sun of T'u Lung eats rice from the enchanted bowls fashioned by potter Jo He Ting, which grant clear thought. However, they do not protect against poison, and he dies. His cousin, Wai Chu Doang becomes the fifth emperor. Exploiting the oath of loyalty, he raises large and strong armies as a defense against the baronial clans and against Shou Lung. His habit of using harsh, military solutions to resolve domestic issues leads to his reign being named "The Law of Fists".
