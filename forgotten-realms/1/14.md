## 146 DR

Aravae Irithyl, the first child of House Irithyl in centuries, is born. Unfortunately, her mother dies in childbirth. Aravae is the only heir of her great uncle, as head of House Irithyl and as coronal of Cormanthyr. From birth, Aravae is defended by three dedicated bodyguards.

 An exiled lord of Tavaray called Uth Myrmoran moves to a small port settlement on the Sword Coast North and builds the Uthtower on a rocky outcrop west of the Sword Mountains, proclaiming himself king of the kingdom of Uthtower. This is the start of the Uth Calendar.
 Settlers from Tavaray found Elembar, east of Ardeep and north of the River Delimbiyr.

 Aided by the invoker Riliton Mandleweave and the Company of the Howling Harpoon, the forces of Hlondeth once again defeats the army of Alaghôn led by Dempster Turmish.
