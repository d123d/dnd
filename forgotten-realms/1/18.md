## -1838 DR

Calishite adventurers loot the treasure hoard of Ylveraasahlisar the Rose Dragon and slay one of her young. In revenge, she obliterates the army of Calimshan, the Caleph's Court, the Raor bakkal, and most of Calimport.

 Dethed the Death Lich is destroyed by Ioulaum after he possesses the body of another arcanist's apprentice.

 Ylveraasahlisar seizes the throne of Calimshan's bakkal and rules the empire.

 Ylveraasahlisar demands the immediate rebuilding of Calimport after her rampage. The workers smooth over the rubble and build on top, creating the subterranean section of Calimport Muzad.
