## Rringlor Noroth

Ringlor Noroth, also known as the City of Soaring Shadows, was the largest settlement of cloakers in the Upperdark.

Geography
It was located in the Underdark beneath the Marching Mountains in Calimshan. The city was founded around an ancient and ruined bridge known as the Shadowspan, which connected the Lake of Radiant Mists to a mysterious lake located in the Shadowfell. The bridge was said to predate the dwarves of Old Shanatar, and had long since collapsed.

Government
The city was ruled by a council of twelve cloaker lords known as the Shadoworb Conclave who took directions from a mysterious entity from the Shadowfell known as the "Emissary".

The cloakers of Rringlor Noroth ascribed to a rigid caste system.

History
Ringlor Noroth was founded when its twelve cloaker lords brought its population of cloakers together over ten thousand years prior the 14 century DR. If dwarven legends of its ancient history were to be believed, it had not changed at all in that time. However, sometime in the 12 century DR, the Shadoworb Conclave began a project to rebuild the Shadowspan in order to reconnect the lake on the Material Plane to its sister lake in the Shadowfell.

## Rrakkma

Rrakkma is an adventure module set in the Forgotten Realms using the Dungeons & Dragons 5 edition ruleset. It is an introductory adventure for Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes. It focuses on members of the Sha'sal Khou working to stop a group of mind flayers from reenslaving the gith race, and is an adventure designed for a group of six pre-generated 9-level characters.

## Rrinnoroth

Rrinnoroth was a settlement located within the dwarven realm of Shanatar. It was a ghost town by .

Geography
The ruins of Rrinnoroth were located beneath the Kuldin Peaks which were located in the surface realm of Tethyr. One of the only access points to the ruins of Drakkalor was down a tunnel which began in Rrinnoroth.

Inhabitants
The former dwarven settlement was now devoid of all life. It's sole occupant was the mighty red dragon Charvekkanathor. He used the ruins of Rrinnoroth as his lair and where he stored his treasure hoard. As a result, the ruins where heavily guarded with explosive traps and teleportation wards.

Trivia
Rrinnoroth, in the Dwarven language, meant "place over enemy land".

## Rrosp

Rrosp was a hefty earth elemental who was controlled by the medusa Sshaastess in the late 15 century DR.

History
When Oriphaun Huntsilver left a piece of the Abyssal Planus Mechanus Nether Scroll with Seamus at Lost Souls' Swamp, the medusa Sshaastess was drawn by its energies and proceeded to dominate the area with foul magic. While defending herself from Harper agents sent to recover the Scroll she summoned up Rrosp and Gruck as her bodyguards, only for them to all be slain in battle.
