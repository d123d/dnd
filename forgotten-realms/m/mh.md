## Mhair Gulzrabban

Mhair Gulzrabban was the founder and leader of the adventuring band known as the Company of the Cloak.

Description
Mhair was a stout small man with tiny dark eyes.

Personality
The wizard was always in control, seeking power above all else and wanting to do as he pleased. He was always on the lookout for new ways to exploit a weakness or two in order to gain a profit.

Abilities
Mhair, along with all of the other members of the Company of the Cloak, possessed an immunity to oil of taggit (which causes unconsciousness).

Relationships
Sharaera Duthtue was Mhair's lover and apprentice. He was prepared for her eventual turn on him. Over the years, Gulzrabban made contacts with the Zhentarim, the Red Wizards of Thay, and the Shadow Thieves and set his organization up as "adventurer killers".

History
Gulzrabban began the Company of the Cloak in 1357 DR in the city of Scornubel within the Western Heartlands. Recruiting cutthroats and brigands, Mhair assembled a band to hunt down buried treasure and forgotten lore.

As time passed, he faced mutiny and dissatisfaction within his company until deciding to stalk fellow adventurers.

## Mhiilamniir

Mhiilamniir was a city of the elven realm of Eaerlann.

Location
The city stood in the heart of the High Forest, less than two days' journey away from the west end of the Old Road and three days' journey east of the Lost Peaks.

History
During the height of the kingdom of Eaerlann, Mhiilamniir was formerly the seat of power for several of the priesthoods of the Seldarine in the North.

The settlement was destroyed in the demonic uprising from Hellgate Keep, and most of it was overgrown with moss and ivy. The female green dragon Chloracridara claimed the whole city and its environs as her domain. She had lived in the partially standing ruins of the temple to Corellon Larethian for six hundred years by 1370 DR. Her rabid paranoia and offspring's hunting made the surrounding area quite dangerous, too hazardous for elven pilgrims to visit any of the temples.

Locations
The city was home to a number of major temples. While the largest was the temple of Corellon, the very oldest was the Temple Beyond Time, dedicated to Labelas Enoreth. According to Labelasan religious texts, it had stood during the early history of Aryvandaar, circa .

Inhabitants
A male half-elf half-dragon woodsman was known to claim that he could grant an audience with Chloracridara for a fee.

## Mhaorathil

Mhaorathil was a magical short sword for dwarves and one of the baneblades of Demron created in Myth Drannor.

Description

## Mhandyvver's Poultry

Mhandyvver's Poultry was one of two poultry stores in the town of Red Larch.

Geography
Mhandyvver's Poultry was located along the Long Road, next to Luruth's Tannery and across from Vallivoe's Sundries.

Interior
The interior of this store resembled a barn, with numerous exposed beams and posts. Pens for livestock filled most of the building's odd corners and halls, leaving only a narrow aisle down to the back where the staff and owner lived. These living quarters were separated from the livestock pens by a workroom that had cutting benches and a central hearth.

Services
Mhandyvver's Poultry produced fowl, fresh eggs, and chicks for raising. These fowl included turkeys, chickens, and ducks whose wings had been clipped. They sold this poultry either roasted or preserved in oil, while eggs were either sold fresh or pickled. They were also known to sell pickled chicken livers.

Inhabitants
In the late-14 century DR the store was owned and operated by Oskler Mhandyvver. He frequently hired local boys to protect his livestock from foxes, hawks, owls, weasels, and other predatory pests.

In the late-15 century DR, the store was operated by a descendant of his named Minthra Mhandyvver and her three children.

## Mhair

Mhair was a human fighter who worked as a boatman at the Temple of Mystra in Wheloon, Cormyr, in the .

History
By Eleint of 1374 DR, Mhair was working at the temple, having a key role in transporting kidnapped petitioners and followers of Mystra out of the city.

Eventually, on Eleint 6, an adventuring party recruited by Tunaster Dranik to investigate the temple discovered the truth, that it was a front for the church of Shar. Mhair's canon fate is unknown, but the module states that, if the fight goes against him, he will try to dive into the river under the temple and swim away.

Activities
Acting under the orders of Lady Naedaenya Arthas, Mhair's role at the temple was to smuggle the abducted, mentally dominated petitioners out of the temple and out of the city. Once a month, during the night, he loaded the captives onto a special catamaran and rowed them out through a river-cave beneath the temple, through a concealed entrance, and out into the Wyvernflow river. When the coast was clear, he pilot the catamaran across the river and deposited them on the opposite riverbank. There, he gave them a map and told them to journey to the Lost Refuge in the Vast Swamp, and there meet with Starweaver Bestra Mornscroll, whom they were to obey. He also told them to act normally otherwise. He also sometimes took the opportunity to rob the mind-controlled victims, against orders.

He drew his maps based on a map of southeastern Cormyr, one happened to depict the little-known locations of the Lost Refuge and the Orvaskyte Keep in the Vast Swamp. Mhair was able to add some new locations, though he only guessed at the location of the Monastery of the Ebon Dome, with the Sharrans neglecting to correct him.

He spent much of his spare time working on his kayak.

Description
Mhair had blue eyes and blond hair.

Personality

Mhair has a sharp tongue, quick to insult folk he talked to.

Although he knew he served false priests, he didn't know who they really worshiped, nor did he seem to care.

Abilities
Mhair was a tough and very capable fighter, and a master of the longspear. He was also an expert swimmer.

Possessions
In battle, Mhair wielded a magical +1 longspear and an ordinary club, and wore +1 leather armor. He owned three potions of cure moderate wounds. He also stole from the petitioners who passed through his hands, acquiring a +1 dagger, three arrows of orc slaying, and a lens of detection, as well as treasures like a gold bracelet, a gold and silver wire circlet, fine clothes, and a bag of coins. These he kept hidden in a secret, locked compartment in his desk.

He operated the special catamaran the temple employed. This double-hulled vessel, worth 500 gp, was steered by long punting pole at the rear. It held up to nine people, including the pilot. He also owned a kayak for personal use, a sleek wooden one-person craft with double-sided paddle, worth 75 gp and perfect for use on the Wyvernflow.

## Mhoaran

King Mhoaran the Tusk-Bearded of the House of Ithal-Strohm-Bormul was the first king of the Lions' Dynasty of Tethyr.

Description
Mhoaran was a thick and tall man and a powerful warrior.

Personality
King Mhoaran was known for his powerful temper, but he was also a shrewd ruler.

Relationships
Mhoaran was a great-grandson of Queen Sybille the Great and the son of the only brother of Cyriana the Great, Prince Atann.Lands of Intrigue Book 3: Erlkazar and Folk of Intrigue conflicts with Book 1: Tethyr in claiming that Mhoaran was the son of Cyriana's third sister instead of her only brother. On the other hand, Book 1 also claims that Mhoaran was a grandson of Sybille, not a great-grandson.

Because of so many assassination attempts on his family, Mhoaran lost three wives and was the father of more than 40 children, as he attempted to produce an heir.

History
Mhoaran was born in 919 DR, the Year of the Chase.

He and his father, Prince Atann, believed him to have a more legitimate claim to the throne, as Atann should have been the male heir of Sybille rather than the line having passed through Queen Cyriana to Queen Teresa the Great as it did. For this reason, Mhoaran built up an army and staged a military coup to take the throne from King Teremir, Teresa's son. When Teremir refused to step down from the throne, Mhoaran's army attacked, and Mhoaran himself slew King Teremir. The year was 959 DR.

King Mhoaran's reign was an unstable time, where the nobles of the land sought to assassinate him and his family to restore the Bormul Dynasty. Mhoaran tried to protect his children by hiding them at birth and having them raised in secret. Even so, the first sixteen sons of Mhoaran died before they could reach adulthood.

Upon his death, his seventeenth son (and 32 child), Nearel, succeeded him as king in 974 DR.

## Mhaere Dryndilstann

Mhaere Dryndilstann was a human cleric of Lathander and an innkeeper of the Yawning Portal in Waterdeep in the mid-to-late 14 century DR. She was the wife of the owner, the famed adventurer Durnan the Wanderer.

Personality
Mhaere was a devoted and strong-willed but quiet woman. She was kindly, alert, and lively. Mhaere's levelheaded competence was one of her greatest strengths.

Description
She was good looking with strong features, albeit not a striking beauty.

Abilities
As a cleric, her hands were able to both heal and kill in battle. Among her spells, she had command, cure light wounds, flame blade, and hold person.

Activities
Mhaere managed the Yawning Portal inn with her husband Durnan. She was hard-working and brisk about her duties. While she picked up much gossip whilst working at the inn, she was not one to share and spread it.

Often, she also healed injured adventurers coming out of Undermountain. Despite Durnan's rules on paying a gold piece in order to be lifted out of the well, Mhaere would on occasion drop one down to those in dire need so they could pay, at least when Durnan's back was turned.

If trouble broke out at the inn, Durnan had a horn he would blow to summon the Red Sashes, his personal vigilante organization. If he was unavailable or incapacitated, then Mhaere and others would find it and use it.

As a sideline business, she sewed basic dresses, working smocks, and towels.

Relationships
She was married to Durnan. Together they had a daughter, Tamsil Dryndilstann; since Durnan had no surname, she took Mhaere's.The Ruins of Undermountain says Mhaere is 30 years old and Tamsil is 16 years old. While all sources agree Tamsil is the daughter of both Durnan and Mhaere, this implies Mhaere had her at only around 14. However, there are alternatives, such as Tamsil being Durnan's daughter from a previous lover or being adopted, magical options, or that these figures are in error. Moreover, Durnan's age was later changed from 45 to at least 75 with magically enhanced longevity, suggesting these ages may be revised too or that Mhaere's stated age reflects an apparent, not actual, age. Maybe she imbibed the same potions of longevity as her husband? Tamsil took closely after Mhaere. They also had two younger daughters. Mhaere was aware of her husband's status as a Masked Lord of Waterdeep, but did not discuss it with him.

She had an uncle in Neverwinter, who was also a priest of Lathander.

She achieved the rank of Helping Hand in the church of Lathander.

History
Mhaere was born in Neverwinter and raised there by her uncle.The Ruins of Undermountain says Mhaere is 30 years old, but this book is undated, making her birth date ambiguous. Other sources place Ruins in 1358 DR, implying Mhaere was born around 1328 DR, but see the previous note. Dragon's Deep reprints this age, but it is more likely to be set around 1372 DR.

She became an adventurer, but later retired.

The murderer Amril Zoar used a gate to enter the Yawning Portal one morning fore-dawn in early Flamerule of the, doubtless seeking to slay Durnan. He rushed at young Tamsil but, quick-reacting, she threw the ewer of water she'd been carrying at him, before Mhaere snatched up the double-crossbow kept ready behind the bar and gave him both bolts. Amril retreated through his gate, dropping a silver Harper pin in the process. Durnan arrived moments later, vowing to find and end the man who'd threatened his family.

In her younger days, she wielded a +2 flail and wore plate armor, which she kept stored in the wardrobe in her bedchamber. In the event of fighting in the inn, she was more likely to wield a handy club or mace. She typically wore a belt knife for work and eschewed jewelry.

## Mhzentul's Runes

Mhzentul's Runes was the spellbook and workbook of the famous mage Mhzentul, who lived in Faerûn during the 13 and 14 centuries DR.

Description
The thin but heavy tome contained twelve pages of vellum bound with silk, sealed with a red wax that was imbued with the blood of a dragon. The covers were made of oiled wood that was wrapped in the stretched skin of a wyvern. It featured a number of scorch marks roughly the size of a finger.

Contents
The first four pages of Mhzentul's Runes contained precise and detailed descriptions on how to create and enchant a ring of spell storing. Four of the seven lost rings of Mhzentul were known to have been of this particular variety.

Continuing on, the following five pages contained Mhzentul's notes regarding the creation of magical rings that could transform into a creature that could subsequently be controlled by the wielder. This section was incomplete and, according to his writings, Mhzentul could "see no manner nor means for imbuing the creature with any animation...".

The final section of the tome contained notes on Mhzentul's research into fire magic, containing a number of runes and glyphs written in varied, particular inks. It contained specifications on the following spells: fireball, fire shield, fire trap, and delayed blast fireball.

History
After Mhzentul's death in 1340 DR, a number of treasure hunters sought Mhzentul's hidden mountain abode but could not recover his namesake tome. It didn't appear in Faerûn until the year 1346 DR when it was seen in the possession of the half-orc adventurer Uthmang. He was slain by the Red Wizard Alail Thang, who took the book and held it until he was defeated by the priests of Greenstone Keep.

Mhzentul's Runes was lost for two years before it was recovered by the sage Lhegrand, who proceeded to describe its contents for posterity. He only kept it for a brief time before he was abducted by the orcs of Darkhold. The most recent rumors of the tome's ownership pointed to the Cormyrian wizard of the Zhentarim named Whisper and was believed to have been interred with him within his crypt.

## Mhairhetel

Mhairhetel, also known as the City of Renegades, was a small city in Serpentes situated at the southeastern tip of the Mhair Jungles. It was considered to be one of Faerûn's most notorious pirate ports.

Government
Mhairhetel was ruled by the Rhaunister family, which was made up of junior yuan-ti pureblood members of House Jennestas. By 1373 DR, the city's High Lord was Istasfis Nsaran, who attained the position by marrying the previous High Lord's widow.

Defenses
Mhairhetel was defended from the Mhair Jungles' monsters and overland attacks by patrols of human-sized snakes stationed on the eastern side of the Guth Mountains and the east end of the Dolsel Gap.

History
In the, Mhairhetel was founded by the Halruaan archmage Hetel Hastalhorn, who sought to take advantage of the Mhair Jungles' hidden treasures. This initial chapter of the city was short-lived, however, and by the following year, its founder had disappeared and nearly the entire population abandoned the settlement.

Despite this initial misfortune, Mhairhetel's population quickly started growing again, supplemented by consistent waves of refugees seeking to escape their homelands, and by 1373 DR it had grown into a small but viable city with a population in the thousands.

Since several centuries before the 14, especially before the alliance between the Rundeen trade consortium and House Se'Sehen was forged after the Time of Troubles, Mhairhetel was thought of as a useful asset to the yuan-ti tribes of Serpentes as a unique conduit for trade with outlanders. The port's role as a common starting point for humans from the coasts of the Great Sea who wished to explore the Mhair Jungles was also seen as a convenient factor for surveillance.

Rumors & Legends
Although the Rhaunister family's yuan-ti identity was kept secret, rumors about their ability to talk to snakes and the conduction of experiments to turn people into human-serpent hybrids ran rampant.

## Mhai Prittee

Mhai Prittee, or Mhai the Mage, was an adventuring mage who gained the favor of a Hillsfaran noblewoman in the mid-14 century DR, that jumpstarted his heroic career.

Description
Like many other mages, Mhai wore wizard's robes.

Personality
Mhai hated rats and gladly used them for his spell target practice.

Possessions
At some pint before the events of 1358 DR, Mhai acquired several knock rings during an adventure.

Relationships
Mhai's trusted familiar was the frog named Dodo. The eloquent amphibian was one year old, or seventy in frog years. Dodo verbosely communicated with Mhai via telepathy.

History
By the, young Mhai the Mage spent his entire life within the city walls of Hillsfar. Without ever venturing outside, Mhai often dreamt of what he thought the farm life outside was like: dwarven maidens with parted beards, churning milk into butter with their hairy feet amidst a pastoral countryside setting. One night, such great changed into a strange vision of two feminine arms luring him towards a woman's face for a kiss and then towards a magic shop Mhai recognized as the establishment always holding a "Going Out of Business Sale."

Mhai discussed the vision with his aged wide frog familiar. Obviously, the vision commanded the mage to break into the magic shop under a cover for the night. They theorized that there was something important in the show, or maybe the vision was a command from the Mages' Guild of Hillsfar, of which Mhai was a member of. The young mage thought it to be his big break, an opportunity to abandon his life of sleeping in Hillsfar's stables. Despite his history of being captured by the Red Plumes while trespassing in the past and being sent to the Hillsfar Arena and receiving a whooping from Lefty the orc, Mhai decided to risk it.

Mhai entered the shop via one of his knock rings and quickly began to search for whatever the "Arm and Lip Lady" from his vision wanted. Eventually, Mhai discovered an item he'd never seen before – a faintly-glowing pick, carved with ornate designs and inscribed with its name – Stonemaster. Still unclear on the vision's purpose, the mage and his familiar decided that the pick needed to be returned to its master, as it was obviously out of place in a wizard's miscellany store. Mhai thought that it belonged to the master of a rock quarry west of Hillsfar.

The young mage was terrified of the wilderness outside of town – all the lions, tigers, and bears. On his way across the town back to the stables, Mhai was confronted by Wak Rathar, who appeared out of nowhere on the Market Street. The old wizard offered to show the youth a magic trick, but Mhai declined as he once before fell for the man's trick and ended up fighting Lefty in the Arena. As the sun began to rise, Mhai tracked down the stable master – Darnin, and convinced him to land the youth a horse for the journey to the quarry west of Hillsfar, past a roadside trading post. Mhai decided to stop at the trading post along the way to gather information. There, he purchased a map of the quarry that managed to only show its approximate location, past the hermit's place.

Mhai did stop at the hermit's hut, but to his annoyance, the old man took a vow of silence and offered no help apart from his maniacal humming. The mage and his familiar proceeded to move through the woods for the next hour and finally reached the quarry, swarming with dwarven and human miners. The quarry master was an extremely irate dwarf who, indeed, was the owner of the Stonemaster. A woman named Diane promised the dwarf that someone would be bringing the pick soon and left an elixir to give to the young mage as a reward. Mhai decided to show the mysterious bottle to the guildmaster of the Hillsfar Mages' Guild.

By dusk of the same day, Mhai was back in Hillsfar and at the stables. The next day, Mhai woke up close to midday to Dodo's mockery and amusement. The mage fantasized about serving his familiar's legs as an entree at Ruby Slippers Pub, but begrudgingly, they headed out for the Mages' Guild. Mhai was invited to meet with Glinda, the guild's Master of Mages. He announced that the potion given to Mhai was the elixir of Diane, believed to had been lost. Without hesitation, Glinda uncorked the vial that started spewing red vapors. The person who emerged from the bottle was the "Arm and Lip Lady," whom Mhai recognized from his vision. She introduced herself as the daughter of Diane. She was trapped, asked for help, and before fading away, she left cryptic directions for the young mage: "The first honor is of exacting aim on the fourth board," and whispered another word, "Tanna."

Tanna's Archery Range was the next stop on Mhai's strange journey. The range offered those not proficient with ranged weapons, like Mhai, magic wands to shoot the targets with. After a long session of hitting targets and scoring well across four scoreboards, Mhai's task was completed. Tanna handed the mage his winnings and, in secrecy, told him that Diane was sending him on the next part of the quest – to the Wizard's Labyrinth outside of Hillsfar, where more elixirs of Diane were hidden. The journey continued on the back of another of Damin's horses.

Using another one of his knock rings, Mhai gained entrance to the Wizard's Labyrinth. After several hours of searching about the hallways and headends, the mage found a vial of the reddish potion, another vial of the elixir of Diane. Another potion was uncorked, and red mist showed the daughter of Dianne once again, and once again, she left Mhai with a puzzle: "The second honor is one of freeing the Mages' Tower from a malevolent spirit. You need to open a special chest within the room it inhabits."

Mhai was set to finish the quest, even if it meant angering a malevolent spirit; the reward was a position in the guild. The Mages' Tower was an abandoned structure in Hillsfar. Inside, Mhai was assaulted with the feeling of palpable evil. Soon after entering, the mage began the search for the chest, pursued by the spirit. At the very moment, Mhai opened the chest, he was grasped by the spirit was ready for his cold grave, but in a blast of brilliant light, a goodly spirit rose from the chest and repelled the young mage's doom. It uttered, "You are honored this day with the second honor of Diane," and disappeared, leaving an elixir of Diane behind. The last and final message for the young mage was, "The last honor, my daring mage, is one of defeating the proud minotaur, Taurus, in the Arena. He is the instigator of my imprisonment."

The next day Mhai rose full of determination to finish his strange quest and defeat the bovine gladiator of the Hillsfar Arena. Somehow, Mhai felt deep and unreasonable hate for Taurus as he rushed to the Arena. With a simple challenge, the mage attracted the minotaur's attention. And within moments, their battle began with the bullman's charge. Mhai showed unparalleled skill with a quarterstaff, and with little aid from Dodo, Taurus was defeated. As the crowds cheered, a woman dressed in some of the best finery approached the mage. She was Diane, and all of the trials were her doing. She guided the hero to defeat Taurus, who sometime earlier trapped Diane's daughter within a mirror of life trapping with its location inscribed in magical script on a piece of parchment. Mhai read the script with ease – the mirror was in the infamous haunted mansion in Hillsfar's southeastern end.

Finding the mirror was an easy task; Mahi found it in the first chest he came across. With the mirror of life trapping smashed, Diane's daughter was freed at last. His reward was a hug from a beautiful young woman whose name he never learned. Lady Diane did leave a kind word with the Mages' Guild. Glinda, the Master of Mages, thanked the wizard youth and awarded him a sack of 21,500 gold pieces from Lady Diane. The reward from Glinda was magic from the ring of wishes that made Mhai sturdier, stronger, and able to withstand more damage.

The strange adventure ended with Mhai using the earnings to purchase one of Damin's horses, Magesteed, and galloping away to Tilverton.

## Mhaug's

Mhaug's was a shop in Lower Port, Skullport, in the late 14 century DR.

Description
Mhaug's provided undead labor for a fee. It was a small shop run by Mhaug, an annis hag.

Goods & Services
She acquired fresh bodies from various sources, including Leech's Quick Cures and Cryptkey Facilitations. She reanimated these corpses and offered them for sale as laborers and bodyguards. Her shop was notorious for its macabre display, where her wares—reanimated zombies—were hung by the neck from hooks outside the establishment. Due to the effects of decay and their prolonged exposure, these zombies developed distinctive physical traits, such as twisted necks and an awkward, shuffling gait. As a result, the citizens of Skullport dubbed them "Mhaug hogs."

The prices for Mhaug's zombies generally started at around 50 gp, with the cost varying based on the size of the corpse and the difficulty she encountered in the animation process. She was known to charge wealthier customers higher prices and had established ongoing business relationships with several notable local figures, including Dalagor the Cold. Mhaug was often seen outside her shop, either engaging in conversation with passersby or tending to a brazier where she slow-roasted questionable cuts of meat.

As of 1370 DR, Mhaug had recently expanded her enterprise by venturing into the brewing of "solutions"—a euphemism for poisons intended to resolve personal conflicts by eliminating the source of the problem. Mhaug's poisons, while sought after by those looking for discreet solutions, had certain limitations. Her poisons were expensive, and she offered no guarantees or refunds. They spoiled within a tenday if not stored in airtight containers, rendering them ineffective. Additionally, approximately 25% of the time, her poisons were inert and failed to work as intended. Despite these shortcomings, her reputation persisted, as many still sought her out for her unique services, fully aware of the risks associated with her wares.

Mharrander Dorolkh had displayed a short-range ability to probe the minds of other creatures. A recluse even in life, Mharrander kept busy by scrying on people, amusing himself with mimicry, and stealing lore from dying archwizards by being first to get there.

Born in  in Tzindylspar as one of forty children, Mharrander was sold to the archwizard Kazindrol, to whom he became apprenticed, learning magic in Thyndlamdrivvar.
