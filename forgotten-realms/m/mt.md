## M'tarl

M'tarl was a drow lieutenant within Jarlaxle's Bregan D'aerthe.

History
In 1358 DR, while traveling through the deadly streets of Menzoberranzan seeking valuable information to help the organization's cause, Jarlaxle and M'tarl encountered Gromph Baenre.

To have a private conversation with Jarlaxle, Gromph transported M'tarl to an extra-dimensional realm. After their meeting ended, Gromph told Jarlaxle his lieutenant perished.In the novel Siege of Darkness, Gromph takes Jarlaxle into an extra-dimensional pocket and destroys a zombie servant with fire. While there is no concrete evidence this zombie is M'tarl, the question is still presented for the reader.

## Mtair Dhafir

The Mtair Dhafir was a nomadic tribe of the Bedine people that traveled between the oases of the Sword in the Anauroch desert.

Organization
Like other tribes of the Bedine, the Mtair Dhafir were led by a single sheikh. As of the early 1360s DR, the Mtair Dhafir were led by Sheikh Sabkhat.

Base of Operations
The Mtair were known to stay at the oasis of El Ma'ra, among others.

History
In the, the Mtair Dhafir formed an alliance with the Qahtan tribe, through the marriage of the sheikhs' children. The outcast sorceress Ruha, daughter of Mtair sheikh Sabkhat, wed the brave Qahtani warrior Ajaman.

Mere days later, Sheikh Sabkhat entered into negotiations with the impressive warrior Zarud, an agent of the Zhentarim that sought to subjugate the Bedine and open a trade route through their lands. Unbeknownst to the sheikh, he was being manipulated by the spellcaster Yhekal and forced to act against his will. Thanks to the efforts of Ruha talking sense into her father, the insight of the brave warrior Al'Aif, and the intervention by the outlander agent of the Harpers known as Lander, the Mtair were able to eliminate Zarud, and avoid entering into an alliance with the Zhentarim.

Unfortunately the Zhentarim army and their asabi mercenaries quickly marched upon the Mtair camp at El Ma'ra. The tribe was all but eradicated by the invading forces, and their bodies were fed to the reptilian asabis.

Notable Members
 Al'Aif, the tribe's most fearsome warrior.
 Nata, its most mighty tribesman.
 Ruha, the exiled spellcaster who guided her people away from subjugation.

## M'tsin Gompa

M'tsin Gompa was a monastery (a gompa in the Tabotan language) of the Way of Enlightenment in Tabot.

Geography
It stood in northwestern Tabot, near the border and a short way northeast of Nad Ho Ting Lake.

Description
M'tsin Gompa was a small and peaceful place spread across a terraced mountainside. It was renowned for its natural wells and pools stocked with koi, which were considered sacred. Among the pools were lots of shrines and devotion poles.

Members
The population was small, with only 340 lamas. They were known for their quiet and harmonious nature, but also their mighty magic. They had easy control over the weather and the elements and used such forces in battle.

Activities
The lamas cared for and contemplated the koi and also mined for gold in the mountains north of Nad Ho Ting. Some estimated them to be the wealthiest gompa in all Tabot, though if was the case, then they showed no sign of it, so they might be the most parsimonious of them too.

History
It was founded some time between  and, when fifteen groups of monks and temple guardians escaped persecution in Shou Lung and journeyed southwest in search of a holy land that would remain pure and lasting, ideal for isolated hermitages. One of these groups built the beginnings of M'tsin.

In, a fire razed over half of the nearby city of Fij and left countless people homeless. The M'tsin lamas sent workers and emergency relief to Fij in an effort to rebuild before the cold winter month of Maki.

## Mtu Barua

Mtu Barua was a seneschal in the service of Ifan Talro'a, a merchant prince of Port Nyanzaru in Chult during the late 15 century DR.

Activities
He often made trips to Port Nyanzaru's Bazaar to run errands for his master.

History
During the events of the death curse, he contacted the Bazaar's Master of the Market to find an armorer who could supply titansteel armor.
