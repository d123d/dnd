## Qerria

Qerria was the leader of the Order of the Gilded Eye in Helm's Hold sometime after the War of the Silver Marches.

Activities
Qerria ruled Helm's Hold and the Gilded Eye. She was totally focused in finding and destroying evil under the guidance of Helm.

## Qelline Alderleaf

Qelline Alderleaf was a halfling farmer that lived in Phandalin.

Description
The farmer was very knowledgeable about the ongoings of the village she lived in. She was also said to be wise and practical. Qelline was kind enough to let adventurers stay in the hayloft of her home free of charge.

Relationships
She had a young adventurous son known as Carp. Qelline was old friends with the druid, Reidoth.

## Qen

Warrior Qen was a githyanki guard from Crèche Y'llek, on Toril during the late 15 century DR.

Description
Qen was a gith with gaunt body and leathery yellow skin. He had long brown hair and he was adorned in a set of heavy githyanki armor and armed with a gith greatsword.

History
Warrior Qen, along with other gith of what would become Crèche Y'llek, attacked and claimed the Rosymorn Monastery of Lathander in the Western Heartlands of West Faerûn sometime before the . Soon after, the function of Crèche was changed as the githyanki war against the mind flayers in Faerûn shifted in balance. Qen stood guard in the Crèche's hatchery and showed stern determination to his duty.
