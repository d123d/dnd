## Xûl-Jarak

Xûl-Jarak, also called The Gray Citadel because of its gray ashen walls, was an orc fortress that had long been in disrepair by the 1370s DR. It was situated two days ride north of Melvaunt along the road to Glister, in the land of Thar. It sat atop a 20-ft-high (7.1 m) barren crag, with another two stories of fortifications. The four levels of dungeons beneath the fortress had an entrance to the Underdark. Within the dungeons was the crypt of King Kursk One-Tusk the Merciless.

## Xuna

Xuna was a rogue living in Neverwinter in the late years of the 15 century DR.

History
Xuna was one of the survivors of the first attack of the Thayan forces on Neverwinter during the Shadovar–Thay War. Her parents were killed during that battle, her mother by a dracolich, and her father by a powerful undead warrior.

In 1479 DR, Xuna took part in the Siege of Neverwinter. She fought against the same undead warrior that killed her father, and although she had the upper hand at first, eventually she was overpowered. She was rescued from certain death by the cleric Celeste. Xuna later joined forces with Celeste and Sergeant Knox to defeat the undead before meeting Valindra's army at the head of a force of Neverwinter's defenders.

Later, Xuna accompanied Elminster and the factions of the North to the Dragonbone Vale, where the allied forces sought to thwart a plot by the Cult of the Dragon. There, Xuna was put in charge of supply lines for the Shield of the North encampment.

## Xunqaq

Xunqaq was the capital city of the beholder realm of Xun'Qoroth.

## Xult's magical doom

Xult's magical doom was an arcane alteration spell that sealed an item against unwanted perusal.

Effects
This spell would seal an item against unwanted intrusion or perusal. The item to be sealed was traced with runes by the caster's fingertip during casting. Only the caster, anyone named by the caster during the casting, or anyone speaking the special word of deactivation specified by the caster could use the item without triggering the runes. The runes could be removed with a successful dispel magic spell.

If triggered, the runes would release the magical doom that affected all creatures within  of the item. The magical doom permanently polymorphed affected creatures into a bat, frog, snake, or toad. Polymorphed creatures possessed the same intelligence they had in their previous form, assuming their transformation did not drive them insane, so they could possibly communicate their plight to others or even use a magic item to restore their form.

The magical doom placed upon an item would last until triggered. The skill of the caster determined how many times their magical doom could be triggered before expiring and needing to be recast.

Components
In addition to verbal and somatic components, this spell required as material components a pinch of dust and a caterpillar's cocoon to cast.

History
Cassana, a wicked member of the Fire Knives, was fond of Xult's magical doom.
