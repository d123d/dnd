## Xzar

Xzar was an insane necromancer and a member of the Zhentarim who traveled the Sword Coast during the mid–14 century DR.

Personality
Xzar was both intelligent and insane at the same time. He was quite unbalanced and would randomly spout gibberish at unpredictable moments; at one moment he could act like a frightened child while the next he may remark that he ate another person's liver paired with beans and a nice wine.

His treatment of others was usually coldly disdainful or derisive.

Relationships
The Zhentarim partnered Xzar with the halfling Montaron, a pairing that was made against both their wishes. Xzar found 'Monty' to be quite insufferable, while he in turn thought Xzar completely mad. After some time however, it appeared as if the two had developed genuine care for one another.

History
In the, Xzar and Montaron were tasked by the Zhentarim to investigate the iron crisis that plagued the Sword Coast. They traveled south along the Coast Way to the Amnian town of Nashkel.In the Baldur's Gate (novel), Xzar and Montaron rescue Abdel Adrian from a horde of gibberlings before joining him on his journey to the Friendly Arm Inn and Nashkel.

By the, Xzar and Montaron had traveled to Athkatla to spy on the local group of Harpers led by Galvarey. At that time Xzar had employed a pair of apprentices, Prebek and Sanasha. The trio were conducting experiments involving the creation of horrific monsters.

Montaron was captured by the Harpers at some point and to Xzar's knowledge was taken to the local Harper Hold. While Xzar sought a means to rescue his friend, Montaron had in fact already been killed. In the same novel, Xzar and Montraon never make it to Athkatla, but are slain by Sarevok during the Iron Crisis.

## Xzorsh

Xzorsh (pronounced with a click followed by "zzorshhh") was a sea elf ranger dwelling in the Trackless Sea.

Personality
Xzorsh was a ranger who was quite curious and relatively open-minded. He always had an interest in the arcane arts, but because of his background was never able to develop that interest.

History
Hrolf, captain of the Elfmaid, saved Xzorsh's life when he was a child. From that point forward, Xzorsh pledged to protect the Elfmaid and all of her crew to the best of his ability. He was particularly involved in this promise in 1361 DR, when the Elfmaid was being pursued by the Kraken Society of Ascarle and by Rethnor of Luskan.

Xzorsh was killed in 1361 DR by a drow group seeking Liriel Baenre. Brindlor, disguised as a human, tricked Ibn into giving a silvery amulet to Xzorsh, thinking that it had to do with Liriel's promise of magical tutelage. When he slipped it around his neck, he was teleported to an unknown location, where Brindlor and Gorlist were waiting for him. Still under the assumption that they were his tutors, he went with them willingly. After giving them some gems that would help them track Liriel down, they swiftly murdered him.

Relationships
Sittl
Sittl, another sea elf, was Xzorsh's partner and close friend. He turned out to be a malenti (a mutated sahuagin appearing as a sea elf) working for Ascarle.

Liriel Baenre
Despite their very different backgrounds, Xzorsh and the drow mage Liriel Baenre developed a friendship of sorts while she was aboard the Elfmaid in 1361 DR. In exchange for his help, she promised to help him learn the ways of arcane magic—a promise she quickly regretted, offering to set him up with a tutor instead.

## Xzand're Hsyl'ran

Xzand're Hsyl'ran  was a member of Bregan D'aerthe active in Waterdeep during the late 15 century DR.

Personality
Xzand're was an clever and creative woman, but suffered from overconfidence.

Possessions
Xzand're possessed a hat of disguise, which looked like a red silk ribbon embroidered with gold thread. She used it to take the appearance of Meggin the innkeeper. She also possessed a poisonous pistol which she used to dispatch her foes.

Activities
Xzand're ran a smuggling ring in the Dock Ward of Waterdeep. In addition she managed the Ship's Prow tavern under the guise of Meggin, one of her victims.

Relationships
Xzand're was deeply loyal to Jarlaxle Baenre, but lost his favors. In an attempt to regain his good graces, she distributed false treasure maps to thwart his competitors while he was looking for Dagult Neverember's dragon cache.

History
Circa the, Xzand're assassinated Meggin in order to take over her inn. She kept the young woman's corpse salted, in a barrel, within a hidden room in the the city's sewers below her shop.
