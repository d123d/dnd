## Xhalph Dlardrageth

Xhalph DlardragethHis full name is confirmed in the glossary at the end of Forsaken House. was a half-fiend member of the daemonfey.

Description
His skin was olive green and heavily scaled in parts, especially his pointed ears, made more prominent by his baldness. Xhalth had six arms, having inherited the four arms of his father, and had a stronger build than any human. He disdained covering his heavily muscled body, thinking his form would better intimidate his enemies when seen.

Relatives
He was reported to be either the offspring of Countess Sarya Dlardrageth's twin brother Khuumal and a marilith, a cousin to Ryvvik, or the son of Sarya herself and a glabrezu.

Activities
He once hatched a plot to unseat Sarya and lead the organization himself, but after learning that she had killed her own son Ryvvik, he wisely reconsidered.

History
After killing Seiveril Miritar in Myth Drannor in the last battle of the Cormanthor War, he alongside most of the remaining fey'ri tried to escape inside the Waymeet but at last was killed fighting Fflar Starbrow Melruth.

Equipment
Xhalth wielded the Blade Dlardrageth and Khuumal's baldric.

## Xharva Deem

Xharva Deem was a dao in service to the Cult of the Black Earth at the Temple of the Black Earth in Tyar-Besil during the Elemental Evil crisis.

History
In 1491 DR, Marlos Urnrayle made a pact with Xharva Deem in order to use the dao's abilities to manage the forge for the Black Earth. She respected the pact and worked at the forge with two duergar assistants.

## Xhost

Xhost was a treacherous glabrezu demon.

Personality
Xhost was happy to deceive even beings who helped him, through offering them cursed items such as the Gift of the Demon.

History
In the, Xhost was discernible using the Spectacles of Spectacle in Avernus, and could potentially be summoned to that infernal realm.
