## -30000 DR

Circa: Dragons all over Faerûn gather in the first Flight of Dragons to assault the aearee in the sky, on land, and underground. The Aearee Empires fall, as Viakoo is burned from the air, and Shara is conquered by the dragon-general Nagamat. This begins the Time of Dragons.
 Circa: Draconic religious factions begin to fight over their different philosophies. The most violent conflicts are those fought among the followers of Asgorath.

 Circa: The start of the War of the Seldarine as the goddess Araushnee, later known as the demon-goddess Lolth, is banished to the Demonweb Pits. Vhaeraun, his mother's accomplice, is simply exiled from Arvandor. Eilistraee foresees times of hardships for her people, the dark elves; in order to be with them when they would need her the most, and to provide them a beacon of hope, she chooses to share her mother's and brother's punishment despite being cleared from any guilt. Malkizid, a solar, receives a brand for his actions and is exiled to the Nine Hells.

 Circa: Annam All-Father, god of the giants, and Othea, a lesser demigoddess of Abeir-Toril marry and have eight terrestrial children. Annam founds Ostoria in their honor. This marks the beginning of the Time of Giants.

## -3000 DR

__NOTOC__

 The drow city of Sshamath experienced a civil war. All matriarchs who worship Lolth are killed and the house wizards take power.

 Kuo-toa establish Sloopdilmonpolop, the City of Pools, off the coast of Tethyr in the Underdark.

## 300 DR

Qysara Shoon V of the Shoon Imperium dies of a fever.

 By this time, satraps of the Shoon Imperium rule all the lands of the Tashalar and greater Thindol, the cities of Lapaliiya, and every major settlement in the Shaar west of the Landrise.
 The theocracy of Minsorran consecrates the "Lands of Balance" surrounding Lake Weng in later-day Amn.

 Representatives of Turmish and Chondath stage mock wars on Shieldmeet, a custom that continues for centuries to come.
 The Academia Vilhonus, a bardic college, is established in the Vilhon Reach. It inspires the Turmish custom of painting dots on one's forehead to indicate learning.

## 309 DR

Halaster Blackcloak becomes ruler of Undermountain after executing Halaster's Hunts between 171 DR and 308 DR to destroy or drive out the resident drow and duergar.

 Karazir Tiiraklar, Queen's Consort of Tethyr, and his brother Ellessor lead army in conquest of the so-called uncivilized lands east of the realm, despite them having long been home to small mountain settlements of humans, elves, dwarves, and other humanoid races.

 The duchy of Elestam is established, with Ellessor Tiiraklar as its duke.
 In the Hungtse River valley in Shou Lung, the port city of Cham Fau is established. The White Tiger monastery nearby also finishes construction.
