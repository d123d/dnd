## 34 DR

Aleph yn Alpeh el Shoon, who would go on to become Shoon II of the Shoon Imperium, is born as the youngest of a set of twins.

 Verraketh, a Talfirian bard, comes across the Shadowstar, which fell as a shooting star and landed in the High Moor. Verraketh is gradually transformed into the Shadowking.

 Verraketh assembles an army of shadow magicians and begins conquering the Netherese and Talfir in the Chionthar river valley to forge the empire of Ebenfar.
 From deep in the Underhalls of Undermountain, a large drow army invades Melairbode, killing King Melair IV and forcing the dwarves to retreat further into their home.
 As Serpentes declines, the Se'Sehen yuan-ti tribe is weakened and, over a decade-long war, driven out of the Tashalar by their human subjects.

 The Oligarchs of Tashluta found the Confederation of Tashtan, laying claim to all the land between the town of Narubel and the Sheir Peninsula, as well as the coastal lands of the Shining Sea in between, including Lapaliiya. All the humans living in this area are eventually known as Tashalans.

## 346 DR

Following the Darkwoods Massacre of 335 DR, courts-martial find seven elf officers of the Akh'Velahr guilty of cruel treatment of non-elven soldiers and culpable for the slaughter. They are exiled from Myth Drannor and Cormanthyr.

 For his failure to notice the problems among his subordinates, Arms-Major Pirphal Faerondaryl tenders his resignation, but is refused by Coronal Eltargrim Irithyl, who orders him to resolve the issues. Therefore, Pirphal takes three new lieutenants—one human, one dwarf, one halfling—and commissions the Baneblades of Demron for them to wield.

 Making an expedition into the western mountains, Saeval Ammath discovers and keeps a red dragon's egg, and keeps it secret. He bases himself in a House Ammath tower outside Myth Drannor to begin work on the egg.
