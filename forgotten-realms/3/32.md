## 322 DR

The blue dragon Iryklathagra lands in Qysar's Square in Shoonach before the Imperial Palace, and demands Shoon VII meet with her, in response to his great-great-great-grandfather Shoon IV's betrayal in 236 DR. ## 324 DR

The organization known as the Harpers at Twilight are founded at the Elven Court by Dathlue Mistwinter, the Lady Steel. The Mistwinter family crest—a silver harp between the horns of a crescent moon, encircled by stars on a black field—is used as the badge of the Harpers. The early members included elf, human, and half-elf warriors, rangers, druids, thieves, and mages, with Ioelena Audark, Elminster, Ruehar Oakstaff, and Lyari Pholont "the Shadesilk" among them.
