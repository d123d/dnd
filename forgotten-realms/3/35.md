## -357 DR

Led by Bane, Bhaal and Myrkul, a coalition of Horreb artificers, war wizards of Barze, horde nomads of Varm, gnoll warriors of Flindyke, goliath hunters of Mount Akoro, and gnome hammers of Forharn unite to defeat the primordial Maram. Though thousands were killed, the army succeeds in reaching the Monument of the Ancients and driving Maram back into the Elemental Chaos, resealing the Anchor of Chaos. Many more die on the journey away from the battle; of 50,000 troops fielded, less than 2000 return.
 Karsus himself finally deigns to travel to Unity, where he personally slays or banishes all of the fiends infesting the city.

 The warleaders who defeated Maram abandon their weakened army. Magic-resistant diseases kill many more as the soldiers trudge back to their respective homes. In total, of the roughly 50,000 people who fought and won, only 2,000 make it back home.

## -356 DR

Less than a year after the binding of Maram of the Great Spear, the demonic general Tyranthraxus led his army of Twisted Ones to devastate the kingdom of Barze.
 After two successful incursions from Shou Lung, the northern half of Tabot is lost to the invaders. Artisans from Ji craft five crystal warriors which are infused with magic by a group of powerful shukenjas and wu jen. With the help of the constructs, the tattered army of Tabot manages to reclaim the lost territory but the crystal warriors then disappear into the mountains.

 The netherese town of Grog is transformed into a ghost town when phaerimm lifedrain magic turns its fields to sand.

## 356 DR

By this time, the realm of Valashar (and with it the Shoon Empire) reaches the Troll Mountains and the High Moor in the north, following thirty-five years of expansion under King Ashar Tornamn. Ashar makes further claims on the northern lands, and expands toward the western border of Cormyr.

 In response, King Azoun I launches a campaign against Valashar, destroying its armies on the Fields of the Dead. The Cormyreans go on to sack the city of Ithmong and countless garrisons. They drive Shoon Empire back to the Giant's Run Mountains. Lord Tornamn is executed.

## 358 DR

Qysar Shoon VII of the Shoon Empire visits the subject kingdom Valashar when he and the dragon Iryklathagra start fighting again. Valashar bears the brunt of the destruction.

 Lann Cymrych, future High King of the Moonshae Isles, is born to Cornall Cymrych.

 The wizard Saeval Calaudra takes to flying upon the back of the dragon Garnetallisar, becoming famous among the people of Myth Drannor. Saeval's enchantments upon Garnet's egg have resulted in the dragon being lawful good, and he shows himself to be trustworthy and humble. The coronal allows Saeval and Garnet to dwell within the mythal, making an exception to its wards against chromatic dragons.

 The first three Baneblades are completed by Demron. They are ceremonially presented to their wielders, lieutenants of the Akh'Velahr.
