## 366 DR

Qysar Shoon VII of the Shoon Imperium and the blue dragon Iryklathagra wage their final battle in the skies over the city of Shoonach and in the streets of its slaves' district. Iryklathagra shatters the bones and nearly bites off Shoon's right leg and left arm, giving her the nickname "Sharpfangs".

 Rapid population growth of the city of Khôltar nearly bankrupts the local government by necessitating the expansion of the city's walls every few years. The dwarves of the Great Rift create an endowment to fund construction, releasing money for a visionary dwarf named Dunsel to begin work on a road from Eartheart to Khôltar. The Dunsel Trail eventually runs all the way to Shaarmid on the Golden Road.

## 367 DR

Pretending to be incapacitated following his battle with the dragon Iryklathagra, Shoon VII installs his daughter Shaani as ruler of the Shoon Imperium. He then manipulates her into poisoning him, but is secretly immune to the poison, thus faking his own death. Shoon then uses the opportunity to research how to transform himself into a lich.

 On the north coast of the Dragons' Sea, the city of Phlan is established.
