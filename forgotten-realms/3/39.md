## -391 DR

The Ivory Plague strikes Shou Lung, killing the first Emperor Chin of the Hai Dynasty and his household. Only the emperor's sister and his grandson survive. The emperor's sister disguises herself as her brother and rules in his place, assisted by the bureaucracy and good spirits, until the grandson comes of age. She later reveals her true identity and escapes the city, and is not seen again. Her reign was later recognized as the second Emperor Chin of the Hai Dynasty, and her grandnephew as the third Emperor Chin. This begins the custom of allowing female regents, though still referred to as 'Emperor'.

## 390 DR

Jarissra Obarskyr, former queen of Cormyr.

 Following the death of her lover and apprentice in a hunting accident, the Srinshee, High Mage of Cormanthyr and Myth Drannor, enters a decade-long period of mourning. Although the Srinshee and others suspect one of the noble houses to behind the death, this is never proved. The Srinshee remains in mourning colors for the rest of her life.

 In the lands that became Amn, the city of Myth Lharast is established. Its role is as a temple city dedicated purely to Selûne the Moonmaiden, as opposed to the orthodox theocracy in Minsorran.

 Worship of Selûne also begins on the slopes of Mount Waterdeep.
