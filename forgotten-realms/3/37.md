## 374 DR

Midsummer Night: The House of Silvanus, later the Emerald Enclave, is founded on Ilighôn.

 Shieldlord Khôltar is assassinated by agents of a fellow dwarf, Inbrurr Harlenstar, in an elaborate plot meant to throw suspicion on the human population of Khôltar. The second part of his plan failed utterly and he fled into the Underdark, self-fulfilling his exile from the Deep Realm.

 After the assassination of Amberu Khôltar, a new Shieldlord by the name of Onskrar Hammershield is appointed by the dwarves of the Great Rift. To quell angry residents, he forms a citizen council to hear complaints from the populace, draft laws, and advise the Shieldlord on matters of city government.

 High King Cameron Cymrych of the Moonshae Isles dies of natural causes.

 Bran Cymrych becomes High King of the Moonshae Isles.

 Circa: One of the Tears of Selûne falls to Faerûn, landing in the Farsea Marshes on Midwinter. From the site rises a goddess, claiming to be Bright Nydra, the daughter of Selûne and Shaundakul. Though Bright Nydra will eventually be absorbed by her mother to become one of her aspects, the Marsh Drovers of the Farsea Marshes will continue to venerate her as the head of their local pantheon as a goddess of hope and the transition of winter into spring.

## -375 DR

Pasha Khalid el Axash of Calimshan is killed by the Empire Plague, bringing the Axash Dynasty to an end. His cousin Syl-Pasha Akkab el Evyrtaan succeeds him.
 The Empire Plague causes the Empire of Calimshan to lose control over the Calishar Emirates and the Arnaden Colonies which results in them fracturing into insular city-states until after the end of the Plague.
 Myth Dyraalis is established in the latter-day Forest of Mir. It forms a sanctuary for elves and gnomes fleeing their foes.

 The Empire Plague afflicts the southern Shining Coast, and lasts for five years. For this first command, Syl-Pasha Akkab el Evyrtaan orders the city of Calimport be burned to end the plague, but the flames refuse to burn. Akkab flees the city.
