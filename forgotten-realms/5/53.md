## -5360 DR

Karlyn of House Kuldever leads the dwarves of High Shanatar against the giants of Nedeheim, in the Giant Wars. Over the next sixty years, over five thousand giants—over half the giant population south of the Cloud Peaks—are wiped out and Nedeheim fragments into scattered clanholds.

Appendix
 Notes
While The Grand History of the Realms marks the Giant Wars as spanning only a single decade, Lost Empires of Faerûn lists it as a sixty-year period. The date in the Grand History is almost certainly a typo, as the very next entry in that tome, dated -5330 DR, mention the Giant Wars still being fought.

## 533 DR

Mameluk slave warriors in Calimshan revolt and seize control of Calimport and several other major cities.

 The free mameluks found a new country free of slavery. As Calimport was traditionally a port for slavers, the mameluk leaders base their capital in Manshaka, shifting the caleph's throne there. The mameluk leader adopts the title of Murabir, or Warlord, a title not used since the time of Mir.
