## 554 DR

Hammer 3: The House of Reverence to the Dark Lady, a palatial temple of Shar, is assaulted by adventurers called the Fellowship of Athul's Sash.  A wagon train of loot leaves the site, claimed by the last surviving member of the group, Rulgond, who murdered his remaining Fellows out of greed. Among the treasure is Gorothir's Girdle.
 To prevent the Dambrathan invasion of Halruaa, the wizard Mycontil Sulaziir and his apprentices sacrifice themselves to defeat King Reinhar I. With his father's death, Reinhar II takes command of the army and retreats to Dambrath.

 Following Dambrath's defeat in Halruaa, the captured nations of Luiren, Estagund, and Var seize their independence.
 Estagund unites under King Bonrial.
 In newly independent Var, noble, mercantile, and religious factions struggle to decide who would be the next Rajah, triggering the Golden War.

## 557 DR

A hobgoblin army causes much destruction across the dwarf duchy of Hunnabar. The hobgoblins are defeated by the forces of Phalorm, though the elf king of Phalorm is killed.
 Yuan-ti sent by Nejizar infiltrate the Reaching Woods and incite the hobgoblin tribes there to attack Phalorm. The hobgoblins are driven back and the yuan-ti assassinated. The serpentfolk of Najara fall back into the Serpent Hills.

 The Golden War in Var ends as Muwadeen of Estagund, a retired mercenary, negotiates peace between the warring factions. All three factions—noble, mercantile, and religious—surprisingly agree for once and invite Muwadeen to serve as the Supreme Potentate of Var, assisted by a council of advisers drawn their ranks.
