## 591 DR

King Reinhar II of Dambrath dies.

 Reinhar III succeeds his father as king of Dambrath.
 Mayor Bynan "Son-Axe" Oakfeller of Silverymoon retires and a popular farmer named Cassius Durind replaces him.

 The Oakengrove Abbey, a fortified temple of Silvanus is founded. It is constructed roughly 70 mi (112 km) west of the Standing Stone in Cormanthor.

## 598 DR

In Merrydale, Jander Sunstar, while in a combat with vampires, is betrayed by a previously transformed friend and is transformed into a vampire himself.The date of this event is based on the passage on page 37 of the Villains' Lorebook which states "before the Mists took him...he was a vampire for 500 years in the Realms." On page 36 it says that Jander was transported to the Demiplane of Dread in 1098 DR, therefore giving his turning date as roughly 598 DR. However, the vampire infestation of Merrydale occurred in 796 DR according to The Grand History of the Realms, page 103. These dates seem irreconcilable without further information.

## -598 DR

In Novularond, a group of arctic dwarves discover a catapult that belonged to frost giants. They take it back to their village, and refuse to return it to them. The frost giants destroy an arctic dwarf village in response. The elders of Gronne intervene, who volunteer to negotiate the dispute to prevent a war. They find the arctic dwarves guilty of theft, and the thieves are turned over to the frost giants. The prisoners become slaves and their descendants remain enslaved to the 14 century DR.
