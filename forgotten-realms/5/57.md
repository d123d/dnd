## 576 DR

Discovering a route to the Underdark under the One Stone ancestor mound, the Red Pony and Golden Eagle tribes of the Uthgardt disappear into the Night Below.

 The sage Alvaerele Tasundrym becomes Magister after Magister Annath Sunfall, because of his failed experiments, renounces Mystra and all magic.

 Tarsakh: Rulgond, the incredibly wealthy leader of the Black Star Band of adventurers is betrayed by three of his men. Two survive: one, Orgloth claims leadership of the Band and Rulgond's wealth and the other claims Gorothir's Girdle.

 The first volume of the nine-volume The Deepwalker Chronicles, written by Halgar "Deepwalker" Ankarkyn of Tethyr, is published.

## 574 DR

As one of few trading posts that flourish in the North, Silver Village and becomes Silverymoon. Bynan "Two-Axes" Oakfeller, a local logger known for his kindness, becomes the first mayor of Silverymoon.

 The threat of orcs grows along the northeastern forest of Cormanthor. Myth Drannor makes preparations for war and Pirphal commissions Demron to forge a fifth Baneblade for the Arms-Major of the Akh'Velahr. Dragathil is completed within the year.
