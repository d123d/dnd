## 585 DR

Aiming to conquer Halagard, capital of Halruaa, a fleet of Dambrathan galleys tries to venture into the channel leading to Lake Halruaa. The Halruaans assault the vessels with magic cast from both land and skyship, destroying nearly the entire fleet.

 Aunrae and Ildibane Nasadra forge a Spidersblood Pact.

## -585 DR

## 580 DR

Ecamane Truesilver is the first human to discover the Quess'Ar'Teranthvar, a transformed Nether Scroll in Windsong Tower. After ten months of meditation and study of the artifact, Ecamane learns its magic and knowledge, but his hair turns prematurely white from the experience. Afterward, he leaves Windsong Tower.

 In northern Kozakura, the Zakura Insurrection begins against the Empress.

 The final volume of the nine-volume The Deepwalker Chronicles, written by Halgar "Deepwalker" Ankarkyn of Tethyr, is published.
