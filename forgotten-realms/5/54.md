## -54 DR

Black Alaric the Pirate, the exiled cousin of Clan Chief Darius Fyrson of Tethyr, leads a band of pirates to settle the Nelanther isles. They prey upon growing sea trade and steal ships.
 The Aluianti families of gold elves leave Cormanthor and found a village just outside of Semberholme.

 After Black Alaric fails in an attempt to capture Helbrester for himself, the Sunset Towers of Helbrester become a neutral meeting ground for all residents and visitors to the Nelanthers.
