## Egeissag

Egeissag was a male drow captain of the troops of Sendai.

Powers
He was highly skilled in melee combat and could cast the most difficult wizard spells.

Possessions
Captain Egeissag fought with two enchanted drow scimitars and drow elven chain mail. He also kept the Bowstring of Gond, an artifact that could be used to upgrade the strongest of bows.

History
When Gorion's Ward arrived to clear the enclave, Egeissag's unusual spectator made a suggestion to the captain. The beholderkin suggested that even if he and his group slay the ward, the credit would go to Sendai, so the captain should challenge him to one-on-one combat. Egeissag saw the wisdom in the beholderkin's suggestion, as when Sendai reported successful, she would tell the matron mothers that it was all her work. The terms were that whoever lost would perish amongst everyone else they were allied with in the room. If the duel was accepted, the spectator made use of a geas spell, so that the terms of the agreement were absolute.

Ultimately, the ward won the battle, and all of Egeissag's allies died, except for the queer spectator. The spectator, of course, didn't subject himself to the geas. He went off to "find the nearest hive" and "check out the ladies".

## Eghagu

Eghagu was a small village located in the Alpuk region, within the Great Glacier.

Geography
Eghagu was located northeast of the Shistak Pass in the Keryjek Ridge.

Layout
The village was built in the center of a stone ring  in height. This wall of stone protected the village, with its crude snowhouses, from the harsh climate common to the Great Glacier.

Inhabitants
The village of Eghagu has developed an unsavory reputation due to the activities of its residents. The inhabitants of the village conducted raids on neighboring settlements, stealing caribou and roamed the countryside as bandits, ambushing travelers. They have even trained kupuk and winter wolves to attack targets.

History
Around 1260 DR, a group of citizens of Lilinuk were exiled for plotting to murder the iquemelum. These exiles joined together with similarly banished Angulutiuns from Hupik and founded the village.

## Egalavin Zauvirr

Egalavin Zauvirr was a noble of House Zauvirr that joined Bregan D'aerthe in the early 11 century DR.

History
Egalavin hailed from House Zauvirr in Ched Nasad. In 1018 DR, he was one of the few true followers of matron mother Sheeva Zauvirr that followed her after she was exiled from Ched Nasad. However, Jarlaxle, Arathis Hune, and Zaknafein were hired to kill Sheeva and lured Sheeva's group, including Egalavin, into a trap. During the fighting, only Egalavin and two other males survived and Jarlaxle offered them a chance to join the growing Bregan D'aerthe.

In 1488 DR, Egalavin, together with Braelin Janquay, acted as support for the resurrected Zaknafein at Bregan D'aerthe's meeting with House Hunzrin in the Waterdeep sewers.

## Ego whip

Ego whip was a psionic power of the telepathy discipline that assaulted a foe's ego.

Effects
This power assailed the ego, confidence, self-esteem, and individuality of a single subject with feelings of their inferiority and worthlessness, or else of the manifester's superiority and megalomania. It rapidly lashed their mind like a glowing whip, its crack cutting open deep-seated feelings of inadequacy. If they lacked the willpower to resist, the victim felt incapable and insignificant. Initially, this left them dazed for up to 4 minutes and unable to perform as well as they once could, and even unable to use all but the lowest spells. After the Year of Wild Magic, 1372 DR, they were only dazed for several seconds, but the damage to their confidence was lasting.

Ego whip was originally one of the five attacks of psionic combat and was a way of making forced contact. Against a closed mind, it drained psionic energy or else opened their mind to contact. Initially, intellect fortress offered the best protection against ego whip, thought shield the weakest, but after 1372 DR, thought shield was the best defense while tower of iron will was weakest.

Prerequisite Powers
It could require contact and mindlink to learn.

Display
Manifesting the power produced the standard auditory display.

Significance
"Ego Whipper" was the title of the third rank of the clergy of Deep Duerra, the duergar goddess of psionics.

Users
Ego whip could be learned by psionicists and psions, as well as by wilders.

It could be known by blue goblins, callers in darkness, cerebriliths, crysmals, embracs, adult intellect devourers, and ikiryo.

In psionics terms, it could also be known by duergar, aboleth, ki-rin,The creatures filed under "in psionics terms" are those who were first stated out with magical powers and rules, which in some cases were described as 'psionics' in fluff text, and then later got stated or revised with actual psionics powers and rules once The Complete Psionics Handbook came out. mind flayers, titans, and by githyanki and githzerai, and by astral devas, solars, and planetars among aasimon; by cornugons, gelugons, and pit fiends among baatezu; by babaus, balors, glabrezus, nalfeshnees among tanar'ri; and only by arcanaloths among yugoloths.

Notable Users

## Egbert Dulkaster

Egbert Dulkaster the Third was a rich and self-important member of one of the most successful Waterdeep family mercantile organizations, the Dulkaster and Sons who has managed a good part of the Sword Coast trading for centuries in the late 14 century DR.

Description
Egbert Dulkaster was a middle-aged male always dressed in well-tailored expensive elegant silk clothes of the latest fashion. He had poshly-groomed mustache with graying silvery strands of hair.

Egbert Dulkaster's voice was described as slippery, reminiscent of a melting icicle, dripping with water.

Personality
Egbert was quite pompous, eloquent, and was surrounded by the air of superiority. He did not like to mingle with the commoners. He was excessively proud of his family name, and the family's prosperous trading business. Egbert was very easily deceived if her were to believe he was conversing with a sophisticated noble-person, especially if the person knew a thing or two about fashion.

Relationships
Egbert Dulkaster was a valued client of Raffaldini of Waterdeep, a famous halfling tailor for the most wealthy noble families across Faerûn.

History
Egbert Dulkaster spent many summers growing up in Archendale, fishing with his grandfather in the River Arkhen.

In 1372 DR, Egbert Dulkaster the Third could be found within the Chateau Elite, the most expensive and pompous inn in the city, during his business visit to Daggerford. His room was just one door away from the spy Astriel Shalyn who was staying at the inn at  the same time. He was hiding inside his room, when the Zhentarim assassins attempted to assassinate her a few days after her arrival.

## Egg

Eggs were commonly eaten in Faerûn, such as in Cormyr in the mid–14 century DR. Rotten eggs were infamous for foul stench of sulfur.

Usages

 Fire-eggs, a dish commonly served by higher quality inns.
 Eggs could be poached and eaten on black bread for a quick breakfast.
 Eggs could also be pickled and kept in jars, though they could turn an unappetizing shade of green.
 The shell of an egg was a required material component of the spell Bigby's crushing hand.
 The recipe for Amnian Dragoon Soup called for four whole eggs.
 In parts of the continent of Kara-Tur, eggshells were blown-out and then filled with various types of powdery material to create eggshell grenades.

Notable Users

Businesses
 The Dryad's Dreams, a festhall in Mosstone, served an egg platter dish that was constructed out of variety of bird eggs, such as quail, swiftwing, grouse, and goose, and a generous selection of dips and spices to roll them in.
 In the Vast, north of Ravens Bluff, the swineherd Swithin Sigirdson and his family sold eggs from out of their farm home at Hog Hill at a price of 3 copper pieces per pair.
Groups
 Fresh eggs were considered a delicacy to the Bedine of Anauroch, who made an effort to protect bird nests from predators by propping up their trees and training thorny vines around the trees' trunks.

Trivia
Beholders did not enjoy the taste of hard-boiled eggs.

## Egg platter

The egg platter was one of the specialty dishes of the Dryad's Dreams, the only festhall in Mosstone, Tethyr.

Description
The egg platter was a collection of various hard-boiled bird eggs, such as quail, swiftwing, grouse, and goose. The eggs were shelled and served on a bed of mouth-burning hot spices and dips, ready to be rolled and eaten. The spices used for the dish were notorious for their heat, and it was recommended to have wine or beer handy to release the feeling of flames in one's mouth.

History
The egg platter was served at the Dryad's Dreams in the mid-to-late-14 century DR. The dish was praised by Volothamp Geddarm in one of his published travel guides.

## Eggbread

Eggbread, also known as eggfry bread, was a dish cooked by soaking slices of bread in eggbatter and frying.

Description
Eggbread was a savory treat that was never served as a dessert. Most commonly, it was served with various gravies, panfry drippings, and condiments made of spices like tamarind. Some travelers and caravaners were known for eating egg bread as a "desperation fare," dripped with molasses. However, that was not considered a proper way to consume the dish. The most common way to eat it was smothered in fat droppings from frying pork, topped with salted pork and cracklings.

Availability
In the City of Splendors, Waterdeep, circa 1370s DR, small eateries that traded in hot drinks and hot handfoods started cropping up throughout its various districts. By the, the city counted over 30 such establishments. One of the newly popular dishes found in many of them were eggbread buns topped with melted sugar.
The Oak-Father's Boon inn in Tethyrean town of Mosstone served medallions of eggbread with the signature bowl of hares dish – long-stewed hare meat in cloves, cinnamon, and leek.

## Egoist

A egoist was a type of psion who specialized in the psychometabolism discipline of psionics, honing their skills in the alteration of the psychobiology of their own body and nearby creatures, achieving incredible feats of healing and transformation. They were equivalent to the transmuters of arcane magic.

Abilities
Egoists almost exclusively utilized powers that fell within the purview of the psychometabolism discipline. Their specialization in this discipline allowed egoists to unlock a suite of powers that were unknown to other psions. These powers were as followed – animal affinity, chameleon power, ectoplasmic form, empathic transfer, fission, fusion, hustle, metamorphosis and greater metamorphosis, psionic restoration, psionic revivify, psychic vampire, psychofeedback, restore extremity, and thicken skin.

Being specialist in psychometabolism, egoists had a profound understanding of their own physiology. Harnessing the psionic energy of the mind, they could perform great feats of psi-biofeedback, altering physical properties of themselves and other creatures.

Beyond psionic powers, egoists were skilled in the art of autohypnosis.

Notable Egoists
 Ovdana Xothcorlar, a duergar egoist and psychic theurge who led the Duerran temple Spear of Conquest in Dunspeirrin.
 Selinah Varhast, a human egoist and assassin that hailed from Zhentil Keep.

## Egil Hakon

Egil Hakon was a dwarven cleric and mining foreman in Hal Stoutheart's Tethyamar expedition during the 1480s DR.

Description
Egil had gray hair and a tidy beard. He carried a pickaxe and small hammer.

Abilities

As a cleric, Egil could call upon the powers of fire storm, energy drain, and blade barrier. After being drawn to madness, he began using necromancy with enthusiasm to reanimate his former companions.

History

During the expedition, Egil worked in Mineshaft 214S with other miners as they searched for the jewels of ardor. His companion Brin Coldstone was actually the demon lord Graz'zt, who shared maddening whispers with Egil and drove him into a peculiar and murderous state. Egil proceeded to spontaneously and violently murder all his fellow miners with his pickaxe except for Hagen Silverthumb, who managed to outrun him. He then set about reanimating his victims as the undead, putting them to work mining the jewels and defending his tunnel from any intruders. Among his minions was an ancient dwarven battlerager.

Hagen escaped to the Tethyamar Mining Outpost to warn the others, and found Asta's hirelings from Cormyr ready to help. He sent them down into the mines to slay Egil, who happily welcomed the visitors before realizing they weren't going to let him neatly kill and reanimate them. After an intense fight, he fell, putting an end to the tragedy but leaving the cause of it all a mystery.

## Eggshell grenade

Eggshell grenades were a variety of diversionary or incapacitating thrown weapons unique to the lands of Kara-Tur that were commonly utilized by ninjas. Despite their name, these objects did not exhibit the explosive force typical of grenades, instead "bursting" with their contents.

Description
These devices were so named for traditionally being made of carefully blown-out eggshells, though it was not uncommon for one to find grenades made from the hollowed out shells of nuts, paper packets, or other materials that were small and easy to break. The interior of these objects would be filled with different types of powder in order to create a distraction or blind an opponent. Because of the materials they were made from, eggshell grenades were quite fragile.

Types
Dust Eggshell grenades filled with dust, designed to thrown at a singular foe's face in order to temporarily blind them.
Flashpowder Requiring a source of fire to activate, these eggshell grenades exploded in brilliant, flares of light that blinded all those looking in its direction.
Grease In the continent of Faerûn, some cat burglars utilized eggshell grenades filled with slippery oil to hinder pursuers.
Pepper Eggshell grenades filled with pepper were designed to hinder a singular foe, inducing choking and sneezing in the target as it got into their eyes and nose.
Poison smoke Much like those filled with flashpowder, these eggshell grenades required a fire source in order to activate. Once activated it released a foul cloud of smoke with an acidic stench. This lightly harmed those within its reaches like poison, blinding and incapacitating them.
Smoke Eggshell grenades designed to produce a cloud of smoke to obscure the thrower so that they may make an escape. If thrown into a source of fire, they would create an even denser area of smoke, comparable to a fog.

Notable Users
 Around the, a tengu in Kozakura used flashpowder grenades in conjunction with stilts and an oni costume to terrorize the people of Myudo village with his band of raiders, masquerading under the guise of "Dai-Oni".
 Hoseki, a ninja and sohei who worked for the Dai-Oni.
 Motoharu Kaijitsu, a ninja and yakuza adventurer from Kozakura.
 Naga, a yakuza in the town of Aru in Wa, was skilled in the use of eggshell grenades and often carried a pair.
 Nezumi and Rokan, a pair of ninjas on the Kozakuran island of Hinomoto.
 Saisho, a ninja and wu jen adventurer from Kozakura's Miyama Province.
 Sujin Makoto, the supreme leader of the Five Deadly Shadows in 1479 DR.

Trivia
Some were known to conceal eggshell grenades within shikomi-zue, another common weapon among ninjas.
