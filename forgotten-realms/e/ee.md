## Eel

Eels were aquatic creatures.

Species
Dreel
Dreel eels, sometimes known as trollfingers due to their appearance, were a species of river eel found in the River Reaching in the Western Heartlands. Their bodies were short, yet fat, and green-black in color. They subsisted upon algae and carrion, which kept the river's waters both clean and clear. They had a taste reminiscent of mucus, but could be quite nourishing, thus they were often fried with certain herbs or spices to mask their natural taste.

Electric
Electric eels were a rarely-sighted species in Faerûn that possessed the unique ability to discharge a field of electricity.

Kreel
Kreels were large eels domesticated by Athasian lizardmen.

Icewind

Icewind eels were a species endemic to the Sea of Moving Ice. They were a commonly served dish within taverns across the coast of Icewind Dale, often served battered and fried.

Lamprey
Lampreys were a species of leech-like eels.

Moonsea
Moonsea eels were a species endemic to the Moonsea.

Moray
Moray eels were large eels with a very dangerous bite.

Mkunga

Mkungas were a large species of eel endemic to the inland rivers of the Chultan Peninsula. They typically migrated downstream to the Trackless Sea to give birth in shallow, brackish inlets. Most would return upstream until they matured, though some would spend their whole lives within those estuary waters.

Onsler
Onsler eels were a species of freshwater eel that inhabited shallow streams and deep rivers throughout the Heartlands. They had pale skin, marked with dusty-white spots, and soft sucker mouths. They were sensitive to extreme temperatures and would die if water got either too cold or too hot. They were a rather passive and herbivorous species, subsisting on algae as well as aquatic mosses and lichen. They were capable of living longer than the average human; their bodies grew longer and broader as they aged, with young specimens being more cylindrical and older ones being more flatter. Their flesh had a pleasant texture, but was rather tasteless, thus they were typically doused in sauce or heavily seasoned.

Splar
Splars were a species of eel with wing-like fins that allowed them to leap from out of the water.

Suckertails
Suckertails were a variety of eels from Laerakond, highly appreciated by the people of Tarmalune due to their eating habits.

Vilhon
Vilhon eels were a species endemic to the Vilhon Reach. They had long and ribbon-like bodies, black in color with green mottling. They often proved difficult to catch by fishermen using nets. They were a local delicacy in Nimpeth.

Weed
Weed eels were a highly venomous species of eel, whose bite could kill even an adult human. Living in colonies in both fresh- and saltwater, they usually hid in the weeds near shore. They looked like seaweed to those untrained at spotting them

Wolf

Wolf eels, otherwise known as akoak by Ice Hunters, were a rare species endemic to the Sea of Fallen Stars. Their bodies were blue and had large crushing jaws capable of eating sea barbs, woolly crabs and oysters. Ice Hunters that swam alongside them during exceptionally warm summers noted that they were generally non-threatening and curious creatures.

Ecology
Usages
 Eel pie was a common dish across Faerûn.
 Eel was used in brassla, a traditional Luskan rice dish.
 It was quite common for inns of average quality in coastal areas of the Realms to serve roasted eel as the third part of a four-course meal.
 Live eels were served in Braundlae's Best.
 Snail-and-eel soup was made by the lizardfolk of the Vast Swamp around 1374 DR.
 Aurora's Whole Realms Catalogue sold eel skins at a price of 2 sp per  squared. The skin of eels was sometimes used to make boots — the minstrel Llewellyn the Loquacious was one known person to own such boots.
 Bandoliers, belts, and harnesses made from treated eelskin was common among all the sentient creatures of Serôs.
 Eelskin was also used to make clothing by watersoul genasi.
 In Tulmene the taverns the Fast Sails and the Sail At Sunset offered their patrons eel-filled tarts and salted eels respectively.
 In Waterdeep, eels could be found for sale in the city's fishmarkets. They were also one of the few things for sale during the winter.
 In Sumbria of the Blade Kingdoms, pickled eels were a common food item found in pantries, including the pantry of the Palace of Sumbria.

History
In 1217 DR, the Princess of Sumbria and an amateur sorceress Milianna Mannicci da Sumbria, managed to accidentally conjure a wild furry green glutinous creature in her attempts to master the Art. The wild thing raided the Palace of Sumbria's pantries, stealing dried hogfish and pickled eels. The perpetrator occasionally left half-eaten eels behind.

Notable Eels
 Ghartii Nung was an enormous,  long eel that once dwelt in the Yellow Sea off the coast of Koryo in Kara-Tur, until it was caught by local fishermen.

Rumors & Legends
In the 14 century DR, the moat surrounding the city of Sundabar was rumored to be filled with man-eating eels. Volothamp Geddarm noted this in his book, Volo's Guide to the North, and expressed doubt that those eels would survive each winter.

Trivia
 A couple of establishments in the Realms bore eel in their name, such as the The Tankard of Eels, Ornagar's Smoked Eels & More, and the Jellied Eel. Though despite its name, the latter did not serve its patrons eels.
 Aurora's Whole Realms Catalogue sold traps specifically designed for capturing river eels, which sold for around 3 gold pieces.

## Eel ship

Eel ships were small and crudely designed spelljammer ships.

Description
The simple design of eel ships, thought to be an early version of the lamprey ship, granted them a dangerous appearance. They were mostly employed in planetary defense fleets, as escorts in attack missions, or as pirate vessels. Their size also made them attractive for stealth operations. If rigged for aggressive missions, it was common for eel ships to be heavily armed, to the expense of crew comfort and landing capabilities.

Early eel ships were powered by furnace helms, but by the mid‒14 century DR most had been replaced by minor or major spelljamming helms.

An eel ship required a minimum crew of four to operate, but usually was held by a crew of 10 to 12. The maximum compliment it could hold without compromising its air supply was 20 individuals.

History
In the mid‒14 century DR, the elven admiral Icarus commanded a large fleet of elven spacecraft that contained five eel ships, among other types of spelljammers. Icarus operated exclusively in Realmspace.

Rumors & Legends
The origin of the eel ship design was unknown. It was rumored that they had been designed by an insectoid race such as the thri-kreen or intelligent giant wasps. The reasoning behind these rumors was the existence of a low and narrow crawl tunnel that led to the stern of the ship, too small for humans to stand up inside.

## Eeyal

Eeyal was a female tortle guide who operated out of Fort Beluarian in Chult in the 15 century DR.

History
By the age of 17, Eeyal had traveled from the Snout of Omgar to Fort Beluarian on foot over the course of a year, had seen Lake Luo, the garden palace of Nangalore, the gorge of Ataaz Muhahah, and even the ruins of Mezro. She had even seen the wreckage of The Narwhal and had befriended the weretiger that lived there.

Personality
Eeyal was seen as careful and wise, and quite cautious, constantly checking for traps when traveling in wilderness. She also had a healthy respect for the Flaming Fist, and would recommend that people who employed her as a guide should acquire the necessary paperwork for exploring the jungle. She also disliked underground areas like most tortles, and utilized a pair of self-crafted goggles to shield her eyes from volcanic ash.

Eel Street was a major road in the asteroid city of Bral.

Description
Eel Street began with an intersection with Lamp Street on the starboard side of the asteroid and ran to its port side at Anchor Street, covering most of the width of the Low City. Along the way, it crossed Hammer Street, Grand Street, and Dragon Street, for a total distance of roughly . It formed the trailing-edge borders of Giff-town and Shou-town.

## Eel pie

Eel pie was a savory pie made with eel meat as a filling.

Availability
 The Broken Lance, a cozy tavern in Waterdeep's Sea Ward, had eel pies as its house specialty.
 In Cormyr, this dish was quite common and popular in the city of Marsember. In Suzail, cheese-and-eel pies were the specialty of The Eel Revealed eatery on the Promenade.
 In Neverwinter, some impoverished folk were known to bake eel pie. It was also sold in the city by the Shining Serpent Inn.
 In the coastal areas of Turmish, eel pies were a staple of local cuisine, often served along with fishhead soup, chives, and asparagus.

## Eel Revealed

The Eel Revealed was a popular eatery on the grand Promenade of the Cormyrean capital city of Suzail in 15 century DR.

Location
The eatery stood on the westernmost part of the second block of buildings off Suzail's Horngate. It was located across the Promenade from the crossing of the Mayhap Lane and the Hunt Bide.

Services
The Eel Revealed was a popular restaurant famous for its cheese-and-eel pies, sausages, a selection of fortified wines, and strapping young jacks and wenches who served them while flexing their oiled bodies. Another dish served was a big platter of tender cooked morels and eels in sardragon sauce (disdainfully called "Marsembian glop" in Castle Obarskyr's kitchens).

The restaurant was open for the early dinner service and early hours of the dawn.

History
In the late 14 century DR, Suzailan Lord Arclath Delcastle was a loyal customer of the eatery. Two of the beautiful young oiled waitresses at the time was Emsra and Varimbra, and a cook named Laethla worked the Eel's kitchens.

## Eenoro vine

Eenoro vine was a species of plants found on the Malatran Plateau in Kara-Tur.

Usages
The main known use for eenoro vine was the creation of a special medicinal oil known as oil of the eenoro vine or shan sao balm. The substance was an oily green concoction. When the oil was applied to the skin of a creature, it healed some of its broken tissue, cured poisons, toxins, and healed diseases.

## Eef

Sharp-eye Eef was a goblin cultist and devotee the Absolute who lived in the Shattered Sanctum during late 15 century DR.

Description
He wore simple clothing accented with a fashionable green neck-wrap and leather-and-bone bracers. He also wore a crude, leather skullcap and an amulet bearing the holy symbol of the Absolute.

He was among the goblin cutlists branded with the mark of the Absolute.

Possessions
In battle, Eef wielded a simple wooden bow.
