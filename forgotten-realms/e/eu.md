## Euclase

Euclase was a rare precious stone found in the form of small crystals.

Description
Euclase was easily cut into faceted gems and came in various colors ranging from colorless to pale yellow, vivid yellow, pale green, and blue. The blue hues commanded the highest price. A typical specimen had a base value of 500 gp.

Powers
These gemstones had a dangerous and violet reaction to magic being cast upon them or upon a being wearing or carrying one. If successfully hit with a spell, a flame strike erupted from the euclase, destroying it in the process and damaging any creature or object in the area of effect.

## Eudhis Bhamm

Eudhis Bhamm was a human male herbalist and the proprietor of Bhamm's Herbs in Eshpurta, Amn, in the Year of the Shield, 1367 DR through at least early 1370 DR, the Year of the Tankard. He had a surly disposition and was quick on the rejoinder. He was usually nervous and with good reason: he was a wizard in a land that had nearly outlawed wizardry. With much persuasion, he could be convinced to allow potential customers into his "compounding room" where he had an almost complete stock of spell components for every occasion—at double the usual price.

## Eulagad

Eulagad the Maul was an innkeeper in Skullport.

Personality
Eulagad lived in the past, remembering the times where he fought evil with his dead companions. He was also concerned about the fact his line would end with him. Eulagad had seen the growth of the Port of Shadow into a large city.

Relationship
Eulagad worked along with his former adventuring companion Ilyana Wyrmmruff.

Activities
Around 1370 DR, Eulagad managed the Burning Troll inn with Ilyana.

History
Eulagad was a famous adventurer with the Trollsinge Vanguard party and one of the few surviving members. With his companions, he explored deep into Undermountain, but they became most famous for their destruction of a marauding troll tribe. Eulagad retired after the death of his friends and for a long time managed an inn in Skullport.

## Eusebius

Eusebius was a storm giant from the storm giant aerie of Lake Woe mountain.This fact is deduced because one of Eusebius' companions was Ramos, and Ramos was the former paramount of the storm giant aerie described in Giantcraft.

Description
Eusebius had pale violet skin and blue-black hair that was very long and unkempt. He was clean-shaven. Eusebius typically wore a simple tunic with a large amount of silver jewelry. His eyes were silver-colored.

Possessions
Eusebius bore a sword in battle.

History
By 1369 DR, the then-paramount of the storm giants of Mount Woe, Anastes, and five other storm giants were summoned magically by Lanaxis to defend him when he needed to rest during his kidnapping of Queen Brianna and her son Kaedlaw. Eusebius was one of those giants. Eusebius was chosen to search the woods for a beehive to gather honey for Brianna to eat, but during this time, Tavis Burdun and armies of giant-kin attacked. Eusebius was then ordered by Anastes to deal with the fomorians. However, the fomorians quickly abandoned the field to pursue Lanaxis, so Eusebius instead assisted Anastes by flanking the firbolg army. Eusebius killed many firbolgs with his lightning and his massive sword, but he was eventually felled by fire magic from the firbolg shaman Munairoe and an arrow from Tavis that had been enchanted by the verbeeg Basil. Eusebius managed to kill Munairoe before he finally died.

## Euphoric imp

Euphoric imps were a specialized variety of imp that served the Nine Hells by bringing corrupt delight to the plane otherwise infamous for torment.

Description
Euphoric imps were  tall humanoids and weighed only . They were terribly emaciated, with imprints of thin bones jutting out from their ribcages and bodies covered in numerous partially healed puncture marks. They had deep circles around their bloodshot eyes, feeble stingers, and an uneven, stumbling gait.

Personality
Euphoric imps were highly intelligent, but shifty, feckless, and lacking in wisdom. They had a taste for their own supply, as evidenced by their puncture wounds.

Abilities
Euphoric imps, like other imps, healed quickly and could see through darkness both natural and magical. They had a few innate spell-like abilities, allowing them to replicate the spells detect good, detect magic, and invisibility (on itself only) at will, and the spell major image once each day. Their unique quality was the hallucinogenic venom they dispensed from their stingers, a potent slime that left the subject in a dazed, euphoric bliss, unable to do much more than basically defend themselves.

Combat
Euphoric imps enjoyed combat, but were unreliable thanks to their fondness for their own venom. They preferred to sneak up on spellcasters while invisible and sting them.

Society
Euphoric imps were skilled alchemists, and acted as both private and wandering dealers to influential beings across the planes of evil. They were solitary creatures found throughout the Nine Hells, and could also be summoned by the spell summon monster IV. Euphoric imps spoke the Celestial, Common, and Infernal languages.

## Euparkeria

Euparkeria, also known as runner among the denizens of the Malatran Plateau, was a species of dinosaur.
Description
Euparkerias were small dinosaurs with four legs and a tail.
Combat
Euparkerias hunted in packs. They usually targeted small prey and humanoids. They have been known to stalk a wounded creature much larger than they were. As a defensive measure, they had the ability to run upright on their hind legs for a short distance.
Biology
Euparkerias were carnivores and lived together in small groups.

## Eustachia Churlgo

Eustachia Churlgo was one of many children of Millie Churlgo who produced the infamous Churlgo Cheese in the Eastern Farmlands of Daggerford circa 1372 DR.

Description
Eustachia Churlgo was a young and hard-working halfling woman with medium-length brown hair.

History
Eustachia was one of the best farm workers among the many Churlgo children. She worked efficiently and took her job seriously if not frantically. She often worked outside along with her disgruntled brother Porto Churlgo, tending to the farm's cows, arguably the most important task as they were essential to cheese-making. She often reacted with annoyance and snarky comments to her brother's constant complaints and desires to live large in a big city like Waterdeep.

Eustachia, along with other Churlgos moved their farm from the Dalelands to Daggerford following a nasty extortion business with the Iron Throne. In 1372 DR, the farm where they resided was still noticeably un-lived-in, but the cheese-making business was in full swing.

## Euphorbia

Euphorbia, also known as spurge, blasting root, or springwort, was a flowering desert plant that was valued by spellcasters.

Usages
Eating roots of the euphorbia plant was believed to improve a thief's manual dexterity, improving their chances of picking a lock.

Some used euphorbia plant in powder form as an aphrodisiac, increasing the imbiber's interest in the opposite sex.

Euphorbia bean oil and spring water were used to mix an elixir that was the material component required to cast the run spell.

## Eugenius

Eugenius was a Netherese priest and arcanist who worked with Karsus in the later years of Netheril.

History
Eugenius intervened when Karsus attempted to build a new set of reflecting mirrors around the edge of the Eileanar enclave, with the goal of reflecting back any stray unused magical energies produced by the mythallars in the Mythallar Housing. While Karsus expected it to create a standing wave as the magical energy was reflected back and then exponentially increase the power of the mythallars, Eugenius pointed out the high likelihood of the experiment simply destroying the enclave in an enormous explosion. Miraculously, Karsus heeded this advice and abandoned the project, sparing the enclave another potential Karsus-induced disaster.
