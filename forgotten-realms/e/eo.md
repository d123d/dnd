## Eodraco

The eodraco (or "dawn dragon", in a dead language) was a genus of prehistoric proto-dragons. Many scholars believed eodracos were the first proto-dragon genus.

Description
According to information taken from incomplete fossil evidence, eodracos were similar in form and appearance to present age wyverns, with an average length of 35 feet (10 meters) and a 50-foot (15 meter) wingspan, with no evidence suggesting eodracos had a stinger on its tail like wyverns.

Due to the size of its skull, it was also believed their brains were smaller than those of wyverns, making them less intelligent than those creatures, but bigger than those of dinosaurs, making eodracos more cunning than their contemporaneous species.

History
It was widely believed by scholars from Candlekeep that eodracos were a divergent genus evolved from prehistoric dinosaurs, although they were never numerous enough to outnumber them. Due to their greater animal cunning, they developed the ability to anticipate and plan, and to cooperate with other members of their kind, making them a successful genus in a time dominated by powerful carnivorous dinosaurs.

It was theorized that their cunning and relatively small height enabled eodracos to survive the cataclysmic events that caused the mass extinction of nearly all species of dinosaurs. Most scholars believed this cataclysm was the Tearfall (although many counter-theories existed), and due to the harsh climatic change Toril suffered in its wake, eodracos were forced to undergo a process of rapid evolution in order to survive, eventually evolving into a huge variety of proto-dragon sub-species.

## Eodraco orientalis

The Eodraco orientalis was a supposed proto-dragon species of the Eodraco genus that eventually evolved into the lung dragons.

History
There was no conclusive evidence of the existence of E. orientalis, and many scholars believed the species was made up by apologists of the proto-dragon theory in an attempt to solve its inconsistencies, specifically the lack of an ancestor species for the lung dragons.

## Eomane

The Eomanes of Baldur's Gate were one of the rich patriar families that composed the city's wealthy noble-class. They earned their riches from the first-class perfumery they owned, along with manufactories that produced oil from fish and whales.

Their estate, the Eomane House, was located in the Manorborn district of the Upper City.

## Eo'tzi Gompa

Eo'tzi Gompa was a monastery (a gompa in the Tabotan language) of the Way of Enlightenment in Tabot.

History
When yeti emerged from the Wu Pi Te Shar mountains and raided Nafeen Gompa in, the dzong-pon (sheriff) of Thok district organized a hunting expedition against them, distributing printed handbills around southwestern Tabot. ## Eogast

Eogast was the chief of the drovers leading the animals in adventuress Zaranda Star's merchant caravan as she traveled from Morninggold Keep to Zazesspur in Mirtul of 1366 DR.

Description
Like many dwarves, Eogast was bearded. He was at least 200 years old when he served Zaranda Star.

Personality
When angered, Eogast had been known to stomp his feet, pull out the hair in his beard, and curse vehemently. When not throwing a tantrum, Eogast was otherwise quite patient, especially with his animals.

History
When the party reached a farm that had been raided by bandits, Zaranda ordered her drover-in-chief to pull the animals off the road to protect them and the merchandise, while she and the other adventurers approached the burning farmhouse. She similarly ordered him off the road when the party encountered the orog Shield of Innocence.

When the party arrived at the walls of Zazesspur, Eogast was angered that Zaranda paused the caravan before entering the city through one of the breaches in the wall, but she convinced him that they would have to pay less in bribes that way, and this satisfied him.

## Eommar

Eommar was an inhabitant of Port Llast and loyal customer of the Alliance Arms Inn in the late 14 century DR.

Description
Eommar was an aging human man with white hair and beard.

History
Circa the, Eommar could often be found in the Alliance Arms Inn, drunkenly leering at women. He joyously greeted strangers and asked them to buy him a drink because "the end was coming." He was referring to a recent development. As of 1374 DR, the streets of Port Llast were frequented by walking undead every night, which Eommar saw as an ill omen. To an especially friendly drinking buddy, someone who would spend coin on the old drunk, Eommar was willing to reveal that he heard one of the wandering undead creatures speak. The undead chanted "Last Port," Port Llast's name from ten generations past. Eommar believed that it was the curse of Last Port's last noble line, the Adreum Curse.

## Eostre

Fist Eostre was a mage member of the Flaming Fist mercenary company from the Lower City of Baldur's Gate in the late 15 century DR.

Description
Eostre was a human woman with long curly dark hair, black eyes, and a pale complexion. She wore a wizardly robes uniform of the Flaming Fist and a circlet, and carried a simple quarterstaff.

In the, Fist Eostre was stationed in the Basilisk Gate's barracks and prison, serving under Blaze Liara Portyr and Manip Seyama. She was tasked with cataloguing requisitioned produce, which was always, to her frustration, "organized by a rabid worg."

## Eolynn Arva

Eolynn Arva was a human healer of Eveningstar in Cormyr during the mid-1480s DR.

Description
Eolynn had pale skin and black hair, and wore a long gray dress with a low-cut bodice.

History

Around the time of the Sschindylryn invasion of Eveningstar, the ranger Peron Uthe returned from Thorngap carrying an unnatural disease. After he went to the Eveningstar War Hospital, Eolynn heard that something strange was happening at his home and on investigation noticed that Uthe Lodge had become overgrown with unnatural vegetation, and brought attention to the issue with Anazar Narroun. Thanks to her, the murderous dryad Sylvie was stopped before she could threaten the town.

## Eormennoth

Eormennoth was a bronze dragon who guarded the treasury of Ravens Bluff in the late 14 century DR.

Description
Eormennoth was a good-looking dragon. His name translated to 'immense treasure' in Draconic.

Personality
Eormennoth was refined, meticulous, and patient. He had a sense of humor. He was often curious about the world outside his vaults and enjoyed gossip. He had a particular affinity for the ocean.

While he did not particularly enjoy living so close to so many humanoids, he appreciated that his lair was heavily guarded, and liked the coastal position of Ravens Bluff.

Eormennoth was prepared to be a good mate, but it was unlikely that he could remain on good terms with a female bronze dragon for a long period of time. Following this, he was prepared to be a generous father.

Abilities
As a bronze dragon, Eormennoth could breathe water. He was also able to control powerful magics. He had access to ESP, polymorph self, weather summoning, create food and water, airy water, charm person, ventriloquism, invisibility, magic mouth, sepia snake sigil, and command.

Possessions
Eormennoth owned a significant pile of coins exceeding 180,000 gp by 1370 DR. He had gradually taken these coins from the treasury of Ravens Bluff as his payment for his position as guard.

Activities
Eormennoth spent nearly all of his time inside the vaults of Ravens Bluff, guarding them. He only allowed funds to be withdrawn if they were approved by a writ that had been certified. While he watched the gold, he quietly collected his payment of 10 gp from it each day.

To enjoy the outside world, he sometimes spent time at the Ravens Bluff docks, polymorphed as a cat, humanoid, or any other animal. After many years of service to the city, he was also comfortable taking brief aerial or aquatic jaunts as himself. He only attacked pirates in his territory when they committed crimes. He would often demand a payment from any pirate ships that he caught, in gems, pearls, or even shark meat.

By 1370 DR, he was actively searching for a female bronze dragon to be his mate, so that he could produce offspring.

Relationships
Eormennoth was friends with the tomboyish and adventurous Imramarthree Emmerdin, the younger daughter of Eskevelt Emmerdin.

Eormennoth hated pirates. A number of pirate crews that had once been defeated by Eormennoth sought vengeance against him. Kothonos, a guildmaster in Ravens Bluff, had once been one of these pirates, but Eormennoth had faith that he was reformed.

Although he was unaware of it, Eormennoth threatened to bankrupt the city of Ravens Bluff with the portion of the treasury that he had collected for himself. City officials like Charles Oliver O'Kane and Vernon Condor had made various plans to take the wealth back from him.

History
In his younger years, Eormennoth came to lair in Ravens Bluff and attacked pirates along the coast. His first proper battle against pirates was a great success. He destroyed the ship's mast and defeated the pirates, some of whom were under the impression that Eormennoth was some kind of illusion. An informal agreement was established by the Lord Treasurer of Ravens Bluff that Eormennoth would be paid 10 gold pieces for each day of his service, upon the dragon's retirement.

Soon after Charles Oliver O'Kane became Lord Mayor of Ravens Bluff, he planned to manipulate Eormennoth into defeating pirates for him. He spread false rumors of a wealthy cargo in the city harbor, which tricked pirate crews into attacking. Eormennoth defeated the pirates, and O'Kane won his favor by sharing the loot with him. By this time, the terms of Eormennoth's original agreement were long-forgotten. O'Kane and his Regent of the Exchequer, Vernon Condor, were both surprised to learn that the dragon was in the city's employ. Condor held a discussion with Eormennoth over a period of three days, and gifted him with pearls to eat. Condor learned about the arrangement, and became concerned about the amount of money that Eormennoth had collected.

Although some elves had a longer view of the situation, Condor convinced O'Kane to change the terms of the original agreement. They planned to charge Eormennoth a special "dragon tax" and confiscate half of his wealth, even preparing a cube of force and a magic longsword especially powerful against bronze dragons should the negotiations turn sour. However, O'Kane was replaced by Amber Lynn Thoden, and Eormennoth remained in the employ of the city. By 1370 DR, he had protected the vault for over 50 years, and was actively seeking a mate.

Rumors & Legends
A number of years prior to 1370 DR, it was falsely claimed that Eormennoth had died.

## Eon, the Poisoned Reign

Eon, the Poisoned Reign was a powerful longbow that was tied to both dragons and the Feywild.

Powers
Eon was linked to a green dracolich, and was suitably powerful. It shot arrows rapidly and with devastating force, and each arrow was imbued with deadly poisons.

History
The bow was tied to a dracolich who was promised a long and fruitful reign of terror that never happened. The bow came to interact with the Feywild at some point and was lost in time as well, before eventually reappearing in the hands of mortals.

It was tied in some fashion to Aeon, the Blazing Reign, a similar weapon bound to the dracolich Karliath in Myth Drannor.
