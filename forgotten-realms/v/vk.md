## Vkriss Valtan Leatherwings

Vkriss Valtan Leatherwings was a dragonborn of Tymanther and a member of Clan Vkriss in 1479 DR.

Personality
Valtan was a cunning and cutthroat businessman.

History
At some point, Valtan lost his wife in a tragic "accident". Two days later, nearly all the prominent members of Clan Gethmara were killed in a gruesome massacre.

In 1479 DR, Valtan was the guildmaster of Djerad Thymar's weaver's guild.

## V'kandress

V'kandress was a mysterious dragonborn follower of Tiamat and a member of Clan Vkriss in 1479 DR.

History
Little was known about V'kandress. Many believed he or she was either a priest of Tiamat or an infernal pact warlock, or an assassin, or all the three things at the same time. V'kandress was also rumored to be the leader of the Knights of the Five-Thorned Rose in the late years of the 15th century DR.
