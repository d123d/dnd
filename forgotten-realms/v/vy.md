## Vyma Gheldieg

Vyma Gheldieg was a noble of House Gheldieg of Amn in the mid–14 century DR. She was the daughter of Namarch Curkon Gheldieg and the wife of Dahaunarch Pehllus Tanislove. She was fun-loving and known informally as the "party-girl priestess".

## Vyaalis

Vyaalis was from the Coral Kingdom, a sea elven kingdom in the Trackless Sea near Evermeet.

History
For a time, he lived in Memnon, learning the art of longsword fighting from Shieldlord Hastor el Khafar.

Something of a wanderer, Vyaalis's curiosity led him to explore the surface world in Calimshan and Tethyr. His interest in the longsword stemmed from his discovery of one among the wreckage of the Pasha's Palace, a sunken Calishite vessel.

## Vyqinak

Vyqinak was an ixitxachitl vampireA "vampiric ixitxachitl" was not the same as an ixitxachitl vampire. While both had the energy drain ability and some other shared traits, the former was not undead. See Monster Manual II, page 129. known as the Count of the Depths in the court of the Night King in Westgate as of the Year of the Tankard, 1370 DR.

Activities
As Count of the Depths, Vyqinak was the junior Night Master in charge of all smuggling in the Westgate harbor. He was also responsible for reports on shipping activity on the Dragon Coast. He was tasked by the Night King to seek out the wreck of the Sapphire of Enki, an Untheric sailing vessel believed to have been carrying a powerful magic tome titled Songs of Sea Serpents, written by Nezram "Worldwalker". Vyqinak concentrated his search on the area between Westgate and Sembia, a sea bed covered in thick silt known as the Haunted Plains.

Relationships
Vyqinak answered to the Duke of Shadows, Tebryn Dhialael, whose portfolio included all smuggling and thieving activity perpetrated by the Night Masks. In turn, Vyqinak's servants included a pack of lesser seawolves that worked on the docks and as hands on ships that regularly returned to Westgate.

Vyqinak recruited Hrarnok Sharkchum as an assassin after discovering he was a seawolf whose shapechanging ability made an excellent cover.

History
Vyqinak was originally a mid-level priest (known as a vitan) of Ilxendren, living in the Xedran Reefs, until the start of the Twelfth Serôs War in the Year of the Gauntlet, 1369 DR. He fled the conflict in shame, abandoning his people and his faith to take up the worship of Demogorgon, an ancient deity of ixitxachitls that predated Ilxendren. He was drawn to the harbor of Westgate in 1370 DR where he encountered Orlak II (a.k.a. Orbakh, a Manshoon clone turned undead), the Night King, who turned him into a vampire (which was a very high status in ixitxachitl culture) and installed him as a Count in the Court.

## Vyzzstek Nhynssoth

Vyzzstek Nhynssoth was the Arcane Brotherhood representative in Skullport.

History
Around 1370 DR, Vyzzstek posed as a loyal lieutenant of Zstulkk Ssarmn but in truth was the eyes and ears of the Arcane Brotherhood under his master, the South Overwizard Deltagar Zelhund. ## Vyerith

Vyerith was a doppelganger that worked for Nezznar, the Black Spider.

Description
The doppelganger appeared as a female dark elf.

History
Circa 1491 DR, Nezznar sent Vyerith as a messenger to convene with King Grol of the Cragmaw tribe. The goblinoid king had recently captured Gundren Rockseeker, an ambitious dwarf who believed he had found the location of the Lost Mine of Phandelver.

Vyerith arrived at Cragmaw Castle to request the dwarf and his map to the lost mine. However, Grol did not wish to hand them over, without being given anything in exchange. The doppelganger wanted to first speak with Gundren to find out if anybody else knew of the location of the lost mine, and then kill him. Vyerith was in no way loyal to Grol, and planned to escape if he was slain. However, if cornered, the doppelganger would fight to the death, preferring that fate over being captured.

## Vyldara

Vyldara was a banshee bound to the ruins of Axeholm as of the late 15 century DR.

Description
As a banshee, Vyldara appeared as a floating, spectral female elf clothed in tattered rags and with thin, wispy hair tangled around her face.

Personality
When she was alive, Vyldara was manipulative and violent. In death, she was delusional and extremely quick to anger. Any attempt to speak with or reason with her was met with the full force of her terrifying power.

History
In life, Vyldara was a moon elf ambassador from her people to the dwarves of Axeholm. For unknown motives, she began trying to foment civil unrest amongst the dwarves, but her plan failed and she was imprisoned. The dwarves sent missives to the elves to demand that they come and personally remove her from their home, but as they waited for a reply, Vyldara attempted to escape. She killed two guards, but then was herself slain. Unfortunately for the dwarves, she then rose from the dead as a banshee. As her wails echoed through the halls, those who succumbed to her power rose as ghouls to feast on their kin. Axeholm was swiftly abandoned, and the entrance was sealed.

Many years later, Axeholm had fallen into disrepair, and Vyldara came to see the ruin, and specifically the once magnificent guest room in which she had stayed as ambassador, as her "house."

## Vynnia

Vynnia was a young dark one who was a member of the Glumguts family of dark creepers in the Shadowfell city of Evernight as of the late 15 century DR.

Relationships
Vynnia was a cousin of Blackclaw, the terrifying and merciless leader of the Glumguts.

Vynnia, another cousin named Thrax, and Blackclaw's son, Blackcut, we’re the only members of the Glumguts who dared to cross Blackclaw. They played pranks across Evernight, embarked on sly ventures, sought excitement by harassing people who did not seem to be native to the Shadowfell, and merrily committed acts that embarrassed and hindered the aspirations of the Glumguts' employers, Netheril. This in turn meant that if the the three youngsters' involvement in these events were discovered, it could ruin the whole family. Before that, they ran the risk of invoking Blackclaw's wrath, although they believed they would be fine because Blackcut was his son.

## Vylxildross

Vylxildross the Malevolent was a powerful red dragon allied with the Cult of the Dragon and the Red Wizards of Thay in the late 15 century DR.

History
In the late 15 century DR, Vylxildross the Malevolent was present at the Dragonbone Vale, where the Cult of the Dragon, with the help from the Red Wizards of Thay, planned on making the prophecy of the Chronicle of Years to Come come true. When Valindra Shadowmantle's Rite of the Dracolich was interrupted, Vylxildross faced adventurers and heroes of Neverwinter on the Scaleblight Summit.
