## Fharaud

Fharaud was a wealthy shipbuilder who lived in Innarlith in the mid–14 century DR.

History
In 1358 DR, Fharaud traveled to Marsember in Cormyr, along with Senator Inthelph, in order to attend a recruiting event held at a college for studying engineering. He was looking to hire a graduate from the college to help him with a project to build a massive ship for the King of Cormyr that was going to be named the Everwind. He met Ivar Devorast at the event and was impressed with his designs. He recruited Devorast to help him with his project and brought him back to Innarlith.

Devorast helped Fharaud complete the ship. The ship was supposed to be transported through a magic portal from the Lake of Steam to the Vilhon Reach, where representatives from Cormyr's navy would take it. However, Devorast distrusted the idea of sending the ship through a portal.

In 1360 DR, the ship was completed and it was launched. Fharaud and a skeleton crew took control of the ship while Devorast remained on shore. When the ship went through the portal, the portal caused the ship to break into pieces and fall from a height above the area where it was supposed to go. Fharaud survived the accident, but he was badly injured and became bedridden. Devorast visited him frequently in the years following the accident.

He died from his injuries in 1364 DR. Shortly before he passed away, however, he wrote a letter to Ransar Osorkon recommending Devorast to him as well as Devorast's idea of building a canal between the Lake of Steam and the Nagaflow.

Relationships
Ransar Osorkon respected Fharaud and was still impressed with Fharaud's work in building ships, even when he was considered disgraced after the accident with the Everwind.

Ivar Devorast worked for Fharaud and had some personal loyalty to him. After Fharaud's accident with the Everwind, Devorast went to visit him frequently while he was bedridden and helped take care of him.

Abilities
At the end of his life after his accident, he began having visions of the future. He explained that this had something to do with what happened to me while he travelled through the portal that was supposed to transport the Everwind.

Rumors & Legends
It was widely rumored that the reason for the accident with the Everwind was because the ship was too large for the portal.

"I’m trying to make sure that the Shou woman will tell him all about the girl with the spirits inside her" - 1364 DR, likely referring to Ran Ai Yu who encountered Phyrea sneaking aboard her ship and saw her talking to people who weren't there, she later reported the event to Ransar Osorkon in 1365 DR

"He’ll be watching you when you meet with the dwarf and the alchemist" - 1364 DR spoken by Fharaud to Ivar Devorast, likely referring to Marek Rymut magically spying on Devorast when he and Hrothgar Deepcarver met with the alchemist Surero regarding smokepowder for use in the canal construction in 1366 DR

"One breath, then another, and another, then the rest of them, and that’s it" - 1364 DR

Fharaud tried to explain his power of prophecy on his deathbed to Ivar Devorast like this, although Devorast didn't believe these things were real:

"I’m putting things in the wrong order. But listen to me. Listen to me when I tell you that when I went through that gate, and when I fell… when Everwind fell and my body was shattered my mind was splayed open and the future poured in. It overwhelms me, but I can see it. I can feel and taste and hear it. I have the future living inside me, but it’s a future that doesn’t include me."

## Fhamin Dread

Fhamin Dread was a would-be warlord human mage, active in the 14 century DR.

History
By his seventieth year, the man only known by the alias of Fhamin Dread was already a notoriously obsessive and power-hungry wizard. He pursued his warmongering and aggressive aspirations to conquer through a personal army and magical power. His target was not just a town or a nation but the entire world. Fhamin accumulated experience by slinging spells in battles against monsters, warriors, and fellow mages. Every coin he earned in his travels through adventuring and mercenary work was sent to finance the future army. However, the young man's egomaniacal tendencies made it challenging to find any loyal followers.

Sometime in the 14 century DR, Fhamin Dread was among the ambitious fortune seekers who were interested in being sent into a mysterious dungeon by an unnamed sorceress to retrieve the equally mysterious magical orb from its depths.

## Fhaoralusyolkiir

Fhaoralusyolkiir  was an Aryselmalyn garrison city, a primary garrison for the empire's capital city Coryselmal, that stood for centuries until eventually being abandoned and left to ruins in -80 DR. In the late 6 century DR, members of the Church of Deep Sashelas moved into what remained of Fhaoralusyolkiir, repurposing it into a temple to Deep Sashelas.

Geography
This city was nestled along and built into the subsea cliffs of Turmish, between Alaghôn and the Aprhunn Mountain, near the mouth of the Vilhon Reach. Its city gates and grandiose guardposts, with gleaming tridents made of blaze coral, were set into the edge of the underwater valley of the Vilhon Reach.

Inhabitants
By 1371 DR, about two hundred sea elves lived in Fhaoralusyolkiir, though most were tied to its temple.

History
Fhaoralusyolkiir was founded in the year . It would continue to stand following Aryselmalyn Empire's demise in, not becoming entirely abandoned and left to ruin until . As the years passed, much of the city built into the cliffside would be buried by collapsing caverns.

In the, the Church of Deep Sashelas took up residence in the palace once lived in by the garrison city's commanders, turning it into a temple to their god. This state of affairs would continue well into the 14 century DR, with the city still retaining some vestiges of its grandeur.
