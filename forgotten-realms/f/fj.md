## Fjord

Fjord was one of the twelve moons of Garden in Realmspace.

Description
Like most of Garden's moons, Fjord was larger than its parent, but there was confusion about its size and shape. Astronomical records indicated that it was spherical, yet its dimensions were listed as  by, indicating rather an ellipsoid. Its rocky surface had no atmosphere.

History
Fjord was once a prosperous mining colony, but it had been abandoned by the later 14 century DR.

## Fjellafar

Fjellafar was a frost giant skald from Svardborg who lived centuries prior to the 14 century DR.

History
Fjellafar wrote sagas that continued to be sung far and wide by giants circa 1356 DR. These chronicled centuries of victories and the challenges of living in the Sea of Moving Ice, such as what the area's frost giants hunted for among the ice during summer and winter.
