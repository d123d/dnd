## Fzoul Chembryl

Fzoul Chembryl  was the Faerûnian god of service to evil as well as an exarch of Bane. Formerly the mortal Chosen tyrant of his dark overlord, Fzoul was killed while fighting the Shadovar and was granted the status of an exarch as his reward for dying in Bane's service.

History
1263 DR Took authority over the Dark Shrine of Bane in Zhentil Keep.
1368 DR Read aloud the True Life of Cyric, which directly caused the fall of Zhentil Keep.
1370 DR Slew Manshoon and purged the Zhentarim of Manshoon's Clones, gaining leadership over most of the Black Network. Summoned the Tyrantfog.
1370 DR Forged the Scepter of the Tyrant's Eye.
1372 DRFzoul struck an alliance between his Zhentarim forces and baatezu Gorgomorz's forces of Dragonspear Castle to bring down Daggerford. The plot was eventually stopped by a troop of adventurers.
1374 DR Allied with House Jaelre and Auzkovyn Clan to prevent The Return in Myth Drannor.
Ordered Scyllua Darkhope to seize Moonsea Ride.
Ordered Scyllua Darkhope to invade Shadowdale with the aid of the Church of Shar and the drow of House Dhuurniv.
1375 DR Blamed all of the Black Networks' failures in the Cormanthor War on Scyllua Darkhope to save face with his drow allies.
1376 DR With his drow allies broken, Fzoul offered peace terms to the elves of Cormanthor, who accept. Ilsevele Miritar gave Hillsfar and the lands directly to the north to the Zhentarim, as well as free passage along the Moonsea Ride and Rauthauvyr's Road so long as they do not fell a single living tree, injure or kill any elf, or leave the road for more than 30 paces.
Between 1376 DR and 1479 DR Killed while defending Zhentil Keep against Netherese attack.
Bane raised Fzoul Chembryl to be his exarch.

Fzoul's tower was located in the western section of Zhentil Keep's Inner Quarter.

## Fzoul's Tower

Fzoul's Tower was the home of Fzoul Chembryl of the Church of Bane and the Zhentarim.

Location
The tower was located in Zhentil Keep's Inner Quarter right next to the Black Altar near the heart of the city.

Description
The narrow tower stood five storys tall. It was constructed of stone with a square shape, though a wooden stable and annex were found at the base which marred the structure's symmetry.

Interior
The interior of Fzoul's Tower held a feast hall, laboratory, office, and main bedchamber. Many hidden passages were found beneath the tower, connecting it to strategic locations in Zhentil Keep. The most useful of these connected to the Black Altar and was accessed by a magically silenced trapdoor.

A private altar of Bane was located beneath the Tower.

Defenses
A trio of wyverns guarded the roof of the tower. The creatures followed simple commands given by anyone wearing the robes of a priest of Bane. An eye tyrant lived in the subterranean passages.

History
The tower was traditionally used as the personal home for the head of Bane's church in Zhentil Keep, who, as of the mid-to-late 14 century DR, was Fzoul Chembryl.
