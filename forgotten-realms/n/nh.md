## Nhyris D'Hothek

Nhyris D'Hothek, later known as the Murkstalker, was a yuan-ti slaver who possessed the Crown of Horns prior to the Spellplague.

History
Nhyris was a slaver living in Skullport, working under his uncle Zstulkk Ssarmn of the Iron Ring. At one point he came in possession of the relic the Crown of Horns. During the Spellplague, the crown warped Nyryis' body and mind into something unrecognizable, which came to be called the "Murkstalker". As of 1479 DR, he lurked in the lowest part of Skullport, where he preyed on the weak.

## Nhar-del

Nhar-del the Black was a necromancer agent of the Zhentarim in the Dalelands.

Description
A pale man dressed in black, Nhar-del had blue eyes, a bald head, and a full brown goatee.

Personality
Nhar-del took great pleasure in slow and painful murder.

Possessions
He wore a cursed ring of affliction (attuned to necromancy), and carried a wand of fear.

History
Nhar-del started out as a street urchin, but eventually became a necromancer in high regard among the Zhentarim. He was tricked into putting on a ring of affliction by a neglected lover.

## Nhalloth

Nhalloth was a floating Netherese enclave above the Sea of Fallen Stars in Faerûn. As a direct result of Karsus's Folly in, Nhalloth fell into the sea and was destroyed. Rediscovered in 1305 DR, it became the subject of many expeditions and widespread attempts at looting. Following the Spellplague, Nhalloth rose to the surface once more as sea levels dropped sharply.

Geography
The ruins of Nhalloth were located in the Mountains of Volar in the Sea of Fallen Stars, roughly 19 miles northwest of Thonn's Fist in the Archipelago of Altumbel.

Description

Nhalloth was notable for its breathtaking architecture, a shining example of the Netherese Age of Discovery style, with towers reaching over 60 feet into the air. Even centuries after the enclave's fall into the sea, most of the buildings remained intact, albeit barnacle-covered and corroded. The shattered base rock of Nhalloth gave birth to a tall reef of claw coral, which could also be found in large quantities scattered around the ruins.

The enclave's buildings remained structurally sound even after it resurfaced, with tales of Nhalloth's rich architecture drawing many adventurers to search through its still-submerged lower sections.

History
Nhalloth was established in, and fell into the sea in −339 DR after Karsus briefly caused all magic to cease functioning.

As the centuries passed, several oceanic races made the ruins of Nhalloth their home, although never for long. Most races preferred to avoid the site altogether, citing an "odd feeling" and reports of ghosts. The last oceanic inhabitants of Nhalloth were a tribe of scrags, who were eventually killed by merrow tribes.

When the submerged enclave was rediscovered by surface-dwellers in 1305 DR, they found it mostly empty of lost lore and magical artifacts. It was suspected that over the centuries, inhabitants of the nearby morkoth country of Olleth had plundered the city of its greatest secrets. Many adventurers and looters visited the underwater ruins, and the Simbul sponsored several expeditions to the area. However, little was recovered save an increased understanding and appreciation of Netherese architecture.

When the Spellplague ravaged Toril in 1385 DR, the sea level of the Sea of Fallen Stars dropped sharply as it was partially drained into the Underdark. As a result of this, Nhalloth found itself on the surface once more, with only the lower levels still partially underwater. Although the enclave was by all accounts stripped of all valuables, agents of the Shadovar were observed digging through Nhalloth's remains.

## Nhalbert Taluth

Nhalbert Taluth was the ex-mercenary and ex-soldier from Cormyr who retired and opened a store called Taluth's Shrewd Flight in Mosstone, Tethyr, in the 14 century DR.

Possessions
Taluth's Shrewd Flight was a small store that sold masterfully crafted bows, bowstrings, quivers, and arrows. The goods were expensive but reasonably priced for the quality.

History
Nhalbert Taluth was an expert sharpshooter. He learned his skills with the bow during his days commanding archers of Cormyr. When he left the Dragon Throne's army, Nhalbert took on a mercenary trade as a marksman-for-hire. His work earned him a foul reputation as his arrows pierced over a dozen nobles of the Vilhon Reach. Once he was no longer welcomed in the Vilhon, Nhalbert joined a trading caravan who moved west. Bandits ambushed the caravan, and in the struggle, Nhalbert tumbled under a speeding wagon, losing a leg in the process. He survived that brigand ambush by the skin of his teeth. The injured archer showered the bandits with all of his arrows, keeping them at bay long enough for the surviving caravaners to rescue him.

With his new peg leg, Taluth was unable to effectively lay in ambush and practice his marksmanship as a bow-for-hire, unable to go to war as a soldier. Instead, he retired to a quaint wooden town of Mosstone along the Trade Way. He used his archery and fletching skills to make a login selling masterfully crafted goods to local rangers and traveling caravans.

Rumors & Legends
Local Mosstonians believed that Nhalbert Taluth knew elven magic that helped him grow good quality straight saplings in the grove where he gathered wood for his trade, to the east of the city walls.

## N'halien

N'halien was an early knight of Neverwinter and the seventh of the Nine knights who served Lord Halueth Never. They were the basis for the Neverwinter Nine.

History
In a battle against the armies of Illusk, N'halien saved Halueth from an arrow, but caught it in his chest and died instead. A statue of N'halien and his sword later stood guard in Halueth's tomb.
