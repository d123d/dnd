## Njal

Njal was a dwarf and part of a valiant group who defended Dead Dwarf Bridge against forces from Narfell who hoped to invade Almorel.

History
A wizard from Narfell cast a spell upon Njal, forcing him to attack his fellow dwarves. The ensuing fight resulted in Molnir and Njal tumbling to their deaths into the Clearflow River river far below.

Activities
Njal would appear as a haunt on the bridge, but only when either Theodo Greataxe or Molnir were also present. Still under the influence of the wizard's spell, even in undeath, Njal would try to possess any living creature in order to attack Theodo or Molnir.

## N'Jast

N'Jast was a village in Narfell that sat at the crossroads of the Long Road and the Cold Road.

Geography
N'Jast lay in central Narfell. It was east of Jiyyd and south of the Teardrops.

Trade
The village was predominantly a trade outpost. Merchants on the Long Road came from Damara in the west and Rashemen in the east, while merchants on the Cold Road came from the settlements and dwarfholds of the Icerim Mountains to the north and Thesk to the south.

It was an important stopover for both merchants and Nars heading to the Bildoobaris trademeet.

History
As of the mid-to-late 14 century DR, N'Jast was little more than a waymeet, but it had become a permanent settlement by the late 15 century DR.
