## Nrymm Aumar

Nrymm Aumar was the youngest of the Warring Princes of Athalantar.

History
Nrymm was very young when his father, and king, died and he was raised by the women of the court.

When magelords seized control, Nrymm disappeared. Magelord records indicated that he was turned into an otyugh by the magelords, then kept in the middens of Athalgard, the royal castle in Hastarl, so that they had another blood heir of Uthgrael in their power, other than Belaur Aumar.

Nrymm was slain in otyugh form in 238 DR by one of the mages hired by Felodar Aumar.

## Nrakk

Nrakk was a githzerai monk active in the late 15 century DR.

Description
He was greenish-yellow of complexion and was entirely bald save for a long braid of dark hair, and long tufts of facial hair on each side of his jaw. Nrakk wore maroon and blue clothing, and a thick scarf around his neck to ward off the cold.

Personality
Nrakk struggled to interact with other people, particularly those he viewed as his lessers. He appeared reserved and aloof. He felt Toril was a chilly and unpleasant place.

Abilities
As a kensei monk, Nrakk was exceptionally skilled with his naginata.

Possessions
Nrakk was outfitted in several magical items. His magical naginata was enchanted to be harmful to mind flayers, and he carried an alchemy jug with him at all times. An ioun stone orbited his head.

History
Nrakk was dispatched by the Great Githzerai, Zaerith Menyar-Ag-Gith, to aid an adventuring party on the Prime Material plane. Though his motivations seemed unclear, he always stayed true to the party's cause.

In the late 15 century DR, sometime after the, Nrakk's visit to Sigil entangled him in a wild spelljamming adventure alongside Lae'zel of Creche K'liir. Lae'zel was summoned to Sigil by her ally, Ezmerath, but found herself accused of disrupting an official investigation commenced by a Harmonium Peacekeeper. With Nrakk's and Ezmerath's help, Lae'zel avoided capture. Together, the unlikely group survived a deadly mind flayer threat in the depths of the Wildspace and across the Rock of Bral, and slew an elder brain, after resolving their ideological differences.

## Nrakka

Nrakka was a spell-slinging pterafolk who menaced Kir Sabal in Chult circa late 15 century DR.

Description
Nrakka had a dark-green leathery skin with lighter aquamarine speckles and striping on the creature's feet.

Abilities
Nrakka was a powerful individual and had knowledge of the arcane, often showering its opponents with fireballs from the sky.

History
In the late 15 century DR, Nrakka took a habit of attacking inhabitants of the Kir Sabal monastery in Chult inhabited by a flock of aarakocra and several your human children. The bird-folk sought help against the cunning and cruel foe among with adventurers who carried to Chult investigating the death curse.

## Nressa

Nressa the Weaver was one of the few inhabitants of Fallenfalcon, a thorp along the Bowshot road in the wild realm of Owlhold in the Border Kingdoms.

History
By the late 14 century DR, Nressa and her family lived in one of the two cottages closest to the main pavilion in Fallenfalcon. She and her kin provided travelers who stayed at the camping pavilion with fresh bread and soup, offering bigger meals upon request ahead of time.

## Nrog

Nrog was a goblin warrior cult of the Absolute and a denizen of the Shattered Sanctum during the late 15 century DR.

Description
He had yellow-green skin, decorated with blue body paint, and kept his head bald. He also bore the mark of the Absolute.

Possessions
Nrog wore goblin-made plate armor adorned with bones along with an open-faced helm. Nrog wielded a large battleaxe in combat.
