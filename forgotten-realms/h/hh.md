## H'hoort

H'hoort was a troglodyte in the Wormwrithings in the late 15 century DR.

Relationships
H'hoort was the mate of Chieftain S'slaar.

History
Circa 1486 DR, S'slaar had a disagreement with his war chief H'slaat about the ownership of a magic sword. Soon a civil war started between S'slaar's and H'slaat's loyalists. H'slaat ordered warriors to capture H'hoort, hoping to bargain her for the sword.

## Hhormun

Hhormun was an elder member of the War Wizards of Cormyr, that served the Forest Kingdom during the late 14 century DR.

Description
He was a rather portly man that could be quite energetic despite his old age.

Relationships
He was the superior War Wizard to Caladnei for a time, before her ascent as Mage Royal of Cormyr.

History
In the, the flying Netherese city of Thultanthar returned to the Realms, and quickly emerged as a threat to Cormyr and the rest of the Heartlands. In Mirtul of that year, Hhormun, Caladnei, and a band of Purple Dragons hid themselves amidst a tribe of Bedine nomads in order to uncover the location of the Shadovar city. During their journey, Hhormun gave just a bit too much leeway over planning to Sheikh Sa'ar of the Bait Mahwa, allowing the Bedine to capture resting veserabs owned by the Shadovar. This act of aggression drew the Shadovar into battle, forced them to call for reinforcements, and ultimately offered the Cormyreans the location of Thultanthar.

Rather than strike out at the flying city however, the Steel Regent Alusair Obarskyr staged the city of Tilverton for the inevitable battle between her people and the Shadovar army.
