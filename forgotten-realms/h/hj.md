## Hjurnur Wyrmrever

Hjurnur Wyrmrever was a notorious frost giant hero of the Thousand Year War.

History
During the Thousand Year War, Hjurnur killed many dragons single-handedly, though he could not match the Vonindod in terms of wyrms slain. During this time he was credited with killing an adult blue dragon with a single blow of his axe and breaking the spine of a black dragon over his knees.

His final battle in life during the war was against an ancient gold dragon general, though some would say it was a king, who had destroyed many a giant city. Hjurnur lured the dragon into a deep and narrow pit he had dug in order to limit its mobility as they fought. The dragon then called upon its twelve lieutenants, who the giant each slayed. But with no cracks for the blood to drain into, Hjurnur died drowning in the blood of the thirteen dragons.

In the centuries that followed he was celebrated in many poems and songs, with the oldest being the Saga of the Dragon Queller, written by his uncle Hrotun. Circa 1356 DR, such songs and poems were only popular among his fellow frost giants.

Rumors & Legends
One legend claimed that during the Thousand Year War he challenged Tiamat in a contest of riddles. He lost that contest, however, and had to allow the Queen of Dragons devour his own child.

## Hjerik Broadkin

Hjerik Broadkin was an inhabitant of Vanesci Hamlet in the Utter East during the time of the Bloodforge Wars (648–657 DR).

Hjerik was an upstart bloodforge mage. When the leader of the Legendary Campaign passed through the streets of Vanesci, Hjerik provoked them and publically challenged them to a bloodforge duel—it was common for such upstarts to challenge renowned bloodforge mages. Unable to ignore the challenge, they waged a bloodforge battle in and around Vanesci Hamlet.

## Hjurgen

Hjurgen was one of the second generation of frost giants on Toril.

Relationships
Hjurgen was a son of Ottar, the father of all frost giants. He had a brother named Hrotun, who was the first of the frost giant skalds. Hjurgen had a son, Hjurnur, who was a great hero.

History
Hjurgen was mentioned in the Saga of the Dragon Queller, an epic ballad about his son Hjurnur Wyrmrever and written by his brother. In that tale, Hjurgen is named as the "leveler of Ranauroch". (It was not clear to scholars where exactly Ranauroch was located, but many suspected that it was a region of Anauroch.)

## Hjollder

Hjollder was a Reghedmen human elder and shaman of the Tribe of the Bear in the late 13 century DR.

Description
Hjollder was a large man wearing deerskins.

Relationships
Hjollder was a son of Hrethric.

History
Guided by visions of the god Tempos, Hjollder was directed to Kuldahar, where he hoped to find a group of adventurers capable of saving his tribe from the plans of Wylfdene, a resurrected great warrior who had proclaimed himself a king of Reghedmen. It was said that this king was a mere vessel for the soul of Jerrod.

In the, Hjollder arrived at Kuldahar and settled in a local house. He aimed to take the heroes he had seen in his visions to Hengorot to stop a coming war between Reghedmen tribes and the Ten Towns. He accompanied the adventurers on their way to Lonelywood, where they had to split up.
