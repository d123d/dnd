## Hmur's Way

Hmur's Way was the sole major clear roadway in the underwater city of Myth Nantar.

Etymology
Hmur's Way was most likely named for Hmur, a mythical figure among the merfolk of Serôs that was said to have been the first of them created by Eadro and the namesake of the Hmur Plateau.

Location
This road was paved in blaze coral and depicted mosaics that over time broke down in appearance. Like the other major roadways of Myth Nantar, it measured  in width. It spanned west from the lowest slope of Mount Halaath to the Fire Fountain at the city's central square.

## Hm-boye

Hm-boye was a human warrior who lived in the city of Procampur in the Vast in the early 1360s DR.

History
Though his mother apparently came from somewhere in Kara-Tur, Hm-boye only ever remembered living in Procampur. He lived in a rough neighborhood in the harbor district, near the wharves. Though he was no criminal, the city guards often tried to arrest him on sight anyway.

Skills
Hm-boye was capable of acrobatic martial arts and more conventional weapon techniques.

Possessions
He owned a ring of jumping.

Appendix
Trivia
"Hm-boye" is presumably a pun on home-boy.

## Hmurrath

Hmurrath, also known as the Hmur Empire, the Hmur Imperium or the Second Kingdom of Hmurrath, was a merfolk empire in Serôs beneath the Sea of Fallen Stars that broke away from the Aryselmalyr Empire. It was succeeded by the kingdom of Eadraal.

Geography
The territory of Hmurrath encompassed the Hmur Plateau, with its most remote outposts being situated on the slopes above the Bymmal Trench.

Government
Each settlement of Hmurrath was centered around a clan, who were collectively governed by one king or queen, referred to by the title of "praessar". The praessar ruled from the empire's capital city of Baomal. For a long time the Hmurran's rulership over Serôs was compared favorably to the aquatic elves that ruled the Aryselmalyr Empire. However, over time the Hmurrath government similarly became subject to corruption and despotism, with its praessars causing destruction in the land comparable to the sahuagin.

Over the course of the empire's history there were forty-six praessars, with thirty-seven of them being male and nine of them female.

Culture
Hmurrath had an isolationist and patriarchal warrior culture. Much of its social structure was derived from the Aryselmalyr Empire, as it had overtaken the original social structures of the merfolk of the Inner Sea that were often kept down by their aquatic elf rulers. Hmurrath also adopted Aryselmalyr's advancements in weapon technology and its complex, organized architectural styles that used a combination of coral and carved stone. Though the empire distanced itself from the elven styles it adopted by adapting various hardier corals for their buildings in place of carved stone.

Following in the footsteps of Aryselmalyr, the empire of Hmurrath systematically oppressed the merrows that lived on the Hmur Plateau, keeping them in a barbaric state over fears of them potentially dominating Serôs. And Hmurrath's merfolk commonly carried a prejudical attitude towards all those who lacked a full fish tail like them.

Time was measured in the kingdom using a system known as the Calendar of Hmur, which continued to see use by some merfolk and many locathahs long after the empire's fall.

History
With the fall of the Aryselmalyr Empire, the Sixth Serôs War was kicked off and many of the races of Serôs that were once subjects to the aquatic elves began to vie for their own control of the undersea world. Following the war's end in, the empire of Hmurrath arose alongside the new empires of As'arem and the Theocracy of the Deep Ones. This signaled the start of the Third Epoch of the Inner Sea, also known by the epithet "Time of the Merfolk".

In the Seventh Serôs War (–) the empire allied with Es'rath, the oldest kingdom in the shalarin empire of As'arem, against the onslaught of the Theocracy of the Deep Ones.

Hmurrath later fought alongside As'arem as a whole in the Eighth Serôs War (–) against the ixitxachitl kingdom of the Paolan Reef, culminating in its destruction.

In, a merrow chieftain by the name of Tarag led an uprising against Hmurrath through a combination of his own battle strategies and the legendary sword Ilbratha. He conquered the empire's western frontiers, founding the Axe Kingdoms. Over time tensions gave rise to the Ninth Serôs War (–). In the second year of this conflict the Battle of the Haunted Plains took place, where the Hmurrans achieved a major victory and drove four-fifths of Serôsian merrows south, forcing the merrows to invade sahuagin territory on the other side of the Sharksbane Wall. This upset drove the sahuagin to send raiding parties over the Wall, dragging other kingdoms into the war and prolonging it.

The genocidal violence that was conducted during Ninth Serôs War arose concern among the various people of Serôs. Four years after the conflict this culminated in Hmurrath, As'arem, and the organization of wizards known as Dukars to draft together the Laws of Battle, thus ensuring that such devastation might not foul the waters of their land in the future.
