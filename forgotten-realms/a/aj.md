## Ajantis Ilvastarr

Ajantis Ilvastarr was a noble paladin of Helm and a knight of the Order of the Radiant Heart that lived during the mid–14 century DR.

Personality
Fitting for a paladin, Ajantis was known to be a self-righteous, yet somewhat naive individual.

Relationships
He was acquainted with another squire of the Radiant Heart, Anomen Delryn of Athkatla.

History
Ajantis was born to the noble Ilvastarr family of Waterdeep. During his youth he received exceptional martial training learning the art of sword-fighting under the tutelage of Myrmith Splendon.

Some years later he joined the Most Noble Order of the Radiant Heart and garnered the attention of the highly respected Keldorn Firecam. He served the elder knight as a squire and a member of the Radiant Heart Auxiliary.

When the iron crisis plagued the Sword Coast in the, Ajantis felt a call-to-action. He sought to investigate the bandits that plagued the region in order to attain the status within his order as a full-fledged knight.

By the, Ajantis made his way down to Amn. Some time that year, Ajantis and other knights of the Radiant Heart spent time traveling through the Windspear Hills. Unbeknownst to them, the knights had illusions placed upon them by Lord Jierdan Firkraag. The magic gave the knights the appearance of ogres, and also made them perceive other humanoids the same way. As a result of the illusion, Ajantis and his companions were slain by Gorion's Ward.

## Ajhuu

Ajhuu was a powerful marid leader during the time of the Calim Empire.

History
Ajhuu was one of Calim's most powerful allies but grew frustrated when Calim capitulated to the elves of Keltormir and the dwarves of Shanatar to fix his borders at the edges of their realms in . Ajhuu was one of several powerful genies who broke from Calim's alliance to establish their own realms, accompanied by his followers Luar and Haj.

By the time Calim returned to his seat of power in Calimport, the city of Ajhuutal had already been built, with the Maridlands taking the eastern portion of his empire. Calim was determined to bring the rogue genies back under his exclusive control and waged war on Ajhuu, starting the Steam Clashes. Calim won the war and destroyed Ajhuu, but the means he deployed—the Shattering—took a heavy toll on both sides and the surrounding land.

## Ajayib

Ajayib, also called the "City of Wonders", was the youngest of Zakhara's Pearl Cities, and its most westerly. Beyond it lay the wilderness of al-Suqut Mountains.

Geography
Located along the western coast of the Crowded Sea along the banks of Al-Malja River, Ajayib was both the southernmost and westernmost of the major Pearl Cities.

Trade
The source of Ajayib's wealth was cultivated on the steep hills which rose above the city. Though famous for their splendid coffee, Ajayib also exported fruits, frankincense, and other aromatic gums. The productions of bright dyes was also a major portion of their trade dealings.

History
Ajayib was the youngest of the great Pearl Cities, having only established an enlightened mosque in 1357 DR.

Inhabitants
Natives of Ajayib were proud to live on the frontier. They were generally independent, strong-willed, and opinionated.

Halima al-Wahsi was the Calipha of Ajayib in 1367 DR.

Defenses
The city guard of Ajayib numbered 8,000 soldiers and 2,000 cavalry. The Calipha personally commanded an elite unit of farisan that numbered 300 soldiers. Ajayib's navy numbered six ships used to combat pirates and western raiders.

Rumors & Legends
It was rumored that Ajayib was built upon the foundation of a much older city. The ancient settlement was supposedly carved into the cliffs shadowing Ajayib. A series of caves could be found in these cliffs, and though they were looted generations ago, many believed that Ajayib's savage ancestors still prowled the dark caverns.

## Ajami

Ajami, sometimes converted to the feminine as ajamiya  were alien wizards in Zakhara.Ajami was a wizard class kit for the Al-Qadim setting. The word "ajami" can also mean "outlander" in general in Midani.

 Requirements
Ajamis had no special requirements.

 Role
Zakharans treated all outsiders with suspicion, but they were especially wary of those who wielded more power.

 Possessions
Ajamis typically had foreign equipment. Replacing such outland equipment while in Zakhara usually cost at least five times the item's value.

 Benefits
Ajamis were able to learn new universal Zakharan spells.

 Hindrances
New Zakharan magic was impossible for ajamis to learn without taking drastic measures.  Even "regular" spells such as fireball were beyond their reach because the Zakharan fireball was based on the elemental "flame" magic unique to the Land of Fate. Only alien elementalists could learn spells from the equivalent Zakharan element.

Ajamis desperate to learn Zakharan magic could abandon their outsider practices and begin study of Zakharan magic but at the cost of forever losing access to all previous spells they learned.

## Ajax Firestorm

Ajax Firestorm was a red dragon who lived disguised as an elven merchant of Huzuz named Aja Pelima.

Description
Aja Pelima, also called Mad Aja, was the eccentric owner of Mad Aja's Magic for Fools & Simpletons, a shop located in the Grand Bazaar of Huzuz. Aja appeared as an elven male with flaming red hair, but there was much speculation as to his actual heritage. Aja preferred wearing brightly colored clothes and sashes decorated with fireballs and lightning bolts.

In his true form Aja Pelima appeared as a venerable red dragon.

Personality
Ajax was highly intelligent and unusually patient for a red dragon. He had a wild personality and was a high-pressured salesman.

Abilities
Ajax possessed many spell-like abilities but usually favored evocation, enchantment, and charm magic. He also had an uncanny ability to figure out who had the coin to pay for his goods.

Possessions
He was known to possess an amulet of non-detection, various magical wands (fire, frost, lightning), and a unique wand that was unidentifiable. This wand was actually the control rod for his stolen ship.

Activities
Ajax came to Huzuz to track a thief who stole a skyship from him. The ship was located in a warehouse of the Al-Danafi family, but the presence of too many genies prevented Ajax from showing his true power and reclaiming it.

He would often use magic to entice his customers. The city watch was often called to his shop after the use of a pyrotechnics spell. He enjoyed levitating customers and conversing with them in the air while he hung upside down.

History
Ajax actually stole the skyship that he was searching for from a mage from Halruaa.

Rumors and Legends
Many believed "Aja" to be a blatant member of the Brotherhood of the True Flame. Others thought he was a genie masquerading as a human. His true nature was not detectable through the use of magic.

## Ajmer Forest

The Ajmer Forest was located in northeast Durpar in the Shining Lands in 1367 DR.

Description
This tropical rainforest was stiflingly hot and humid.

Inhabitants
The Ajmer Forest was home to the only sizable community of elves within Durpar. These elves were unique among their kind in that they followed the teachings of Adama. The elves monitored the loggers to make sure they collected their timber in a manner that would allow the forest to recover quickly.

The southern regions of the forest were home to monsters and many exotic animals. It was even rumored that a rakshasa noble had a headquarters there.

## Ajan al-Yaqud

Ajan al-Yaqud was the leader of the members of House Bakr who chose to remain in the High Desert, as of 1367 DR, after their sheikh decided to live in Tajar.

Activities
Ajan often led his desert riders in the south-central region of the desert in attacks against bandits who preyed upon caravans bound for Akota. Ajan believed remaining in the desert was the best way for his tribe to avoid danger. He maintained relations with the Sheikh in Tajar by sending representatives to exchange news. He refused to enter Tajar himself because of a vision of his own doom that would transpire should he do so.

Relationships
Ajan was the son of Sheikh Ali al-Hadd's sister and the nephew of the Sheikh.

## Ajaman

Ajaman was a warrior of the Qahtan tribe who roamed the deserts of Anauroch with his fellow tribesmen during the mid–14 century DR.

Description
He had a rugged appearance, with a lean, muscular build and skin that had been weathered by years traveling under At'ar the Merciless. He kept a thick moustache that was common among the mature men of the Bedine.

Personality
Ajaman was a good-natured and measured man with a strong sense of duty and tradition.

Abilities
Having grown up as well-trained warrior, Ajaman was skilled with the bow.

Relationships
Ajaman was the son of the Qahtani sheikh by his second wife. He had a young brother Kadumi who was well on his way to becoming a mature warrior of the Qahtan.

For a very brief time, Ajaman was married to Ruha, a sorceress of the Mtair Dhafir tribe. Like everyone else in both their tribes, Ajaman was unaware that his wife was a spellcaster. While the two were only married for a matter of two days, they formed a connection quickly and according to Ruha, truly had every opportunity to fall in love with one another.

History
In the, two days after his union with Ruha, Ajaman was tasked to watch over his people at night. He took sentry atop El Ma'ra Dat-ur Ojhogo but was struck down by a bolt of magical energy cast by some Zhentarim spellcaster working with asabi mercenaries. His body was later buried by his brother Kadumi and widow Ruha, who kept his jambiya with her for quite some time.

## Ajhuutal

Ajhuutal was a city built by the marid Ajhuu and his followers when they rebelled against the djinni Calim and founded the Maridlands.

Geography
The city was built on a coastal promontory west of the Alimir Mountains, in what was then the eastern extent of the Calim Empire. Following its destruction it sat in  of water in the Shining Sea,  south of Almraiven at the underwater edge of the bay.

History

When Calim's empire building paused in  to appease the Shanatar dwarves and the elves of Tethir and Darthiir, his marid vassal Ajhuu took it as a sign of weakness and seized the eastern frontier of the Calim Empire, founding the Maridlands. The Steam Clashes that followed were highly destructive, culminating in the Shattering in . With a great genie-crafted ritual, Calim created an earthquake that smashed the city and the surrounding area, causing it to fall into the sea. The power of the magic was grossly excessive, however, and it caused thousands of square miles of land to break up, collapse, and be replaced by rushing waters as the coastline was reshaped.

While Ajhuu and his realm was destroyed, the city itself remained mostly intact under more than 300 feet of water. Encrusted with corals and seaweeds, it came to be inhabited by merfolk and some sea elves who took control of the remaining marid magics of the city, creating a mythal-like field over the city that let them control the waters around it. When the Curse of Irphong sank the Pasha's Palace in the, the residents of Ajhuutal and the undersea Dragon's Neck Peninsula thoroughly looted the wreck, carrying off valuable rust-resistant magical tools and weapons while the kraken and sahuagin focused on gold and other treasures.

Over time, the story of Ajhuutal became conflated with the unrelated ruined settlement of Lost Ajhuutal in the Spider Swamp.

In the 1370s DR, the city became visible and accessible to the surface. This prompted a great deal of excitement for treasure hunters and explorers, but wiser heads worried that Ajhuu or his kin had returned and could present a threat.
