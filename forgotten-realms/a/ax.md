## Axe of Mirabar

The Axe of Mirabar was the two-thousand strong militia of Mirabar, consisting of humans and five hundred dwarves.

Organization
It was commanded by Torgar Hammerstriker.

Activities
The Axe was responsible for keeping the mines of Mirabar clear of monsters.

Possessions
Its members wore the crest of Mirabar (a deep-red double-bladed axe with a pointed haft and a flaring, flat base on a black field) on their shield or armor. They fought with crossbows, lances, and hammers, riding mountain ponies, and rothé in the winter.

Members
New members had to take an oath to protect the Marchion of Mirabar.

## Axe

An axe was a single- or double-bladed weapon made from a metal or stone head mounted on a haft of varying length. The haft was usually made of wood to reduce the overall weight. The term "axe" is used here as an archetype of hand-held slashing weapons that fit this description and where the Blade length is considerably less then the overall weapon length.

Axes were the favored weapons of many fighters and barbarians (especially dwarves).

## Axe Kingdoms

The Axe Kingdoms were a pair of merrow kingdoms, Khuur and Nmalk, in Serôs under the Sea of Fallen Stars in the 6th and 7th centuries DR. They represented the last of merrow civilization in Serôs.

History
In the Year of the Deep Wound, 582 DR, Tarag, a chieftain of the sea ogres, used the powers of the sword Ilbratha and his own battle strategies to conquer the western frontiers of the merfolk realm of Hmurrath. There, Tarag founded the two Axe Kingdoms of Khuur and Nmalk.

The Axe Kingdoms and Hmurrath fell to fighting in the Year of the Killing Blow, 653 DR, beginning the Ninth Serôs War.

The merrow suffered a major defeat in the Battle of the Haunted Plains in the Year of the Coveted Briars, 654 DR. Following their defeat, four-fifths of all merrow in Serôs were forced to flee south to the other side of the Sharksbane Wall. The rest survived on the Hmur Plateau or living amidst the ruins on the Haunted Plains.

Three years of conflict culminated in the destruction of the Axe Kingdoms and the fall of the merrow to the merfolk of Hmurrath in the Year of the Volanth, 655 DR. This saw the end of merrow "civilization" in Serôs.

Geography
The Axe Kingdoms lay along the Dragon Coast and the coast of Sembia, in the Sea of Fallen Stars.

## Axar Xyrl

Axar Xyrl was a morkoth and an arcount of the Arcanum of Olleth in Serôs in 1370 DR.

Personality
Axar Xyrl was a calculating and scheming individual who spoke what he truly thought on very rare occasions. Normally Axar Xyrl remained behind the scenes and personally acted only if his subordinates were all defeated or he had a very personal interest in the matter. He greatly disliked being touched by anyone or anything.

History
Around 1270 DR, Axar Xyrl found the Iltkazar Scepter.

During the Twelfth Serôs War, Axar Xyrl tried to acquire the  Claw of Xynakt during the morkoth attack on Abydos but his plan was ruined by the triton Keros.

In 1370 DR, Axar Xyrl accepted an alliance with the aquatic elf tyrant Gantar Kraok in exchange for the promise to gain a copy of the Tome of Tujar the Dark Adherent, which explained all the secrets of the Dukars, but in truth he planned to betray the elf and steal all he could find.

Axar Xyrl first sent his subordinates Major Qyzal and Grand Caster Duupox to organize an ambush against some surface-dweller enemies of Kraok. Later, he and Gantar Kraok entered into the battle to help his subordinates. However, Gantar did not care about hitting the morkoth and when Axar Xyrl and Duupox confronted the elf about it, Gantar insulted everyone in the place and teleported away. Thus, in revenge, Axar offered an alliance with the surface dwellers.

Axar apparently fled Olleth when the Abolethic Sovereignty took over that realm, as, in the late 15th century Dale Reckoning, he was discovered living on Hawk's Isle, where he took his pleasures kidnapping and vivisecting people, only to release them as mongrelfolk. After kidnapping a Turmishan noblewoman though, he was defeated by adventurers sent to rescue her. The noblewoman, Zehira Deryan, took the Iltkazar Scepter from his lair.

## Axelcrantz

Axelcrantz was a dwarf mystic in 1484 DR.

History
Just after the start of the War of the Silver Marches, Axelcrantz sensed a menace caused by a drow artifact in the Underdark near the city. He sensed that to recover this object he needed a particular adventuring party that would arrive in Mithral Hall in some time.

However, followed by two drow, Axelcrantz abandoned Mithril Hall. He left a letter and orders that it be delivered to the adventurers when they arrived. In the letter, he predicted that they would meet in Citadel Adbar. All went as he had foreseen and Axelcrantz met the adventurers in Citadel Adbar, explaining about the artifact they needed to destroy.

Studying the recovered Wand of Tir'Lien and thanks to visions sent by Moradin, Axelcrantz discovered the drow wanted to use the Fell Fortress, at the border of the Shadowfell, to summon an undead army. He hired the adventuring party to stop this evil plan. Thanks to the Wand, the Fell Fortress was sealed.

Later, Axelcrantz went to Blingdenstone and called the adventurers, asking them to investigate problems in the trade between the deep gnome city and Gracklstugh.

## Axe beak

Axe beaks were tall and aggressive flightless birds.

Description
Axe beaks got their name from their sharp wedge-shaped beaks. They had strong necks and powerful legs, which they used to sprint at high speeds.

Abilities
Axebeaks were faster runners than most horses.In v.3.5 rules, both axebeaks and horses have the Run feat, which allows them to move five times their normal movement speed over flat, even terrain, but axebeaks have a speed 5 higher than that of a light horse. This calculates to  for the horse and  for the axebeak. For comparison, in the real world, some horses can reach speeds of over, but v.3.5 consistently gives lower maximum speeds for animals, likely because the Monster Manuals include representatives of average specimens.

Behavior
Axe beaks were aggressive hunters and usually hunted during daytime. They were always prone to attack any unfamiliar creatures that approached them.

Combat
The main natural weapon an axe beak used in combat was its heavy and sharp beak.

Ecology
Axe beaks were most commonly found in hills and grasslands, but they were also common to the jungles of Chult and the Snout of Omgar.

Baby axe beaks were called "skike" . Flocks of axe beaks were believed to be protective of their skike, rushing to their aid in case of danger.

The tortles of Ahoyhoy sometimes captured wild axe beaks to sell them to visitors.

## Axe of hurling

The axe of hurling was a unique magical axe that would return to its owner.

History
A group of adventurers heading to Menzoberranzan acquired the weapon at the second level of the Temple of Dumathoin. After heading down to the lowest level of the temple, they sacrificed a diamond to an enchanted fountain of healing power.

They were met by the spirit of the fountain, which told them that the spirit of Dorn Black Axe had awoken, and requested an audience with them.

Dorn retold his story of battling with the drow alongside kin in the Cavern of Cloven Heads. His clan were forced from their home, and he was slain in battle. He urged them to take up his fight, and avenge Clan Black Axe. Here, Dorn bestowed upon the party the magical returning axe, the Axe of Hurling.

After leaving the Temple of Dumathoin, the adventurers went further into the drow territory of the Underdark. They finally reached the city of Menzoberranzan, and cut down many dark elves.

Isundoun Impaerlath, a dwarven blacksmith from Saerloon, was known for forging these weapons.

## Axeholm

Axeholm was a dwarven fortress carved into a mountainside, within the Sword Mountains range in the Sword Coast North. The keep's crest was a dwarven gauntlet holding a handaxe.

Location
It was located in the northern slopes of the mountain, about a day's travel south of Phandalin.

Interior
The lower halls of Axeholm included an imposing entrance gauntlet that opened up to the great hall, along with a large barracks and numerous guard posts. The dwarven protectors had easy access to their well-stocked armory and smithy. At the rear of the grand hall was the keep's throne room, as well as its dining hall and kitchens.

History
Long before the 1490s DR, the fortress was infiltrated by an elf named Vyldara. She was apprehended, imprisoned, and eventually killed while trying to escape custody. Risen as a banshee, Vyldara haunted the site of her death, causing the dwarves to abandon their home for other lands.

Inhabitants
After Axeholm became haunted, a number of ghouls formed their den within within its lower halls.

## Axe storm of Clangeddin

Axe storm of Clangeddin was a spell used by clerics of Clanggedin Silverbeard.

Effects
When cast, this spell increased an axe-wielding dwarf's skill in melee combat. Their strikes were faster and more powerful, and axes thrown at a target never missed. The amount of individuals this spell affected was based on the caster's skill level. This spell was not cumulative, and slow cancelled out the effects of the spell.

Components
In addition to verbal and somatic components, this spell required material components, the holy symbol of Clanggedin Silverbeard and a small vial of air that was capped during a thunderstorm.

## Axalt

Axalt was a Revered Counselor of Nexal who ruled over the empire in its most prosperous era in the early-to-mid–14 century DR.

History
Axalt ascended the throne in the  at a young age, similarly to his ancestor, Ipana III some two centuries prior. The new Revered Counselor was the grandson of Chalco, the previous ruler of Nexal. The boy's tutors convinced the noblemen of Nexal that despite his youth, he had the keen mind and great instincts of a true ruler.

Like so many of his predecessors, Revered Counselor Axalt faced Kultaka several times in minor conflicts, yet none were as successful as the war waged by his grandfather. The old adversary of Chalco, now the Revered Counselor of Kultaka, Takamal was unbeatable in the game of strategy, leaving the Kultakans still unsubjugated. Axalt's own son, Naltecona, was the general who led several of these campaigns.

However, it was not military conquests that made Axalt the great leader he was. The Revered Counselor oversaw the creation of the grand palace in Nexal, later referred to as the Palace of Axalt, as well as the Aqueduct of Axalt that carried fresh water from Cicada Spring  away to the center of the city. Fresh water was made available to all citizens and benefited the city greatly.

The Revered Counselor Axalt died still a young man in the . He left Nexal in a relatively peaceful state and the empire at its strongest. Nexal was paid tribute by the subjugated Pezelac, Huacli, and Kolan with no rebellions, and traders did business with faraway Payit. The rule was passed along to his son Naltecona, the last Revered Counselor of Nexal, who was killed in, by the Golden Legion of Amn.

## Axe Bridge

The Axe Bridge, originally named the Clan Kuldelver Bridge, was an ancient dwarven bridge that spanned the Splendarrllur river in the eastern reaches of Amn.

Location
The bridge carried the Eshpurta Road over the Splendarrllur, positioned a little under halfway from Keczulla to Eshpurta. The similar Star Bridge lay further to the west towards Keczulla.

Structure
The Axe Bridge was a massive stonewrought structure that spanned the widest possible floodheight of the Splendarrllur, and it was of identical design to the Star Bridge. It was built of extraordinarily resilient stonework, such that by the 14 century DR it bore only the slightest traces of wear and tear, mostly from recent heavy cart traffic. It was decorated with the heavily faded runes for Clan Kuldelver Bridge on the railing stones, with the axe symbol granting it its later name.

History
The bridge was built around  by the dwarves of the High Shanataran realm of Korolnor as part of their great network of surface projects. It was inscribed with the name Clan Kuldelver Bridge, after the dwarves that built it.

Long after the fall of Shanatar, humans from Calimshan settled the region and discovered the twin bridges. With little dwarfcraft to compare to, the true origins of the Kuldelver bridge went largely unknown, though they were widely used by travelers. Only the careful examination by scholars and dwarves of the bridges' eroded runes eventually revealed the fact that the bridges had originated in the ancient dwarven ages, built by Clan Kuldelver.

During the 14 century DR, the Axe Bridge marked the eastern extent of the Council Army patrol from Keczulla.

## Axe for the tall kin

Axes for the tall kin were a variety of enchanted double axes created on the Sword Coast North in the late 14 century DR, but their design was traced back to the land of Rashemen.

Powers
There existed three variants of the axes for the tall kin weapon, each possessed a +1, +3, and +8 enchantments what made the axe's blade deadlier and guided the wielder's hand towards the target. All three versions glowed with a gentle white radiance, illuminating  around. However, the strongest version from Rashemen increased the radius to, dealt fire damage to the targets, and possessed keen edges.

An axe for the tall kin could be created from an enchanted double axe and a slab of adamantite.

History
According to the Rashemaar legends, the axe for the tall kin was once wielded by a legendary giant king called Kirsk. Its blades were powerful enough to effortlessly split a mountain into pebbles. The story then claimed that the axe was lost after Kirsk split a mountain that bordered a sea that spilled over and drowned all the giants. This legend was one of the reasons why many civilized lands considered Rashemaar quite strange.

More prosaic versions of axes for the tall kin with a simpler +1 enchantment were forged by Master Blacksmith Marrok of Shining Knight Arms and Armor shop of Neverwinter to help the heroes who sought the cure from the dreaded Wailing Death of the . Later the same year, more powerful versions were created by Master Blacksmith Barun of Beorunna's Well to help the forces of Neverwinter stand against Lady Aribeth the Betrayer, who led a marching army of Luskan in the deadly but short-lived Neverwinter-Luskan war.
