## Zvopua

Zvopua was a foulwing involved in the Zhent invasion of Shadowdale in 1375 DR.

History
Zvopua was the loyal mount of the high ranking member of Zhentarim Eregul the Freestave; like his master Zvopua was part of the Zhentarim Occupation Army in Shadowdale in 1375. Eregul and Zvopua were escorting a Zhent supply caravan when they were attacked by some adventurers and Eregul and Zvopua were killed.

## Zvestra

Lady Zvestra was the leader of the avorals and one of the Companions of the Celestial Lion Talisid of Elysium. According to the World Tree cosmology, Zvestra resided with Talisid and the other guardinal paragons, in the House of Nature.

Abilities
Beyond the powers common to all avorals, Lady Zvestra was trained as a wizard and a cleric.

History
Zvestra was the replacement for Duke Windheir, who retired from the Five Companions in the mid- to late-14 century DR. Her tenure was very short, however, and she was soon replaced by Dutchess Sathia for unspecified reasons.
