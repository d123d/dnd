## -27000 DR

Circa: The Fey open gates from Faerie to Toril, allowing the first elves to cross over and settle, as part of an effort to undermine the power of the dragons. However, one tribe, the Ilythiiri, negotiates with the dragons and establishes a small southern kingdom based around their capital, Atorrnash, which stands as a glorious example of elven culture for millennia to come.

## -276 DR

The bladelord psiocracy of Jhaamdath is disposed in a bloody coup, and is replaced by the militant Emperor Dharien. The emperor orders the construction of a great navy on the Sea of Fallen Stars.
 Members of the church of Auppenser protest events in Jhaamdath. Hundreds of dissenters are arrested and imprisoned. Several are accused of treason and executed. The majority are subjected to mind-altering psionics that instil loyalty to the emperor. The crackdown mutes the church's protests, and many of the faithful go into hiding.

 Jhaamdathans logging in the Chondalwood feud with the native elves. Over the next two decades, Jhaamdathan armies hunt, kill, and massacre the elves.

## -274 DR

In the last battle between Shou Lung and Wa, the Shou land forces overwhelm those of Wa, but their fleet is grounded on reefs concealed by wu jen working for the Wa warlords.

 The sixth Emperor Chin of the Hai Dynasty of Shou Lung and his court are killed in the fleet's destruction, ending the Hai Dynasty. Rourke, a foreigner piloting the emperor's ship, rescues the Emblems of Imperial Authority and returns to the Imperial City. But Rourke is killed  and the Emblems disappear. The Black Cycle of Years commences in Shou Lung.
  In T'u Lung, the Black Cycle is characterized by frequent invasions, both from the south and from minor lords in the north, and natural disasters, such as blights, crop failures, and the flooding of the Fenghsintzu. Order collapses, except where local feudal lords still hold sway. The Wai and Yang clans begin to rise to prominence.

## -273 DR

A truce is signed between General Yoshibei of Wa and the Shou governor of the northern part of the island. Yoshibei installs himself as warlord of the Wa province.
 Over the next century, Wa suffers under wars between the nobles of Wa and the governors from Shou Lung. Over time, Shou governors of Wa assimilate into native culture, and have more allegiance to the Emperor of Wa than to that of Shou Lung.
 In this period, the Order of the Snake gains influence in all levels of Wa's governments.
 In Jhaamdath, Emperor Dharien commands the army to seize udoxias still controlled by the church of Auppenser. Hundreds of faithful citizens stand before the temples, preventing the soldiers from entering. After a brief standoff, Dharien and his troops back down.
