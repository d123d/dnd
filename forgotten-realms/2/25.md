## -25400 DR

Circa: Gold elf refugees of the fall of Tintageer in the world of Faerie come to Toril through a magical portal. Led by Prince Durothil, they settle on a continent they name Faerûn, meaning "the One Land". They mix with the existing green elves and the gold elves become known as sun elves, while the descendants of the lone silver elf, Sharlario Moonflower, give rise to the moon elves.

 Circa: The gold elf priestess Bonnalurie dies to open the gate to Toril and allow the elven refugees of Tintageer through.

## 253 DR

With Shou Lung forces driven from Wa, and facing defeat there, the twelfth Emperor Chin of the Kao Dynasty instead decrees The Unleashing of Shackles, formally recognizing Wa as an independent state. The remnants of the Regiment of the Grey Blossom are withdrawn. In the capital of Shou Lung, monuments are erected that describe the emperor as a wonderful and wise emancipator of noble foreign peoples.

 The Grey Blossom Regiment is formed from troops from Shou Lung who defect to Nakamaru's daimyo.

 For six days in autumn, major cities along the Fenghsintzu River in T'u Lung experience the Nights of the Screaming Demons, when huge roaring spirits visit the cities and drive off the streets. In the mornings, prominent bureaucrats notorious for incompetence and corruption are discovered missing, encouraging the remainder to lift their game. A H'sin-to crime lord links the Screaming Demons to the Black Leopard Cult in a 295 DR biography.
 Josidiah Starym is granted a gift from Anders Beltgarden, a human wizard who settled in Cormanthyr. The gift is a newly created magical figurine of a black panther which can transform into Guenhwyvar, who becomes Josidiah's friend.

## 256 DR

Illuskans from the island nations of Tuern and Gundarlun invade the Moonshae Isles. The Ffolk surrender the northern islands to the invaders.
 The monk Jinchin started a revolt in Kanazaki, gathering an army of ronin, bandits, criminals, and sorcerers.

 Talana Brakuularn, the reigning magister, is slain in bed at a magefair by an unknown assailant. Shortly afterwards her killer was slain by another mage, as was his, until ten or eleven more mages died. The last assailant staggers back to bed, then is slain by another mage polymorphed in the form of a lizard man, who in turn is slain by the mage Eirl Rauthantannar.

 Eirl Rauthantannar becomes the next magister following a string of mage murders.
