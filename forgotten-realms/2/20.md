## 205 DR

Uktar: The Harakhti Uprisings fail when Qysar Amahl Shoon VI slays Harakhti in single combat. Some 180,000 lives are lost as Shoon and Harakhti's armies clash northwest of Ankhapur.
Unther and Mulhorand drive the barbarian forces from their lands, chasing them back into the south where they are destroyed.

 Illusk is recolonised by settlers from Uthtower, Yarlith, and the Mlembryn.

 Qysar Amahl Shoon VI is found dead in his bed, slain by the sting of a poisonous scorpion.

 Amahl's brother, Shoon IV takes the throne as emperor of the Shoon Imperium.
 Taman Steeldrake becomes Grand Prince of Illusk.

## 204 DR

Qysar Amahl Shoon V dies of old age. His third grandson Amahl Shoon VI leads the Shoon Empire in two months of mourning.

 Qayadin Amahl Shoon VI becomes the next emperor of the Imperium.

 An incarnation of Anhur wielding the legendary blade Hadryllis leads the Mulhorandi forces to victory against Eltabranar in the War of Claws. The demon lord Eltab is defeated and imprisoned in a demoncyst under Thaymount.
 An escaped gladiator called Harakhti gathers a force of over 25,000 escaped slaves and embittered peasants then overwhelms the complacent border guards of the eastern Shoon Imperium, freeing a further 120,000 slaves.

## 20 DR

The elven holiday of Cinnaelos'Cor, or "The Day of Corellon's Peace", is adopted by humans and named Shieldmeet. It is included in the Calendar of Harptos, and celebrated every four years afterward.

 Large-scale Jhaamdathan migration out of the Vilhon Reach ends with the founding of the trade-city of Iljak there. This commences efforts to construct a new human realm in the Reach.

 The yuan-ti of House Se'Sehen retreat from their territory in the Tashalar, unable to hold it without the influence of Sseth. They drift into somnolence in their remaining holdings on the northwest coast of the Lapal Sea for over 1300 years.

## -2087 DR

The last era of the Dragonfall War between Tiamat and Bahamut begins with the rise of Unther, when the Mulan introduce Tiamat's human-oriented worship to Faerûn.

 Enlil and his priesthood declare that Tiamat is the Nemesis of the Gods. Over the coming centuries of Unther's rise and fall, the god-kings blame for her Unther's every setback. Conflict begins between Tiamat and Marduk the Justice Bringer.
 Enlil discovers pearls on the western shore of the Alamber Sea. There he builds Unthalass, the City of Gems.

 The nation of Unther is founded, with Unthalass as its capital.

20,000 Leagues Under the Sea was a book found on the Sea of Swords and Nelanther Islands circa 1372 DR.

History
The book was penned by an unknown author peculiarly named Jules Verne, sometime before the . Several copies of the novel could be found in the pirate-ridden Spindrift Town, notably, one copy belonged to the Willigam family of the pirate town and was kept in their manor.
