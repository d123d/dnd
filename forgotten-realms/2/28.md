## 285 DR

Untisczer launches a failed revolt against the Shoon Imperium, provoking the empire to embark on the Tashalar Campaigns. Untisczer is destroyed as a demonstrated of the Imperium's power. Shoon V's installs military governors in every city along the Tashtan coast.
 Lapaliiya is seized by the Shoon Imperium. However, Lapaliiyan cities remain largely independent under Qysara Shoon V.
 The Shoon Imperium advances into Thindol, but its armies suffer in battles with lizardfolk.
 The Shoon Imperium attempts to conquer and colonize the Nelanther Isles. While the invasion succeeded, the colonization effort failed due to the haughtiness of the chosen noble rulers over the cold weather and wind.

 The slave-nation of Nimpeth is established. It becomes the first city-state in the Vilhon Reach to successfully opposite Chondath's power, as frequent attempts to conquer it end it failure.

 The Archmage of Untisczer, Anthilar, escapes to a hidden mountain base west of the Tashan Gap, where he transforms himself into a lich and plots to control Tashlutan merchants.

Appendix
Notes
Serpent Kingdoms and The Grand History of the Realms, page 68, name this the "Year of Wasteful Pride". However, the widely shared copy of the Roll of Years names this the "Year of Wasted Pride". The published name is adopted here.

## -2809 DR

In the eastern jungles of the Chultan Peninsula, the enslaved Lapal tribes revolt against their yuan-ti masters.

 On Katashaka, the continent southwest of Faerûn, a number of tribes, including the Eshowe, Tabaxi, and Thinguth, accept the couatls' preachings of Ubtao. Following the couatls, they migrate across the seas to the Wild Coast of the Chultan Peninsula and travel to the Peaks of Flame. There, an avatar of Ubtao welcomes them.
 The Thinguth tribe settle in the central jungles of the Sanrach and Thindol Basins.
