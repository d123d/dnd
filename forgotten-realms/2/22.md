## 228 DR

Summer sees the publication of many tomes of impressive sagecraft in the Realms, detailing some of humanity's greatest magical accomplishments. Though each tome and it's author are eventually forgotten, a single copy of The Trail of Wonder by Aumtevel Dlarrin of Elembar was to become stored in Candlekeep.

## -220 DR

In Serôs, the city-state of Nantarum is besieged by merfolk led by Ristan when the city's mythal emits a strange humming and magical energy discharges in the surrounding sea. Thirty contrite Dukar and nobles are found transformed into coral statues along the avenue to the Council Academy of the Dukars. The Second Dukars' War is ended.
