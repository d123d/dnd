## 262 DR

Five prominent elf houses of Cormanthyr, the Bharaclaiev, Hyshaanth, Rhae-vaern, Tellynnan, and Starym, refuse to accept Coronal Eltargrim's tolerance of non-elves, especially humans, not their continuing encroachment on ancestral elven lands. Avoiding conflict with fellow elves, the five houses leave Myth Drannor and work to catalog the humans' so-called crimes against nature. From them, the Eldreth Veluuthra, a group dedicated to removing all humans from Faerûn, is formed.
