## 216 DR

Uthgrael Aumar, the Stag King of Athalantar, dies in battle against orcs near Jander.

 The seven sons of Uthgrael Aumar, called the Warring Princes of Athalantar, openly battle one another to win the throne of Athalantar.
 Orc armies fight one another for dominance across the North. Thousands of goblinkind die in the fighting.

 The city of Thuridru is founded in the Sea of Fallen Stars as part of the merfolk kingdom of Eadraal.

## -212 DR

__NOTOC__

 Tethyrian barbarian clans and elven allies, led by Chieftain Darrom Ithal capture Myratma for a second time from the Calishites in the Battle of the Purple Marches.
 The white dragon, Ufloq, awakens in its lair in the Olyniak Crevasse of the Great Glacier. Several dozen Nakulutiun witnessed the event, attracted to the crevasse by the loud rumbling. Many were promptly devoured by the hungry dragon. This is the only recorded sighting of Ufloq.

 Darrom Ithal becomes king of Tethyr, and Calimshan finally grants it its independence. It is the beginning of the Calendar of Tethyreckoning, known as the Founding Year.

## 211 DR

The dwarves of Clan Melairkyn are driven downwards and out of Undermountain and their ancient home, Melairbode, after succumbing to drow attacks. Clan Melairkyn ultimately disappears. The victorious drow claim much of Melairbode as their domain, and call it Kyorlamshin.
 As the Untheric and Mulhorandi armies invade the Council Hills region, the four Arkaiun barbarian tribes flee. They migrate to the southwest and journey along the grasslands of the southern coast of Faerûn. Eventually, they settle around the Bay of Dancing Dolphins.
