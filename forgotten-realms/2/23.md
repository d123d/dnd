## 238 DR

The Mad Mage Ihundyl dies in battle against Elminster Aumar and Myrjala Dark-Eyes.

 Prevalent damp weather and rotting diseases all across northern Faerûn promote the rampant growth of mushrooms. Trade in the harvested mushrooms and mushroom-related products (especially fried ones in salted gravy) is rife. More alarmingly though, reports of myconid sightings also become common.

 Boldovar Obarskyr succeeds Tharyann "the Elder" as King of Cormyr.

 Tamara Cymrych, future High Queen of the Moonshae Isles, is born to Cymrych Hugh.

 King Tharyann "the Elder" of Cormyr dies.

## 236 DR

Qysar Shoon IV and Iryklathagra conspire to slay the silver dragon Rhimnasarl the Shining of the Marching Mountains, who has long opposed Shoon rule. Afterwards, Shoon IV betrays Iryklathagra and seizes Rhimnasarl's hoard for himself. Iryklathagra responds by making and defending her own secret lair in the Marching Mountains.
 The shadow dragon Shadraxil attacks Cormyr's easternmost outposts. In response, the War Wizards trap Shadraxil in a prison on the Plane of Shadow.

 A Mulhorandi wizard develops a spell that creates poisonous gas clouds which last for days at a time. A coalition of neighbours and rivals slay the wizard, but not before at least part of the spell is stolen.

 King Dalagar's road from Athkatla to Ormath is completed after six years. Its' works began in 230 DR.

 Eirl Rauthantannar begins an eleven-year stint as the Magister.

## 237 DR

An insane wizard living as a hermit in Tsharoon (which would later become part of the Quoya Desert) named Ansryn Tehvyae, develops a powerful spell that remotely teleports dragons against their will to where the caster desires. The paranoid man uses it often, leaving much of the eastern Realms in disarray. Eventually, Tehvyae is identified and located by some of the surviving dragons, who descend upon him in a fury. The spell itself is not accounted for.

 Warren Cymrych I, future High King of the Moonshae Isles, is born to Cymrych Hugh.

## 234 DR

This year is marked for the discovery of an arcane healing salve by a Calishite alchemist made from the petals of a plains wildflower called the bloodflower.
