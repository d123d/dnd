## -2 DR

Following a year and a half of war against Tethyr, Calishite forces destroy Ithmong and nearly wipe out Clan Ithal. Tethyr is defeated.

 In Greengrass, King Amahl II of Tethyr swears allegiance to Syl-Pasha Akkabar el Shoon of Calimshan. With rule over both nations, this is the beginning of the Shoon Imperium.
