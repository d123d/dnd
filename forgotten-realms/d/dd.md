## D&D Attack Wing

D&D Attack Wing is a strategy-based tabletop game released in October 2014. It is set in the Forgotten Realms setting

It can be played in three different forms. The first form is free play, where the players build their legion according to the basic rules of the game. The second form consists of playing scenarios involving good versus evil, with added neutral allegiances in certain modules. The third form involves tournament play; this form is usually played in a local store sponsored by Wizkids. In it, the players set up a battlefield with objectives. They are subject to time limits and must play in rounds. The winner gets a limited-edition prize figure.

The starter set comes with a copper dragon (Galadaeros), a blue dragon (Eshaedra), and a red dragon (Balagos). Some of the basic game tools are attack/defense dice, movement dials, objective tokens, character cards, upgrade cards, and custom movement measurement sticks.

## D&D Beyond

D&D Beyond is a companion application and toolset designed for use with the fifth edition of Dungeons & Dragons.

Description
The application allows players to browse digital versions of D&D source materials such as rulebooks and materials, as well as provides various tools assisting with the gameplay mechanics of Dungeons & Dragons, including a character builder and sheet manager, combat tracker and virtual tabletop maps.

In addition to digitized versions of physical source media, some digital content pertaining to the Forgotten Realms has been released exclusively on the platform.

History
The service was originally released in 2017 by Curse, as an alternative to the previously existing D&D Insider service created by Wizards of the Coast. In 2018 Curse had been acquired by Fandom. Later in 2022 D&D Beyond was purchased by Hasbro (the parent company of WotC), and eventually became the official companion app for Dungeons & Dragons.

## D&D: Adventurer 1/King under the Hill

King under the Hill is an encounter published in Dungeons & Dragons: Adventurer #1. The encounter was the first in the series of encounters comprising Adventure #1.

The Stonehill Inn was assaulted by a swarm of cranium rats. The creatures were pushed by unknown forces to acquire the brightblade interred within the tomb of the human chieftain buried beneath the inn's foundation. The adventurers came in time to save the staff and guests of the inn.

## D&D: Adventurer 2/The Forgotten Vault

The Forgotten Vault is an encounter published in Dungeons & Dragons: Adventurer #2. The encounter was the second in the series of encounters comprising Adventure #1.

Summary
Circa 1451 DR,The encounter said Daran Edermath found the hidden vault 40 years prior to the events of the Dungeons & Dragons: Adventurer adventures. when Daran Edermath located a secret vault beneath a ruined elven villa in the Neverwinter Wood. However, he subsequently lost all memory of this discovery. Decades later, around 1491 DR, he stumbled upon a note in his own handwriting referencing the vault, but it failed to jog his memory. To solve the mystery, Daran enlisted a group of adventurers—who would later gain fame as the Champions of Neverwinter—to uncover the vault's secrets.

## D&D: Adventurer 4/Daylight Robbery

The Forgotten Vault is an encounter published in Dungeons & Dragons: Adventurer #3. The encounter was the third in the series of encounters comprising Adventure #1.

Summary

While transporting their mistress, the vampire spawn Etterine Tzavari, her servants were caught in a mudslide not far Phandalin. A sole surviving servant, Malet Crint hired a group of adventurers—who would later gain fame as the Champions of Neverwinter—to uncover the vault's secrets. to escort him and his mysterious "cargo" safely to Waterdeep. Their journey was interrupted by an attack from Zhentarim bandits, forcing the Champions to defend their charge and his dangerous secret.

## D&D: Adventurer 8/Fun at the Faire

Fun at the Faire is an encounter published in Dungeons & Dragons: Adventurer #8. The encounter was the eighth in the series of encounters comprising Adventure #1.

Summary

Lady Keris Daunt visited Phandalin, organized, and sponsored a faire for the denizens of the town to honor the deeds of the group of adventurers who would later gain fame as the Champions of Neverwinter. The celebration offered the adventurers a chance to compete in games and earn renown. Lady Daunt's own knight, Sir Bertalot Malcer, was plotting to kill Lady Daunt. The adventurers had to navigate the festivities to uncover a treasonous conspiracy and defeat the servant of Dagult Neverember.
