## Dzunn

Dzunn was a merchant from the city of Sheirtalar in Lapaliiya in the 14 century DR.

Dzunn partnered with six other merchants—Alvund of Ormpetarr, Chond of Calaunt, Jhasso of Baldur's Gate, Nammna of Milvarune, Pomphur of Almraiven, and Shield of Everlund—to form the Seven Suns Trading Coster. The "Seven Suns" referred to the seven merchants themselves. Dzunn's own small merchant company was converted into a regional base for the coster.

Dzunn and the Seven Suns were in business by 1358 DR, and still going by 1374 DR.

## Dzance's guardian

Dzance's guardian was a magical shield of unknown origin, though it was believed to be dwarven in craft.

Powers
The shield enveloped its bearer in an aura that deflected magic missiles and absorbed magical electricity, such as that from shocking grasp. The latter effect would remain in the shield and, if its wielder so desired, could be discharged at nearby foes. When this occurred, there was about a one-in-three chance the shield and its wielder would be shifted into a random plane of existence.

History
The shield was discovered in the depths of Gauntlgrym by the ranger Dzance. Upon his retirement in Silverymoon, he bestowed it to a young knight named Belpir. Upon leaving for adventure, Belpir, and the shield, have not been seen since.

## Dzance

Dzance was a ranger of the North who retired in Silverymoon after his years spent adventuring across the Realms.

History
At some point in his adventuring career, Dzance explored the depths of the ancient dwarven city of Gauntlgrym. While exploring the former capital of Delzoun, he recovered a magical shield within a chest that came to be called Dzance's guardian.

When the veteran ranger finally retired in the "Gem of the North", Dzance gifted his namesake shield to the young knight of the city, Belpir, before 1357 DR.

## Dzalmus

Dzalmus, also known as dzalmaus dragons, were three-headed dragons native to the Hordelands. Their cruel hunting habits and voracious appetite made them the bane of barbarians living in the region.

Description
Dzalmus had flatter and longer bodies than other dragons, from atop which their three long necks smoothly jutted out. They had dun-colored, yellow to sandy brown scales. Their small wings were thin and leathery and their tails were wedge-shaped.

Personality
Dzalmus only sought to establish lairs at an older age. They preferred to spend their youth roaming and hunting, and looked for lairs in areas with abundant food sources.

Combat
Experts at hiding among tall grass, dzalmus preferred ambushing their foes. When fighting, they usually started with surprise attacks, engaging as many simultaneous opponents as possible. Although their small wings were not powerful enough to use for attack, their three heads provided a significant advantage. Their breath weapon was a cloud of life-draining gas that only one head could breathe at any given time.

Society
Dzalmus tended to live solitary lives and did not typically form families to raise their young, rather immediately abandoning them in the nest. Nevertheless, they met frequently for mating, a habit that ensured a higher reproductive rate than other dragons.

## Dzurdan Crommarch

Dzurdan Crommarch was a cleric of Tymora at the Gate of Good Fortune temple in Mulmaster in the Moonsea in the mid–14 century DR.

Activities
Every two tendays or so, Dzurdan rode south to the village of Maskyr's Eye, accompanied by two lay followers and four men-at-arms, to collect offerings left at the shrine of Tymora there from the local priest Khonduil Ammargath. If he learned that anyone had desecrated the shrine, stolen the offerings, or hurt the old priest, then Dzurdan would grimly search for those responsible.

## Dzaan

Dzaan was a former Red Wizard of Thay and later member of the Arcane Brotherhood of Luskan in the late 15 century DR.

History
As a Red Wizard of Thay, he studied the arcane art of illusion but later moved west to the Sword Coast North, where he joined the Arcane Brotherhood. They accepted him with open arms, being eager to gain a Red Wizard among their ranks. Dzaan could have been a great asset to the Brotherhood. However, he went north to Icewind Dale where he was seemingly burned to death by the people of Easthaven after slaughtering an adventuring party. Despite his apparent death, Dzaan had cast the mighty simulacrum spell, and had kept a copy of himself at a lost Netherese spire within the Dale. This construct stayed with Dzaan's previous bodyguard, Krintaas, a wight that has accompanied him from Thay.

Adventurers would eventually find this Simulacrum and potentially assist it come to life and become real with a lost piece of Netherese technology.

Personality

Abilities
Dzaan was capable of casting many arcane spells.

Possessions
He had a scroll of fireball in a copper tube and a gray bag of tricks.

Activities
Relationships
Although their relationship was minor, Dzaan trusted his Wight bodyguard Krintaas to protect his vital Simulacrum. Having brought him from Thay, the Wight was his only real ally in the Dale, and seemingly possessed a significant amount of loyalty to his master beyond his enslavement as a Wight, as he was determined to do whatever it took to ensure the Simulacrum of Dzaan obtained a life-spark upon learning of his master's demise.

## D'Zani outpost

The D'Zani outpost was a fortification of Sschindylryn in the Underdark beneath Cormyr.

Location
The outpost was found in a cavern accessed by portals set up by the Sschindylrynyr. One set of portals eventually led to the Demonweb Pits, another to Sschindylryn, and another up to the Eveningstar Cavern at the surface.

History
During the Sschindylryn invasion of Eveningstar in the mid-1480s DR, the outpost was used by drow forces as a midpoint on the march from Sschindylryn up to Eveningstar, and overseen by Master Wizard Tsabyrr. Although they made use of many portals to move around, there were still lengthy sections of regular marching through the Underdark along the route. When Eberronian heroes arrived through the Demonweb after encountering Lolth at the Spinner's Prison, they followed the drow forces up through the outpost and attacked them from the rear. Despite the Master Wizard and his garrison, the drow were unable to stop the lost but deadly Eberronians.
