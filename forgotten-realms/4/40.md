## -408 DR

Karsus discovers heavy magic, a honey-colored form of solid magic. In one of his first experiments with heavy magic, Karsus nearly causes his enclave to fall. He removes the heavy magic he created, pushing it over the side of the enclave. The heavy magic falls to the ground, where it slays the renegade arcanist Wulgreth, turning him into a lich when the heavy magic absorbs his life energies.

 Nayloss Talanthil, the fourth Sorcerer–Arkhan of the Talanthil Dynasty in Raumathar, dies.

## 409 DR

__NOTOC__

 The largest and longest battles between the Shoon Imperium and the beholders of the Lake of Steam since their invasion of Calimshan begin. These "Eye Battles" (as they come to be known) last for 21 years and are largely inconclusive until the great warrior Amahl Shoon VII sweeps through the region on his way to claim the throne from his grandmother's treacherous advisors. No beholder or beholder cultist openly controls any of the Arnaden cities when Amahl VII leaves.

## 400 DR

The Dark Alliance of humanoid races marches out of Thar and assaults Northkeep. More than 40,000 priests, mages, and shamans of the alliance perform a dark ritual that causes the city to sink beneath the Dragons' Sea. The allied forces also sack the nearby settlement of Phlan.
 In the lands that would become Sembia, the cities of Chondathan and Chauncelgaunt are attacked by orc and goblin raiders. The battles continue for fifteen more years.

 Chessentans cross the Wizards' Reach and found the city of Escalant.

 Kemble Cymrych, future High King of the Moonshae Isles, is born to Carrig Cymrych II.

 Circa: A large meteorite falls to earth near the Vale of Ixtzul in Far Payit. The impact is felt all over Maztica. The meteorite is in fact an enormous egg that hatches H'Calos the Star Worm. The beast begins to feed.
 Circa: The Golden Age of the Payit begins, lasting six hundred years. It is a time of peace and learning, with the sciences of astronomy, medicine, writing, and water-collection flourishing.
