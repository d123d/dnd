## -4400 DR

The Return of the Dark occurs in central Faerûn, when a combined force of duergar and drow raid and pillage the Elven Court and the dwarven realm of Sarphil. The Dark Court Slaughter slays many elven clan leaders, including the coronals Jhyrennstar and Rystall Wood. The Elven Court was pillaged and deserted. Sarphil was divided in two to be ruled by the invading forces.

 Following the deaths of his family at the hands of drow, Shevarash vows to Corellon to become the Seldarine's weapon and kill Lolth and all the drow.

 The deaths of their coronals see the ends of the Seventh and Fifth Rysars of Rystall Wood and Jhyrennstar, respectively. The wizardess Enajharas becomes coronal in Rystall Wood, marking the Eighth Rysar, and Oacenth, prophet of Labelas Enoreth, becomes coronal in Jhyrennstar, marking the Sixth Rysar.
 Elven leaders call for war against the drow, now that they have learned the nature of their enemy.

## 449 DR

Silvyr Ithal marches to Ithmong to claim the throne of Tethyr as its rightful king. This triggers rebellions against the Shoon Imperium in both Tethyr and Amn, and the Tethyrian War of Independence, but Silvyr is slain in battle by Qysar Amahl Shoon VII.

 Eleint 8: King Priam Tornamn abdicates the throne of Tethyr in favor of Silvyr Ithal.

 The son of Arun completes his education under Mentor Wintercloak in Myth Drannor. He leaves the city with the stated intention of exploring and learning about the wider world, and in turn teaching the magics of Myth Drannor. He also aims to become one of the first Harpers beyond Cormanthyr and the eastern lands, and to prove his value to his father's clan, Maerdrym. He adopts the name Arun's Son, the only name he can be proud of, and vows to claim a place in House Maerdrym when he's earned a new name.
