## 438 DR

Following the revolt of a number towns along the River Shaar and Lake Lhespen, Qysar Amahl Shoon VII of the Shoon Imperium orders seventeen troop ships to the area. Over two years, a series of battles are fought between the rebelling towns and the merciless Qayadin (general) Hakam yn Sarak el Sallah. The qayadin's troops rampage through Channathgate, Kormul, Lhesper, Rethmar, Sebben, Sheirlantar, and Sheirtalar in a campaign called the Seven Burnings. A large number of Shaarans are slain as the Imperium takes back control of the western Shaar and makes bloody reprisals.

## -4366 DR

Imaskari artificers open a pair of gates to another world, from which the Imaskari take thousands of human slaves. The artificers then close the gates and create a planar barrier that blocks all contact between the slaves and their deities on that world.
 The Imaskari begin marrying or producing children with these slaves, and gradually produce the Mulan people.
