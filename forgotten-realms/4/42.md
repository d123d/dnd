 Baerauble Etharr, High Wizard of Cormyr, dies aged 545.

## 427 DR

Qysara Shaani, Empress of the Shoon Empire dies.

 Shaani's seventh grandson, Amahl Shoon VII takes the throne as emperor of the Shoon Imperium.

 Qysar Amahl VII orders all wizards within his realm murdered. More than 38,000 suspected arcane spellcasters are killed over the next year and every magical institution is burned down. Some 44,000 more people flee north or east to escape the purges and no mage is safe within the Imperium until its end.
