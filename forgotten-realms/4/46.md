## 460 DR

A deadly virus is brought to Angalpuk in the Great Glacier by treasure hunters from Sossal. The disease decimates the Angulutiuns and caribou populations, and leads the locals to shun the area around the Ahtahqugotak Sea for centuries to come.

 The red dragon Hoondarrh is born somewhere in the eastern Sword Coast North.

 Amin, the former Western Emirate of the Shoon Imperium, establishes itself as its own country, called Amn, under King Esmel Torlath, forsaking Shoonite imperial rule and beginning the Torlath dynasty. It comprises the three major city-states of Athkatla, Crimmor, and Murann.

 Demron is commission to forge another Baneblade for the Spell-Major to replace the Ary'Faern'Kerym elfblade, which was lost a decade previously by Zaos Durothil.

## -460 DR

The wall of fog spell is invented by Netherese arcanist Veridon with the name Veridon's wall.

 The archwizard Serreg concludes his research into the life-draining blight following the floating city of Delia, resulting in a confrontation with a group of phaerimm. Though nearly killed, he is aided by Targus, who gives him an enchanted dagger that ultimately lets him escape through a series of uncontrolled polymorphs. Eventually, Serreg is transformed into a black dragon and, rather than return to Netheril, decides to continue his existence in this new form.

 Horlarth Talanthil, the third Sorcerer–Arkhan of the Talanthil Dynasty in Raumathar, dies.
