## PJ Stubbs

PJ Stubbs was sailor and a friend of Bosun Gannet of the Blushing Mermaid tavern in Baldur's Gate in the late 15 century DR.

History
In the, during the Absolute crisis, PJ Stubbs sailed to the nation of Calimshan, south of Baldur's Gate. There, he visited a popular local watering hole – The Copper Tap, the establishment that gave him the worst night of his life, worse than an incident with a bear in Chult. The Copper Tap was known for its own award-winning brews, yet when PJ tried one, he described the taste as "flowers having a wank on his tongue." Even after drinking ten pints of ale did not clear the taste out. The sailor told of the misadventure in a letter addressed to Bosun Gannet and asked the barkeep to prepare a couple of barrels of drinks for PJ's next visit that was sure to wash the flower taste out at last.
