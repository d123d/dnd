## C. Balderk

C. Balderk was an inhabitant of Baldur's Gate who died some years before the late 15 century DR.

History
Balderk died at sea, sometime before the, and was buried in the Lower City Graveyard, next to the Durinbold Mausoleum. The gravestone was etched with a single inscription: "C Balderk. Taken by Umberlee's rage. Recovered by Her grace." Balderk's grave may had been accidentally desecrated, and the body risen as a zombie by Nina Dortmell, a talented child with the gift, who was trying to resurrect her recently deceased brother.
