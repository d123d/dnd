## -7684 DR

Unable to destroy the rebel city of Ajhuutal and witnessing the growing envy of his own people at the freedom Ajhuu's rebels enjoy, Calim rashly sacrifices his dao allies and earth magic artifacts to destroy Ajhutaal with a greatly enhanced earthquake spell. The spell's effect, later referred to as the Shattering, causes the city to crumble and fall into the sea. Unfortunately, it also destroys all the land in a thirty-mile radius around the epicenter, creating the Spider Swamp.
