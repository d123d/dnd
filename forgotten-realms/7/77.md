## -7700 DR

Circa: Marid renegades of the Calim Empire move to Selmalyr, triggering conflict with the sea elves. The marids and their jann later move to Serôs, where they make alliances and share power with neighboring aquatic races. They give rise to the Marid States. Located deep underwater, they face no further retribution with the sea elves.

 The Second Rysar of Uvaeren begins.

## 77 DR

Crown Prince Baranth of Impiltur and his allies, the moon elves of Vedrymmell, defeat the "Usurper King" Morlorn at the Battle of Ilithra's Smile. Morlon's mercenary supporters, serving the Obarskyr family, are driven from the kingdom.
