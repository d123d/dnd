## 747 DR

A Chancelgauntian ship-owner named Orlephar Flostren begins a large-scale development project in the trading camp at the mouth of the River Tesh on the Moonsea. It is renamed Flostren's Hold. It eventually grows into the city of Zhentil Keep.
 The elves of Shilmista and King Strohm III of Tethyr forge an alliance against the Ring of Eyes in Cortryn.
