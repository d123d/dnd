## 712 DR

The Weeping War between Cormanthyr and the Army of Darkness rages on, with four major campaigns fought this year alone. Many heroes and Harpers are slain, and Myth Drannor is nearly overrun by the Army of Darkness.
 During the Battle of Silversgate, in the midst of battling the mezzoloth force led by Cvor, Nameless Chosen is gravely wounded. The Silversgate Portal is created and transports him to Silverymoon Pass. Ecamane Truesilver and mage allies from Silverymoon wipe out the surviving mezzoloths then take the Nameless Chosen to Silverymoon for healing.
 The good red dragon Garnetallisar recklessly assaults the Army of Darkness in the Battle of Garnet's Pyre. Although he divides the main force, traps it behind forest fires, slows their advance, and breaks their supply lines, Garnet goes missing during the battle and is never seen again.

 Ecamane Truesilver destroys the Silversgate Portal to stop any more fiends of the Army of Darkness from traveling to Silverymoon Pass.

 Ecamane Truesilver, already old and frail, dies of the strain of destroying the portal.
 Artox Frayhammer dies in Myth Dranor.

 Ecamane's great-nephew and top apprentice Aglanthol the Red succeeds him as High Mage of Silverymoon.
 The elves of Cormanthyr and the the drow of House Dhuurniv declare the Spiders' Truce while they battle the Army of Darkness.

 Vehlarr gains the help of the deity Sehanine to begin construction on the Circle of Vehlarr, a magical portal that will allow the werewolves of the city of Vehlarr to attack Myth Lharast. The Circle will not be used for another eight years, in 720 DR.
 Milsor the Valjevo commissions Valjevo Castle for his palace. It is completed in 730 DR.

## 710 DR

After drow attacks in Cormyr, members of three noble families are believed to be dead. Most of them are actually kept as slaves in the Underdark.
 Iyachtu Xvim emerges from a portal to the Abyss above the city of Westgate and, backed by a large army of tiefling warriors and demons, seizes the Westgate throne.

 The summoning of the Army of Darkness begins north of the Dragon Sea. By the end of the year, they begin their march on Myth Drannor.

 Thartryn Ilistar I, the King of Westgate, dies.

## 713 DR

Symrustar Auglamyr, Chosen of Mystra, is believed slain in the Weeping War.
 Aolis Ildacer is slain by drow.

 The Twisted Tower, in what will become Shadowdale, is again conquered by the drow, leaving the lands in western Cormanthyr under the control of the dark elves as the Lands Under Shadow are refounded.
 Forces of the Army of Darkness are defeated in battle near the Elven Court The elves reclaim the area.
 Three major campaigns are fought in the forests of Cormanthor. The elves manage to slay two commanders of the Army of Darkness, Malimshaer and Gaulguth. In the resulting brief peace, between Greengrass and Midsummer Night, many flee Myth Drannor to cities outside of Cormanthor, including Semberholme and Tangled Vale.

 The defeat of the Army of Darkness forces near the Elven Court and the death of Aolis Ildacer bring an end to the tentative Spiders' Truce between the elves of Cormanthyr and the drow of House Dhuurniv.

 Paladin Sarshel Elethlim, future king of Impiltur and leader of the Triad Crusade, is seen carrying Dornavver into battle against the demon hordes at the Battle of Snowsblood Trail and the Battle of Standing Stone's Blood.

## 711 DR

The Weeping War begins as the Army of Darkness invades northern Cormanthyr on the Feast of the Moon. They battle many elf patrols and destroy a number of elven villages and clan enclaves, committing the Northern Massacres.
 In late autumn, the Army of Darkness seizes mining and trading camps on western shore of the Moonsea (the site of latter-day Zhentil Keep).

 Reacting to the Army of Darkness, senior members of the Ruling Council of Cormanthyr request Amara and Aolis Ildacer form a group of covert agents and spies called the N'Vaelahr, or "Shadow Soldiers".

## 714 DR

Drow from the city of Maerimydra conquer what is to be known as Shadowdale.
 The Artblade is lost at the Battle of Stars Shining.
 In the Battle of Two Gates' Fall, the Warrior's Gate portal in Myth Drannor is destroyed. The blast emerges from the other end of the portal, devastating the city of Delimbiyran and large swathes of southern Delimbiyr.
 The last campaigns of the Weeping War see Cormanthyr's army and cavalry wiped out by the Army of Darkness.
 Kythorn 21: The elven city of Myth Drannor is besieged by the invading Army of Darkness, in the Siege of Shadow.
 The Weeping War peaks with Banes' Duel between Aulmpiter, the nycaloth leader of the Army of Darkness, and Captain Fflar Starbrow Melruth, commander of the Cormanthyr's defenders. The epic duel between both leaders lasts two whole days, culminating in a battle to the death on Flamerule 15. In the end, a magical blast slays both of them and both armies are left leaderless.
 On Flamerule 15, following the Banes' Duel, the leaderless horde of the Army of Darkness engulfs Myth Drannor, overwhelming the defenders with basic numbers and brutality. In the Final Flight, barely 200 elves and allies out of 3000 defenders escape the Fall of Myth Drannor.

 Many refugees from Myth Drannor emigrate to the city of Ascalhorn.

 The elves re-establish the Elven Court as the capital of Cormanthyr while Myth Drannor is besieged. Following the fall of Myth Drannor, Elven Court remains as the capital, and non-elves are forbidden to enter.
 The surviving splinter kingdoms of Delimbiyr decline following the damage done by the broken portal.

 The last thirteen living High Mages of Cormanthyr give their lives to create a mythal around the Elven Court, and forging the legendary Highfire Crown.

 Kythorn 25: Josidiah Starym is killed defending the Speculum in Myth Drannor.
 Flamerule 15: Fflar Starbrow Melruth dies in the Banes' Duel at the climax of the battle at Myth Drannor.
 Flamerule 15: Aulmpiter, the nycaloth commander of the daemonic Army of Darkness, dies in battle with Fflar.

## 717 DR

Reinhar VIII of Dambrath is born.

 The Windlass, a conclave of wizards that rule Turmish, send their armies to battle the druids of Silvanus (later known as the Emerald Enclave) after attacking their sanctuary of Cedarspoke. The druids defeat the Turmish army, marking themselves as a significant force in the Vilhon Reach.

 After staying out of the Tenth Serôs War, apart from defending innocents and upholding the Laws of Battle, the Dukars now steadily withdraw completely from all involvement in Serôs's politics. Their reasons are unknown. By the end of their year, no Dukar can be found, and their schools sit silent behind huge, impenetrable shields or vanish altogether. This marks the end of the Dukars.

 At dawn on Ches 1, the boy prophet Rhyester of Silverymoon miraculously receives his sight. That same year, he helps found the temple of Lathander's Dawn.
