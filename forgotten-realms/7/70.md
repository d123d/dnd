## 70 DR

Realising that simple men-at-arms wouldn't deal with all of the threats currently besetting Cormyr, Rhiiman the Glorious orders Baerauble Etharr to gather together all of Cormyr's wizards to deal with the attacks coming out of the King's Forest. Using only magic, the wizards destroyed several portals and eliminated the attacking monsters. These mages became known as the War Wizards, an organization that has lasted to the present day.

## 709 DR

Eleasias 4: An overambitious archmage attempts to gain the power of the Magister by attacking the incumbent along with a dozen clones of himself. The nine surviving clones then annihilate each other, and the original is murdered by his own apprentice. Another mage in turn kills him, only to then be killed by his own apprentice, marking the Night of Fourteen Magisters.
 The Tenth Serôs War erupts as over seven separate civil wars break out in Hmurrath. Almost every race in the Inner Sea is at war with with every other race.
 Yuan-ti use a portal to secretly abduct halflings from the southern Lluirwood. They spirited away to the Black Jungles to undergo experimentation.
 Three nycaloths freed the previous year from a prison over Cormanthyr, and their summoned yugoloth allies spend twenty-nine months assembling an army. In addition to lesser yugoloths, they gather bugbears, flinds, hobgoblins, gnolls, orcs, ogres, and trolls in the mountains and hills north of the Dragon Sea.

## 701 DR

## 708 DR

Cultists and monsters devoted to the fallen god Moander make a comeback near his fallen temple. They are routed and dispersed by three battalions of Akh'Faer mages as well as High Mages. From the ruins, senior priests recover many spells thought lost and restore them for use.

 Pendar Cymrych, future High King of the Moonshae Isles, is born to Alec Cymrych II.

 Saeval Ammath flies Garnetallisar, his adopted dragon, over Myth Drannor. This fulfils the condition of imprisonment of three nycaloths, namely a "red dragon that never held malice or greed in its heart [flying] over the coronal's throne." The dimensional pocket prison, invisible and located over Cormanthyr, is sufficiently weakened for the nycaloths to escape.
