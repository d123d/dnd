## 786 DR

In Impiltur, the great-grandsons of King Sarshel Elethlim confront Ndulu and his demon army in Moaning Gorge in the Earthspur Mountains, outside the gates of the Citadel of Conjurers. In the Battle of Moaning Gorge, the Paladin Princes wield the sword Demonbane and the shields Kimeltaar, Naelotaar, and Dizeltaar and together open a vortex portal to force the demons back into the Abyss. However, Crown Prince Essys and Prince Araln fall in battle.

 The surviving and youngest Paladin Prince, Nord, is declared crown prince and heir to Impiltur.
