## 734 DR

Iyachtu Xvim's tyrannical rule of Westgate is ended when he is driven out by Farnath Ilistar, the rightful heir to the throne, at the head of a mercenary army.

 Vhaeraun warns the drow of Chaulssin of an impending attack, and they retreat into the Plane of Shadow. A few days later, drow from Menzoberranzan invade the city but find it empty of people. Amidst frequent attacks by monsters from the Shadow Plane, the Menzoberranzanyr drow claim victory anyway and leave. The Chaulssinyr, meanwhile establish Chaul'mur'ssin, the Shadow City, on the Shadow Plane itself.

 Raulbaera, the so-called "Maiden King" and descendant of Ulbaerag Bloodhand, lays claims to the lands around what is later the village of Amphail. There she founds the settlement of Rowan Hold.

 The Greatsword of Impiltur, forged by the dwarves of Earthfast to mark the coronation of King Sarshel Elethim of Impiltur, is completed.

## 731 DR

Sarshel Elethlim's paladin army defeats the demonic army of the balor Ndulu at the Citadel of Conjurers. Sarshel breaks the Crown of Narfell with the blade Dornavver. This ends the Fiend Wars and the Triad Crusade.
 King Strohm II of Tethyr repels an invasion by Lower Tathtar.
 Cortryn's ruling house is slain and its territories conquered by the Ring of Eyes, a cabal of beholders and beholder cultists originating from the Lake of Steam. The elves of Shilmista begin battling the Ring of Eyes without pause.

 High King Ernest Cymrych of the Moonshae Isles dies of natural causes.

 Carthus Cymrych becomes High King of the Moonshae Isles, but abdicates the same year.
 Alec Cymrych II becomes High King of the Moonshae Isles.
 The fallen duchy of Elestam officially becomes a part of Tethyr.

 Unthalass in Unther is inundated by the Second Great Flood of the River Alamber.

 Gilgeam, god-king of Unther, lays claim to the ancient mountain stronghold of the slain brown dragon Vulpomyscan and names it the Citadel of Black Ash. As historical documents and records in Unthalass have been destroyed by flood, Gilgeam tasks a group of scribes to take the ruined books and scrolls to the Citadel, restore them, and create an eternal archive of his achievements.
