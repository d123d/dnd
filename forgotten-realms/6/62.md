## 629 DR

An assassination attempt on King Draxius Obarskyr of Cormyr by Luthax, senior member of the War Wizards, and Elmariel Emmarask is foiled by Thanderahast and Amedahast, at the cost of Amedahast's life. Thanderahast, her apprentice, takes her position as High Mage.

 Amedahast dies in a magical explosion when she detonates her own staff, killing many others in the brotherhood of the War Wizards, although the fate of Luthax is not clear.

## 621 DR

Princess Bridget of Callidyrr and her entourage disappear while traveling to Norland. Due to marry Prince Scothgar of Rogarsheim in a political union, Bridget's disappearance sours diplomatic efforts between the Ffolk and the Northlanders.

 Ffolk from the Moonshae Isles arrive in the Utter East. They begin to subjugate the native Mar tribes and colonize the land.

 Led by Chief Umggok, the Granitefang tribe of orcs founds a village in the Moonwood.

## -620 DR

Following sixty  years of negotiations, the syl-pasha of Calimshan and the Matron Mother of Guallidurth come to an agreement: secret trade activities in exchange for a withdrawal of drow forces beneath Calimport and other cities of Calimshan. However, as not all the drow hold allegiance to  the Matron Mother of Guallidurth, the withdrawal takes ninety years. This is also hampered by a house rebellion in Guallidurth, which continues the Night Wars.
 The first Emperor Chin of the Ho Dynasty of Shou Lung and his son both die in mysterious circumstances. The emperor's grandson is named the second Emperor Chin.
 The new Emperor Chin ends the Time of Contentions and declares the Time of Sharing Meals, which encourages religious freedom while banned temple militias. The Path of Enlightenment is chosen as the state religion of Shou Lung. These edicts are less effective in the southern provinces.
