## 679 DR

An army of monsters bred by a deepspawn emerges from the Beast Marches and nearly destroys Hillsafar. Cormanthyr sends the Akh'Velahr to reinforce Hillsafar, and they drive the monsters off by winter. They leave a garrison to defend Hillsafar and the northern forests and settlements until Myth Drannor falls in 714 DR.

 High King Tobert Cymrych of the Moonshae Isles dies in battle.

 Ernest Cymrych becomes High King of the Moonshae Isles.
 After a long and expensive campaign, Unther formally recognizes the League of Samathar, a group of cities on the Wizard's Reach that had seceded from the empire. Unther cannot recover from the campaign and the Second Untheric Empire ends.

## 670 DR

Southern barbarians invade Shou Lung and overrun Kahgang Su and the We'peng garrison. The clan militias and mercenary forces that gather to resist them outnumber those of the Emperor for the first time in Shou Lung's history. However, the invasion is suddenly abandoned and the barbarians depart, perhaps for the death or disappearance of their leader. Nevertheless, the local military forces stay in place.

 Following an incursion of drow in north-west Cormanthor, Spell-Major Paeris Haladar enters the Vault of Ages in Myth Drannor, the first time years that anyone has stepped foot inside. But Paeris finds the vault empty, all the great and ancient treasures of Cormanthyr missing.

## 671 DR

During the construction of an ivory tower in Thar, Abarat the Alabaster contacts his former colleagues and begs for their assistance against a siege by a monstrous army. Help arrives in a matter of hours, but rescuers find the tower entirely empty, with Abarat and all the monsters gone, no sign of a battle or anything out of the ordinary. Abarat is never heard of again, while the incomplete tower becomes known as Abarat's Folly.

 The gnolls of the Shaar are rent by internal strife. Their raids into the Lapal League dramatically decline.

## 673 DR

King Bellodar III of Impiltur dies of natural causes.

 The mages Aganazzar, Grimwald, Ilyykur, and Presper found the Covenant, an alliance of mages with the aim of promoting peace among the human nations of the North and preparing them for future battles with the orcs.
 Elves, half-elves, and humans build the town of Hillsafar (named for the Hillsafar dwarf clan) on the western shore of Tailings Bay. Their main aim is to trade with the dwarves of the Scarp, but some of the founders seek to keep the area free of drow and to watch over Moander's Crypt. Many less established crafts-folk of Myth Drannor migrate to Hillsafar seeking opportunity and work.
 Following the death of his father, Morus becomes the new king of Impiltur.

## 675 DR

After descending into madness, King Morus of Impiltur accuses his brother, Alathos I of attempting to seize the Golden Throne. The two duel one another in a sword-fight and Alathos slays his brother. He is crowned as the new king of the Realm of the Sword and the Wand.

 The noble House Ulondarr scandalizes Myth Drannor by openly accepting half-elves in the line of succession to lead the house. For half a century, Ulondarr is censured and harassed by the other houses, some of whom send mercenaries and assassins against them.

## 676 DR

Taeral Olortynaal returns to the Incanistaeum in Myth Drannor and rejoins the Seven Wizards of Myth Drannor. However, a politically motivated spell-battle breaks out between several noble elves and humans, and Taeral and two human students are accidentally killed by gold elf students. These students frame the only surviving non-elf, Arlesn of Arabel. Although innocent, Arlesn is exiled from Myth Drannor and the students are never charged.

 Dogmen of the Shaar, a book written by Dhynthar of Kormul, is published.

## 677 DR

Hym Kraaven, a senior member of the Seven Wizards of Myth Drannor, discovers the truth of Taeral Olortynaal's death in a spell-battle the previous year. He is soon killed in a supposed wild tavern brawl by agents and allies of the ambitious young mages of House Faerondarl.

 The Wizards' Reach rebels against Unther, so Gilgeam sends a naval armada to quell them. However, the fleet is wracked by a huge storm and returns without result.
