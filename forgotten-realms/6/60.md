## 606 DR

Reinhar V of Dambrath is born.

 Assisted by Lord Samar and forces of Nimbral, the people of western Thindol defeat the yuan-ti and force them out of the Sanrach Basin. To protect the western Thindolese from further serpentfolk infiltration, Samar conceals the roads and mountain passes to the west in illusions.

 Separated from eastern Thindol, the western Thindolese name their land Samarach after their savior and treat themselves as a vassal state of Nimbral. The eastern Thindolese remain as the realm of Thindol, governed by a council formed by each city.
 Coramshan is founded.
 The ranger Tara "Two-Blades" Oakfeller returns to Silverymoon to find her entire family dead. She discovers that Mayor Torus has been sacrificing all of his political and financial rivals to evil deities. Tara kills Torus and takes his place as mayor.

## 605 DR

The people of Thindol discover their nation is rife with yuan-ti infiltrators, from House Sseradess and House Sauringar. Thindol falls into anarchy as the Thindolese spend months searching out and killing serpentfolk.

 Many frightened people of western Thindol decide to flee, sailing a fleet of ships to the isle of Nimbral. However, a storm wrecks many of their vessels on the Beacon Rocks. Lord Samar of Nimbral, sympathetic to their plight and their pleas, returns the refugees to Thindol and agrees to battle the yuan-ti on their behalf.

 In and around Silverymoon, over a dozen people die in suspicious circumstances, including the former mayor Bynan "Son-Axe" Oakfeller and his family whose home is burnt down.

## 600 DR

The four cities of Minsorran on the border of Amn are struck by a terrific blizzard. Trapped by snow for over a month, the entire population fall preys to the white dragon Icehauptannarthanyx. As the snow clears, the cities are found empty and believed to be deserted.

 A group of humans, that included the wizard Tharilim of Calimport, die on an expedition in the Spine of the World when the red dragon Hoondarrh devours them.

 On a hunting trip in the Gnollwatch Mountains, King Reinhar III of Dambrath is killed by giants, along with his two sons. A short-lived war of succession ensues, but Reinhar's nephew is crowned king, becoming Reinhar IV.

 Circa: In Maztica, human tribes leave the Vale of Ixtzul. The task of guarding the beast H'Calos falls to the Bacar, a species of giant, intelligent ants.

 Circa: Modern sailing ships make safe sea travel possible for the first time. Many Faerûnian nations begin to ply the Inner Sea and other seas, though most journeys are still made within sight of land.
