## 616 DR

Following the fall of Phalorm, its human kingdom, Delimbiyran, lays claim to all the former lands of Phalorm and renames itself the Kingdom of Man. Delimbiyran makes alliances with the gnomes and halflings of the region.

 A wizard called the Handweaver arrives in Westgate and dispels the illusions concealing the ruling gynosphinx Nessmara, revealed her to actually be a lamia. Lyonarth, the androsphinx ruler she seduced and deceived, battles Nessmara to the death of both. Farnath, an apprentice of the Handweaver, is declared king of Westgate, beginning Ilistar Dynasty.

 The shadow dragon Aurgloroasa, the "Sibilant Shade", moves from the lair of her mother, Shhuusshuru, to make her own lair in the southwestern Thunder Peaks. She would not intervene in the area for the next century.

 Construction is completed of the dwarven fortress city of Sarbreen, by the kingdom of Roldilar in the Vast.

 Circa: Selûne appears in the dreams of several assembled priests, informing them of the existence of a relic called the Moonweb, which was lost in the fall of Netheril. Two of these priests already have two of the four rings that make up the relic and together quest for the remaining two in secret.

 Lyonarth, the King of Westgate, dies.

## 619 DR

A horde of orcs attacks the elven empire of Cormanthyr and the Dalelands from the west, with Semberholme threatened with destruction. The combined forces of the elven armies and the Dalesman militia push back the invading forces at the Thunderpeak Mountains. The battle was commemorated in the ballad "Battlestars at Tilver's Gap".

 Mayor Tara "Two-Blades" Oakfeller of Silverymoon dies of a fever. Donal "the Wise Bear" Ethen, a popular local wizard replaces her.
 The dwarves abdicate the government of Khôltar, leaving a power vacuum. After many days of bloody conflict between prominent human families (the Onsruur), Embran Orntathtar leads the garthraun (police) to quell the violence and declare himself the first Belarkh (ruling lord).

## 615 DR

Responding to Uthtower's plea for help, the lich Iniarv uses powerful magic to raise the level of a portion of the Sea of Swords, which floods the kingdom of Uthtower, killing untold numbers of humanoids and creating the Mere of Dead Men when the waters recede.

 Iniarv's flood wipes out the armies of Uthtower, Phalorm, and the orc hordes besieging them. As the surviving orcs flee into the Sword Mountains, the elves of Phalorm retreat to Evermeet. Already wounded by orc attacks, the Tri-Crowned Kingdom of Phalorm falls.
 With the retreat of the elves of Phalorm, Ardeep is only nominally an elf realm.
 The lamia Nessmara takes the form of a gynosphinx and seduces Lyonarth, the "Winter Sphinx", ruler of Westgate. The two rule the city together.

## 614 DR

Ches: The Granitefang tribe of orcs begins several concerted raids on Silverymoon. The tuskers establish a camp in the hills to the east for ease of access.
 Already seriously harmed by the Everhorde, Phalorm is assaulted by two more orc hordes. The orcs sack Dolblunde. The dwarf king of Phalorm, Oskilar, is killed in battle with the second horde. After dealing with remnants of the Everhorde, Phalorm's northern armies move south to the kingdom's defense, but are forced to retreat into Uthtower.

 High King Embro Cymrych I of the Moonshae Isles dies following an accident.

 Embro Cymrych II becomes High King of the Moonshae Isles.
 Hlondeth once again gains its independence from Chondath. House Extaminos rules Hlondeth from behind the scenes.

## -6100 DR

The war between Calim and Memnon, known as the First Era of Skyfire, is ended when elves use High Magic to disembody both genie nobles. They bind the element essences of Calim to the sky and that of Memnon to the ground. The two continue to struggle against one another, forming the Calim Desert.

 The Marid States are attacked by krakens while many marids and jann mysteriously vanish or die. The majority of the disappearances and deaths occur in a single year, but it continues for another sixteen years.

 The Order of the Long Death is formed circa this year.

 The Marid States practically disappear. Conveniently, this removes any major rival to Aryselmalyr's domination of Serôs. The Dukars prove a moderating influence on aquatic elven rule.

## 611 DR

The Everhorde orcs pour out of the Spine of the World and rampage across the North, plunging the region into war. Yarlith is ruined, as is Illusk, though the Host Tower survives. Neverwinter is also spared, with the help of Palarandusk the Sun Dragon.

 On Evermeet, Amlaruil Moonflower is born.

 The members of the Pentad Retreat become sharn after the traitorous precept, the vampire wizard Palron Kaeth, used the orcs of the Everhorde to attack the hidden enclave of Miyeritar.

 The dwarf realm of Roldilar begins construction of Sarbreen.

## 612 DR

The Everhorde orcs are soundly defeated south of the site of future Triboar in the Battle of Firetears by the forces of Phalorm and allied powers of the North. The victory costs many lives, however, including the king of Ardeep, Lathlaeril "Leafspear".

 The spellbook The Hand of Helm goes missing at the Battle of Firetears. None know if its owner Helbrace Orthon gave it to someone else, hid it away, or simply lost it.
