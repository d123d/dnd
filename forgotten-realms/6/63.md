## 633 DR

After working for years, Demron finishes Keryvian, the most powerful of the Baneblades. Demron becomes extremely exhausted after completing the sword, and perishes two days later. Due to his great work in the city, House Ammath and others finance a grand temple to Azuth, who Demron worshiped.

 After a year-long siege, Iyraclea uses the Gatekeeper's Crystal and utterly destroys the city of Myth Ondath, including its mythal.

 A Talassan heretic named Tothur appears in the western Shaar, displaying the Chanting Chain, a holy relic of Talos. Orthodox Talassans attack him continuously for more than 200 years but his success, powers and extended lifespan seem to prove his piety over the church and a scattered cult forms around his teachings in the Shaar and the Tashalar.

## 632 DR

In Eleint, Iyraclea the Ice Queen sends an army commanded by the lich Vrandak the Burnished to lay siege to Myth Ondath, and raises great winter storms that lash the city throughout the year. This attack is called the Ice Queen's Winter.
 In the Moonshae Isles, King Stromm of Norland battles the Ffolk of Callidyrr.

 Thanks to her assault on Myth Ondath, Iyraclea's power becomes common knowledge and her claim to be greatest cleric of Auril is proven. Auril grants Iyraclea with new and more powerful cold-based magic.

 The Great Glacier, the home of Iyraclea, swells as a result of her frosty power.

 High King Embro Cymrych II of the Moonshae Isles dies in battle against King Stromm of Norland.

 Alec Cymrych I becomes High King of the Moonshae Isles.
