## 697 DR

Dark Mother Lalondra Worul succeeds in turning herself into a lich, killing all of the True Servants of Shar whose own lives are tied to her life force. With all but one of its members dead, the True Servants of Shar cease to exist. As the ranking members head all of the Church of Shar's operations along the Sword Coast, the church suddenly finds itself leaderless. Followers of Shar riot all along the Sword Coast.

 King Davyd of Delimbiyran is assassinated amidst the Sharran riots. There is no legitimate heir to take the throne, so the kingdom collapses into civil war. Several "kingdoms" break away, including Calador, Scathril, and Loravatha.

 Dark Mother Lalondra Worul descends into the Underdark hoping to found her own realm but, displeased with her recent actions, Shar strips the Dark Mother of her magic, leaving her near-powerless in caverns full of drow and alhoons. She doesn't survive long.

## -69 DR

A third wave of shalarin migration arrives in Serôs, for the first time finding it peaceful. In less then thirty years, they found two shalarin kingdoms, Es'daan and Es'purr.

## 694 DR

Darcassan, a sun elf diviner from Myth Drannor's Windsong Tower portends his first prophesies of doom. He relays his seeings to Windsong Tower's elders, who choose to keep the knowledge secret to avoid a public panic. However, the news is also hidden from the city's council as the seeds of distrust and factionalism begin to bear fruit.

 The mages of Windsong Tower and the Circle of Flames commence relocating magic items and arcane tomes to safe locations, among them the Scrolls Ardentym and the Keryfaertel. Over the next twelve years, most of these are given to trusted fellow mages in Silverymoon and Ascalhorn, or to mages who've relocated themselves to these cities. However, some mages claim the treasures for themselves and flee to other lands.
