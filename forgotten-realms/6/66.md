## -665 DR

The Nether Mountains are wracked by a great earthquake. The city of Tzindylspar is destroyed.

 Persecuted monks and temple guards flee from the wrath of Shou Lung. They go southwest into the mountains over the next 55 years. Avoiding the local barbarian tribes and having to battle monsters, they eke out a meager existence in isolated mountain holdings and call themselves lamas. They are the precursors to the realm of Tabot.

 Darthen III, who would later become the tenth Arkhan of the Nhalass Dynasty in Raumathar, is born.

## -669 DR

The first Emperor Chin of the Ho Dynasty of Shou Lung declares the Organization of Thought, an effort to make the Path of Enlightenment the official religion of the empire. This triggers the Time of Contentions, in which rival faiths feud and struggle against one another, among the people and the bureaucracy. Finally, temple militias wage small civil wars.

## -664 DR

Drow assault the town of Keltar in Calimshan, covering it in darkness and nearly obliterating it with their magic. Over 37 hours, the drow pillage the city and carry off half of Keltar's people. The army of Calimport arrives to late to stop them, but liberates Keltar and drives the drow back into the Underdark. Three thousand people disappear into drow slavery.

 While being transported to Eileanar, the remaining Nether Scrolls are stolen.

## 662 DR

Rathdaen, a great mage and friend of many of Myth Drannor's inhabitants, dies in his sleep. His apprentice Narsel inherits the Tome of Rathdaen, and immediately journeys west, fearful of attacks by jealous rivals attempting to steal Rathdaen's legacy.
 King Strohm I, Elfking of Tethyr, dies.
 The heirs of Houses Bleth, Crownsilver and Truesilver are killed while adventuring in the hideaway called the Auantiver Labyrinth. The fashionable practice of young Cormyrian noble scions exploring the Labyrinth ends as a result.

 Strohm I's grandson, Strohm II takes the throne of Tethyr.

## 661 DR

A number of small ogre tribes from the Cloud Peaks leave to join their kin in the Small Teeth. They are led by the Horned Mage, a charismatic ogre mage.

 From the Small Teeth, the Horned Mage raises an army that sacks the towns of Imnescar and Trademeet in Amn. General Rashturl of Amn leads an army against them, halting the Horned Mage's advance. The Horned Mage disappears, his fate unknown.

 On the final day of the Midsummer festivals, Coronal Eltargrim Irithyl of Myth Drannor dies, leaving the leadership of the city open, and ushering in The Dusk. Eltargrim leaves the Ruler's elfblade, the Ar'Cor'Kerym, hovering about the Rule Tower. His niece and heir Aravae Irithyl declares a five-year-long period of mourning for the whole city. This marks end of the Sixth Rysar of Cormanthyr.

## 666 DR

The Mourning Days in Myth Drannor end with a Claiming Ceremony to choose the next coronal that will Cormanthyr. Chaos ensues, with many elves, humans and other people of Myth Drannor slain and the Rule Tower destroyed. Finally, the Srinshee claims the Crownblade and restores the Rule Tower, but vanishes with the elfblade.

## 669 DR

The noted elf archmage and planewalker Abarat the Alabaster resigns his position as head of the Six Tyryl Towers wizard school in Myth Drannor. He announces his intention to construct a new tower in the Beast Lands (later Thar). He does just that, fashioning a magically strengthened ivory tower to defend against the local tribes and link the local elf nations as a hub of commerce and travel.
