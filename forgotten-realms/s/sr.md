## Srivven

Srivven was a mage and, along with Iraun, one of the self-proclaimed kings of Adaerglast around 1374 DR.

They originally came from easternmost Murghôm after a battle broke out in their school of magic between students and their masters. Before arriving in Adaerglast, Srivven and Iraun traveled Faerûn plundering mages' tombs and seizing spells from living wizards. Srivven has no actual right to rule over Adaerglast, although he and Iraun believed that the gods had appointed them this task. They had resisted several attacks and, due to the strong magic in Adaerglast, had protected themselves by setting up many very powerful spellwebs.

## Srinshee's spell shift

Srinshee's spell shift was a mighty abjuration spell that let one manipulate a spell cast by another creature.
Effects
This spell caused the caster to be able to perceive the Weave which allowed the caster to swiftly alter others' spells. The version best known by the Fall of Myth Drannor allowed the caster to manipulate others' spells, returning them at their caster if they were within a  range, to reduce their area or range by up to three quarters, or to redirect them at a different target. This was only effective on arcane spells, though, and also created a  diameter sphere, blocking all spells but those who were at most one step less powerful than it was. The spell burned out after the caster used it to spellshift two of the most powerful spells, but stronger casters might be able to achieve slightly more than that.

By, the most common version allowed a mage to either counterspell or manipulate the spell being cast by another spellcaster.

History

It was developed by the Srinshee. Unknown to all but three of her students, Elminster Aumar, Mythanthar and Aravae Irithyl, there was a stronger version, which could only be wielded by those who had trascended the power of conventional magecraft. This version of the spell, recorded only in her own secret spellbooks, was capable of blocking all conventional spells from its inner barrier, and was not limited to countering or manipulating spells, being also capable of creating transient dead magic or wild magic bubbles, as well as aiding other spells, bolstering their power. This spell was considered a precursor to globe of invulnerability, but the existence of its superior version was seen as up for debate.

## Srossar

Srossar was a bronze dragon possessed by Tyranthraxus.

History
Srossar was an ancient dragon. Around 1240 DR, some agents of Tyranthraxus (who were in turn deceived) tricked Srossar into believing that bathing in the pool of radiance, a portal to an unknown dark plane underneath Valjevo Castle, would give him greater wisdom and understanding of the metaphysics of the universe.

However, in truth, when the dragon entered the pool, Srossar was possessed by Tyranthraxus. Tyranthraxus immediately commenced building an army of monsters for the subjugation of Faerûn.

Using Srossar's body, Tyranthraxus took control of most of the city of Phlan until 1340 DR.

In that year, an adventuring party hired by Councilman Porphyrys Cadorna defeated Tyranthraxus, killing Srossar in the process.

## Srandro Chree

Srandro Chree was a thug-for-hire operating in the city of Delzimmer in southeast Faerûn circa the Year of Wild Magic, 1372 DR. He had no qualms against killing people, as long as the price was right.

One of his victims was the shopkeeper that wouldn't stay dead, Antholo Kraul. After being killed gruesomely and miraculously returned to life, twice, one of Antholo's cousins hired Chree to assassinate him, hopefully permanently. Srandro Chree did his job late one night, but by the next evening, Kraul was seen drinking in the Reaching Hand tavern with friends as if nothing unusual had happened. The nagra (local police) never proved the cousin hired Chree, but caught her in the act of killing Kraul herself by tipping a heavy flower urn off a balcony onto his head (from which he again returned from the dead).

## Srinshee

The Srinshee (born Oluevaera Estelda) was once a baelnorn but was reborn as a mortal elf once more, either sun or moon. She aided three Cormanthyran Coronals: Miirphys, Tannivh, and Eltargrim and all three trusted her implicitly. She helped raise the Mythal over the elven city of Myth Drannor in 261 DR.

History

Oluevaera Estelda was once the matriarchal ruler of Clan Srinshee and lived in the elven city of Ylraphon before its destruction by drow and orc. From the fires of Ylraphon's fall she earned the title of savior of Yrlaphon's Survivors as she led them to safety in Cormanthor. As the last member of Clan Srinshee she took the title of The Srinshee.

The Srinshee played a vital role in the creation of the Mythal of Myth Drannor in 261 DR amid the ruins of the shattered court of the Coronal after she ended the madness of the Starym-led attack (which was an attempt to hinder the plans of Coronal Eltargrim). She led Mythanthor, Coronal Eltargrim Irithyl, Lady Ahrendue Echorn, Lord Earynspieir Ongluth, and another ten mages to create the magical ward.

## Srelanat Road

The Srelanat Road was a road in the Kuong Kingdom in Malatra. It was the main overland route to the Kunong River.Like many locations in the Kuong Kingdom, the Srelanat Road has not been labelled on any map of Malatra.

It started at the town of Srelanat on the mouth of the Ranu River and continued to at least the town of Denokanburi on the Kunong River.

In the mid–14 century DR, travelers preferred the Srelanat Road to the hazardous old roads through the Akashnu Mountains.

## Srelanat

Srelanat was an important trade town in the Kuong Kingdom in Malatra. It stood on the mouth of the Ranu River at the terminus of the Srelanat Road.

Economy
The town was home to the oldest and most productive emerald mines in southern Malatra. These mines were found on temple land, and thus they and the town were tightly controlled by the priesthood under Priest Kalivan circa 1357 DR.

With such riches, there was much crime and jinyan flourished here. The town was the center of a widespread gem smuggling network.

## Sreve

Sreve was a village of shepherds and root-farmers in the 9 and 10 century DR. It was the native village of the Magister Kurtal.

Geography
Geographical Features
Kurtal had a mill, a main street, and a ridge to the east where a mage's tower, originally belonging to Justal Iroon, stood. The mill had a millpond.

Flora & Fauna
Fish lived in the millpond. The villagers were mostly shepherders and root-farmers.

History
At some point prior to, Radellus came to Sreve to take Justal Iroon's tower, and after a fierce battle, he prevailed. Radellus was later defeated by Kurtal, who froze some of the rubble falling from the tower in time to serve as a warning to the villagers.

Sreve was destroyed during Kurtal's tenure as the Magister, due to the number of duels with his challengers that took the fighting there. The town had been reduced to nothing by the late 14 century DR.

Notable Locations
The tower of Justal Iroon was close to Sreve, and so was Kurtal's abode.

## S'repic

S'repic was a species of carnivorous and scavenging fish found in the Sleepy Lake of the Malatran Plateau in Kara-Tur.

Description
S'repic were large slender fish of huge shiny silver scales. Most specimens reached  in length.

Ecology
The fish were dangerous flesh-eating creatures that often schooled under the floating raft villages of the Nubari Zantira tribe, waiting for food to be dropped into the lake. Locals believed that S'repic not only ate scavenged human food, but also disobedient Zantira children.

S'repic were hunted by the Zantira spearfishers for their delicious meat and huge bright silver scales, used for decorations and accessories.

## Srubaash

King Srubaash was the goblin ruler of the Neidlig tribe in the mid-to-late 14 century DR.

Activities
Srubaash led his tribe with cunning and diplomacy, forming alliances with other communities and avoiding warfare whenever possible. His ultimate intent was to build a realm similar to Hlundadim, unifying goblins, orcs, and other humanoids. Thanks to his successes and belief in his grand destiny, he styled himself King, rather than Chieftain of his tribe.

History
Srubaash claimed to have been visited by a mysterious spirit that urged him to rebuild the ancient realm of Hlundadim. Following this experience, he began assembling an increasingly expansive multi-racial realm, making Neidlig the largest and most powerful force in the Goblin Marches by the 1360s DR.

## Sretsam

Sretsam was one of the countless worlds on the Prime Material plane. It was home to a powerful Magi member of the Order of the Eternals, Lady Morgandella the Mighty.

History
In the late 14 century DR, the Order of the Eternals, a trans-dimensional guild for magic-users of impressive power, elected to hold its 1,238 annual Symposium in the city of Ravens Bluff on the world of Toril, in order to secretly investigate its unusually high levels of magical energies and planar incursions. The Symposium's attendees included Magi Morgandella the Mighty. All the guests stayed at Glittering Star Inn for the multi-day event. During the Symposium, Lady Morgandella held the plenary lecture titled Toward an Understanding of the Mechanisms Leading to the Creation of the Draco-Lich.
