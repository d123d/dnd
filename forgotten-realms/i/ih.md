## Ihtafeer

Ihtafeer was a female rakshasa who posed as Adratha, the potion seller at Druid Grove, after killing the original. She was an enemy of the dao of Khan Zahraa.

Eventually, Gorion's Ward became involved with the dao, who asked him to find and kill Ihtafeer. With the help of the Shadow Thief of Amn Itona, Gorion's Ward unveiled Ithafeer's true identify and after fighting killed her. Later, Gorion's Ward took her head to the dao as proof of her death.

## Ihanvas

Ihanvas was a spirit naga in the Doomvault dungeon in 1486 DR.

History
Ihanvas was tasked by the Red Wizards of Thay with managing captured dragon turtles. She fulfilled her duty until 1486 DR when heroes from Daggerford fought and defeated her.

## I Harp as I See It

I Harp As I See It was a book written by the Harper Abranthar "Twoquills" Foraeren. It was published in 1365 DR.

History
As of 1372 DR, a copy could be found in the Leaves of Learning in Highmoon and the original rested in the shelves of Candlekeep.

I Harp As I See It referred to one of the Seven Sisters, Qilué Veladorn.

## Ihanora

Ihanora the Merciful was the chosen of Auril who led a crusade against others in the North in the name of her goddess during the late 11 and early 12 centuries DR.

Activities
Ihanora traveled alongside a savage band of frost giants and yetis, that fought alongside polar worms and even white dragons, striking fear into the hearts of mortals. They assaulted anyone that attempted to seek refuge from the bitter cold of the North or that sought to negate the harsh effects it caused.

History
In the, Ihanora crafted the Snow Maiden's Reaver,  a +2 bastard sword that could turn any being it struck into solid ice.

She was finally defeated nearly a decade later, in the, by the Red Mask Brigade led by the infamous Bitter Harley.

## Ihlazur

Ihlazur was a glabrezu demon of the Demonweb Pits.

History

During the Sschindylryn invasion of Eveningstar in the mid-1480s DR, Ihlazur emerged from a stray portal between the Demonweb Pits and the Underdark near Sschindylryn. He began fighting everything in sight in the hopes of a good challenge, and upon finally being bested happily dissolved back to his Abyssal home.
