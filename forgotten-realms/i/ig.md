## Ignan

Ignan was a language from the Elemental Plane of Fire.

Description
Ignan was a language full of sharp clicks and hisses.

Speakers
It was the tongue of many creatures of fire, including fire elementals; azers; efreet and jann; magmins; fire, magma, and steam mephits; and salamanders, as well as fire genasi.

It was considered a dialect of Primordial, so that creatures who spoke one of the other elemental dialects such as Aquan, Auran, or Terran could understand Ignan and vice-versa.

## Ignace Dethingeller

Ignace Dethingeller was a loremaster of Baldur's Gate who journeyed to Anchorome in the 1370s DR.

He communicated with Tethtoril, the First Reader of Candlekeep. In one letter, he reported he had journeyed north of Fort Flame to an unnamed island where the wreckage of a flying ship was discovered. From a journal that Dethingeller was able to recover, he learned that the ship had been captained by Eartharran Neirdre from Evermeet and that it had crashed after battle with a wing of dragons. The journal contained sketches Eartharran Neirdre had made of statues from a floating city that had been abandoned. These statues depicted a race that had both draconic and avian features which Neirdre speculated either belonged to a draconic race that had evolved these features or possibly a race that was a common ancestor to both the dragons and aearee. The city was said to have been protected by a wing of dragons.

## Ignazar

General Ignazar was a fire archon that long-served as an ally of the fire primordial Maegera.

Activities
Unable to concede defeat, Ignazar never stopped his endless war – he forewent conflicts on the plane of Elemental Chaos and brought forth a seemingly unending stream of elemental archons onto Toril to conquer the material world.

History
Tens of thousands of years before the Present Age, General Ignazar fought for Maegera during the Dawn War.

In the late 15 century DR, Ignazar aligned himself with the mad fire giant Gommoth, the self-styled King of Mount Hotenow and leader of the burgeoning cult of Maegera. Ignazar was granted use of the ancient dwarven stronghold of Fireguard Fortress to use as his own personal keep, to defend Gommoth and his followers as they took action to reawaken the slumbering primordial in the Prime Material plane.

## Igan Sokol

Igan Sokol was the quartermaster of Sokol Keep in Phlan in the late 15 century DR.

History
Igan was a scion of the mercantile House Sokol. He grew up in Sokol Keep and in time he became the quartermaster of the keep, which served as a lighthouse for ships entering Phlan. Harae, a ghost that haunted the keep, somehow befriended Igan. Under Harae's hidden suggestions, Igan wanted to restore the shrine to Tyr inside the keep. He also involved in the matter his friend Grim Thornbuck, the leader of the Knights of the Black Fist contingent on the isle.

Out of gratitude, in the, Harae directed Igan, Grim, and fellow Black Fists to a dungeon under the keep that contained treasure. However, when they arrived, Grim and his fellows were filled with greed and Grim killed Igan. This angered both Harae and the undead guardian of the dungeon, which had once been a temple to Dagon. Thus Grim and his fellows were trapped and besieged inside the room.

Igan was raised as a zombie to torment Grim until some adventurers hired to solve the mystery of the keep destroyed his mortal remains and sought justice for his homicide.

Personality
Igan was a naive individual. At first, he considered all the stories about ghosts and treasure as just a game. After he befriended0 Grim, he became more serious and in the end became obsessed with uncovering the story of the keep.

## Igi Tajima

Igi Tajima was the deputy governor of Miyama Province in Kozakura in 1357 DR.

History
Tajima hailed from Igi clan, originally a samurai clan that thanks to good marriage became a branch of the Tsu family. Normally Tajima had to handle the actual civil governing of the province while Tsu Gonsuke was busy with plots in the capital. However, most of his functions were usurped by the shugo-daimyo Niwa Hirotada and Tajima acted as a counselor for Hirotada. For his work, the Niwa head paid him a portion of the taxes from the province. Secretly, Tajima was gathering as many supporters and lands as he could.

Personality
Tajima knew well that his power was in constant decline and he had no possibility of opposing Hirotada. His plot was to gather many supporters and lands to one day declare himself an independent daimyo. This ambition had deeply corrupted Tajima. Outwardly, he was very pleasant and trustworthy. Inwardly, he would do anything to reach his goals.

## Ignidraco

The Ignidraco was a genus of prehistoric proto-dragons.

Ecology
Scholars from Candlekeep theorized that ignidracos had an evolved digestive tract that enabled them to regurgitate intestinal gas and ignite it with an enzymatic, hypergolic reaction in the back of their throats, making them capable of breathing out blasts of fire. As the ignidracos continued evolving, some of the species began to modify their digestive tracts, developing special gastric enzymes that allowed them to spit acid, exhale corrosive chemicals, and even a chemical substance able to absorb heat to the point of freezing the air and objects around it.

History
Scholars believed that ignidracos were an evolved strain of the eodraco genus. It was theorized that certain species of ignidracos developed the first stages of self-awareness and real intelligence, not just the improved animal cunning shown by earlier species of eodracos. A few scholars believed ignidracos began to develop the earliest manifestations of the draconic language, although there was not enough evidence to prove anything. It was also believed that, as ignidracos depended mostly on their intelligence for survival, the species began to develop longer life spans.

Scholars didn't have enough evidence to theorize why it happened, but eventually ignidracos evolved into two new genera: the ferrodracos and the inficedracos.

Detractors of the proto-dragon theory believed that the fact that some species of dragons were capable of breathing out kinds of elemental energy other than fire (such as the blue dragons ability to breathe out lightning) made the theory of the evolved digestive tract illogical, and that the theory that explained the white dragons ability to breathe out frost as some sort of chemical reaction capable of absorbing heat was just rubbish.

## Igidujin

The Igidujin were a tribe of humans living on the Plain of Horses in northwestern Kara-Tur or the northeastern Hordelands. They were among the tribes mistakenly called "Tuigan" by outsiders following the Horde Wars.

Home
They made their home around the city of Quaraqand, deep in the southern Chigidi Mountains.

Society
They were only a minor tribe of the Plain of Horses, but were known for their ferocity. They were ruled by the cruel and despotic Qaland family. Circa, their qaghan was Qaland T'akk, a man without mercy and bent on conquest.

Under the vicious law of the Qalands, thieves and protestors were executed, no trial necessary. However, the most common crime in Quaraqand was apparently treason, and resulted in the alleged traitor, their family, and even their livestock all being slain. Furthermore, Qaland T'akk forced those who served him to kill enemies according to weekly quotas. Failure to kill enough was of course punished by execution, by T'akk himself.

They spoke the Chuchian tongue.

Culture
The Igidujin had an animistic faith.

Their warriors favored clubs, both the war club known as chokhor modo and the throwing club called a sidam. Their commanders also employed hawks trained to attack targets. Their beaks and claws were both sharpened to a razor's edge.

Activities
From Quaraqand, the Igidujin often raided the surrounding villages, nomad camps, and caravans, especially those of the rival Fankiang tribe, whom T'akk hated. Ostensibly, most of these raids were to steal Steppe horses but in truth it was largely to sate their bloodlust. They massacred their victims, leaving no man, woman, or child alive.

History
Originally, they were a collection of simple mountain hsing (clans), only loosely assembled before they fell into deadly warring with one another. After almost a century of this, the pitiless Qaland Shurijah, united the clans and forged the Igidujin tribe. The cruel and despotic Qaland family reigned over the Igidujin ever since.

## Iggwilv

Iggwilv, also known as Tasha or Natasha,Iggwilv and Tasha were originally separate characters until they were retconned to be the same person in Expedition to the Ruins of Greyhawk in 2007. and the alias Zybilna, was an Oerthian archmage and demonologist. She famously wrote the Demonomicon of Iggwilv, a tome on demonic lore.

Later in life she created her own Domain of Delight in the Feywild, and ascended as one of the plane's powerful archfey.

Description

The exact appearance of Iggwilv was unknown to many. Some described her as a ravishing beauty, while others claimed she was a bald and hideous crone. In fact Iggwilv was quite beautiful in her youth with raven hair that eventually went stark white. Even after becoming an archfey, she still retained a human-like appearance.

Abilities
During her stay in the Abyss, Iggwilv became extremely knowledgeable about the political hierarchy and goings on between the various powers within. She was considered a master at summoning fiends, planar exploration, necromantic magic, and later in life, gained command over fey beings.

As an archfey, Iggwilv was immortal and could not die from aging.

Possessions

Iggwilv had a vast collection of magic items, oddities and personal mementos that she kept within the Palace of Heart's Desire in her home plane of Prismeer. Notable among these were a magical mirror that showed the individual gazing into it what they desired most in the reflection within; three jars of time, that displayed the visage of a being at three different points in their life, a magical chessboard gifted to her by the archmage Mordenkainen, and the vorpal greatsword Snicker-Snack.

One of Iggwilv's most wondrous and powerful items was her personal cauldron, an artifact that she crafted with the assistance of her adoptive mother. It held two different forms and many magical properties, including the power to permanently 'freeze time' for everyone within a  radius.

Her personal library held tomes that originated from across the planes. Notable titles included The City That Waits, I, Lyzandred, Kingdom of the Ghouls, The Many Masques of Mith, Nadir of Nessus, Prophecies of Explictica Defilus, and Thingizzard's Night Balloon.

On her person Iggwilv wore a robe of the archmagi and an amulet of the planes. During her time in the Abyss, she wielded a staff of power.

Activities
While Iggwilv was most famous for creation of her namesake Demonomicon, she also authored the unpublished tome, The Faceless Lord, a more focused study on the Demon Prince Juiblex, and Tasha's Cauldron of Everything.

Spells
Iggwilv was credited with a number of spells, most of which were described in the Demonomicon. Her spells included: Dolor; ensnarement; exaction; imbrue; implore; lover's call; minimus containment; Tasha's caustic brew; Tasha's hideous laughter, used circa the late 15 century DR in Waterdeep as a security measure against burglars; Tasha's mind whip; Tasha's otherworldly guise; and torment

Apart from spells developed by her, Iggwilv was credited the creation of Tasha's Atrocious Robe that was brought to Toril by planeswalker Mintassan the Magnificent. The enchanted robe was among the wizard's extensive collection of curios from across the multiverse, displayed in Mintassan's Mysteries in Westgate as of the .

Relationships

Iggwilv was considered one of the most powerful beings in the multiverse, on par with demon lords.

Iggwilv had many confusing relationships with the demon prince Graz'zt. They were both lovers and enemies, their relationship constantly changing. Both have at various points been the consort, prisoner, and master to the other. While they had almost no trust between them, they also held no secrets from each other. Both Iggwilv and Graz'zt sought to use or betray the other, yet also supported or even rescued one another when in peril, and in their own twisted way, both genuinely shared a bond of love with the other. Iggwilv believed Graz'zt was the only demon worthy of her gaze while she was the only consort of Graz'zt that inspired feelings of jealousy (as evidenced when he attacked Fraz-Urb'luu).

While held prisoner by Graz'zt in his plane of the abyss, Iggwilv bore him many children. Graz'zt quickly and securely placed them in various locations and protected their identities from any beings that could bring them harm.

The witch queen was an enemy of the Nine Hells, owing partially to her interest in Graz'zt. She had a quasit familiar named Iggrik. At one point she had four arcanaloth apprentices.

Iggwilv was the adopted daughter of Baba Yaga, the archfey sorceress known across the planes as the 'Mother of All Witches'. She had three adoptive sisters that together formed the Hourglass Coven, Bavlorna Blightstraw, Skabatha Nightshade, and Endelyn Moongrave.

For centuries, Iggwilv was aided by the dwarven clerk named Kalimanzaros, who remained unfailingly loyal throughout their many years together. During her time in the Feywild, Iggwilv garnered the aid of the high elf Raezil Uthemar, who spied on other fey courts on her behalf. As Zybilna, she was known to sponsor at least two warlocks, the amenable old warlock Madryck Roslof and the kenku Kettlesteam.

History

Iggwilv was born in the world of Oerth and was adopted by the witch Baba Yaga, at which time she was known as Natasha "the Dark". She became known later as Tasha, before adopting her alias of Iggwilv, although it was not clear whether any of those was her original name. It was at that early point that she was credited with inventing the Tasha's hideous laughter spell.

At one point she moved to Niflheim, the second layer of Hades.

Some time later Iggwilv settled in the Abyss and immersed herself in the study of demon-summoning and lore regarding demonic spirits. She sought to garner more power for herself, but made many enemies among the chaotic fiends and fled to worlds beyond for her safety. Eventually, Iggwilv moved to her own Domain of Delight, known as Prismeer, in the Feywild, where she ruled from the Palace of Heart's Desire in the guise of Zybilna. Over time she transformed into a fey being and refocused her studies on that newfound state of existence rather than demonology.

She was eventually betrayed by the three daughters of Baba Yaga—Skabatha, Bavlorna, and Endelyn—the hags of the Hourglass Coven, and their cronies in the League of Malevolence. who used the magic of her personal cauldron to freeze in time the Witch Queen and many of her palace's other inhabitants.

## Ignite flame

Ignite flame was a common evocation spell of the southern magic tradition.

Effects
The spell created a small, hot flame. The maximum size of the flame was 3 square-inch (7.6 square-centimeter) and could be cast in a 10 yd (9 m) radius from the caster. The spell would automatically destroy certain flammable materials, such as paper. A creature targeted by the spell could smother the fire before the flame spread. The fire produced by the spell was not magical.

Components
The spell required only verbal and somatic components.

## Igaleus

Igaleus was one of the elven slaves captured by Chieftain Saslick's lizardfolk in 1372 DR.

Description
Igaleus was a large man. After capture, his lower jaw was striped with long red scars, reminiscent of trauma left by a spiked mace. The fresh and raw torn flesh attracted ravenous swamp flies.

History
In the early fall of 1372 DR, Igaleus, along with his brothers, worked as a hunter in the vicinity of the Lizard Marsh. They hunted and foraged fresh meat for a huge orcish army led by a terrifying devil Gorgomorz. Igaleus harbored no goodwill towards the orcs or their Lower Planes master, but they paid for meat very well. Igaleus' hunters picked the lands clean and started diving into the Lizard Marsh, following herds of white-tailed deer‎. Eventually, the hunting party was ambushed by Saslick's lizardfolk, and Igaleus was snatched. He spent several weeks in captivity until the Hero of Daggerford infiltrated the lizard tribe while wearing the Green Hag's of Lizard Marsh polymorph. Igaleus promised to lead his fellow slaves away from danger, through the marsh, and to safety if he were given a key from the lizardfolk slave pen keeper, easy to spot by his size and a large wart on the snout. However, it was unknown if the Hero helped the slaves, angering the entire village of lizardfolk and their black dragon patron - Thuraskulusk.

## Igulik

Igulik was a legendary mighty hero from Rashemen who once famously wielded a powerful artifact, the Sea Reaver, in a battle to save the tribes of his country long before the 14 century DR.

History
If the legends were to be believed, Igulik received aid when he visited Lake Ashane, where a great water spirit blessed him and gifted him the Sea Reaver scythe, a powerful magical weapon that possessed enchantments deadly to elemental creatures. With the scythe in hand, Igulik confronted an evil wizard, who brought an army of elementals to enslave the tribes of Rashemen. As per tradition in the Rashemaar legends, Igulik was triumphant, but died after ending the evil wizard's life. After his death, the Sea Reaver returned to Lake Ashane, where it waited for a new hero to wield it against the foes of Rashemen.
