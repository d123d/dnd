## Upsen Downs

## Upper planes

The upper planes (sometimes Upper Planes) was a collective term used in the Great Wheel cosmology model to represent the Outer planes of Good alignment, located across the top of the Great Wheel. These were Arcadia, Mount Celestia, Bytopia, Elysium, the Beastlands, Arborea, and Ysgard.

The World Tree cosmology model rearranged, renamed, and reorganized the upper planes into the Celestial planes but some people still used the older term to describe the good-aligned planes.

## Upsetting onslaught

Upsetting onslaught, also sometimes known as binding blast, was a spell used by some warlocks, particularly those of the dark pact.

Effects
When upsetting onslaught was cast, the warlock cast a bolt of pure arcane power at a target of his or her choice within fifty feet. If the target failed to resist the bolt's effects upon their minds, they dazed into a stupor for a brief moment or took even greater injury. While the spell usually attacked the mind of the opponent, dark pact warlocks could choose instead to attack the body if they viewed it to be more vulnerable.

Upsetting onslaught could be cast with relative ease and reacquired after its casting through a rest of a few minutes or more.

History
Prior to the Spellplague, upsetting onslaught was known more commonly as binding blast and was able to be cast at-will, rather than once every so often. In virtually all other ways, however, the spell was identical.

## Upstream Span

The Upstream Span was a bridge across the River Mirar in the city of Luskan.

Location
Of the three bridges that crossed the River Mirar within Luskan (the other two being Dalath's Span and the Harbor Cross), the Upstream Span was—appropriately—the furthest upstream and therefore the northernmost. At its north end was the North Gate—the northern entrance to the walled city—thus the bridge provided quick access from the city's South Bank directly to this gate. Baliver's House of Horses was situated at the south end of this bridge.

History
After Luskan descended into anarchy following the events of the Spellplague in the, the Upstream Span was nearly destroyed and could no longer be used to cross the river. In the decades that followed, local gangs would occasionally try to make repairs to the bridge—sometimes by simply fashioning a makeshift crossing out of ropes—in hopes of charging tolls. The bridge remained impassable through the, but was usable by the, albeit crumbing, treacherous, and controlled by Ship Rethnor, who demanded tolls for its use. By the, the Upstream Span had been fully restored.

## Uplift

Uplift was a cooperative spell that allowed a priest to boost the spellcasting ability of another.

Effects
Both priests had to be able to cast this spell, which took 12 hours to complete. At the end of this long ritual, the more powerful priest was granted greater divine magic temporarily, allowing them to receive and cast extra spells.

During the casting of uplift, the priests decided which new spells they desired and supplicated their deity or deities for them. Upon successful completion of this spell, the priests touched palms and the superior priest immediately gained the additional spells and had 10 minutes in which to cast them. At the end of this time, the priest that received the uplift became mentally exhausted and required eight hours of bedrest.

Components
The only somatic component of this spell was the touching of palms at the very end. Verbal components included naming the bonus spell or spells desired. The material components were the priests' holy symbols and an offering from each priest worth at least 500 gold pieces.

Notable Users
Circle singers, the specialty priests of the Church of Angharradh, were capable of casting this spell once per tenday.

## Uplands

The Uplands was a sparsely populated stretch of farmlands in the realm of Impiltur, located east of the Earthfast Mountains and north of the much more populated region known as the Easting Coast.

Description
The Uplands contained a few large towns and small cities but was most noted for its numerous farming communities known as "thaedars". These collectives saw a number of small farms banding together for mutual protection, easier transportation of goods and a greater shared labor force during the harvest months. Among the most successful and well-known of these groups included the Red Ram, Silver Plough and Three Horns thaedars.

The miners and other laborers of the Uplands led hard lives that often led to heavy drinking rowdy behavior. While this behavior was seldom tolerated, these hard-working folk earned the nation significant wealth, and were a granted a fair amount of leeway by otherwise austere Impilturan constabulary.

History
Before the Great Glacier receded from northeastern Faerûn the Uplands were dominated by the great hobgoblin realm of Haekrukkha.

## Uppendown

Uppendown was a rock gnome baker living in Gnomengarde in the late 15 century DR.

Activities
Along with Panana, Uppendown was responsible for prepping bread dough made from Gnomengarde's distinctive green mushrooms. His role was to shape the dough into loaves before baking. He took his work very seriously, and worked like an artist in deep concentration with his tongue sticking out at an angle as he sculpted the dough.

Abilities
Like all the other gnomes in Gnomengarde, Uppendown had some training as a wizard in addition to his innate abilities. In combat, he preferred to attack with spells like ray of frost and magic missile.

## Upper Towers

The Uppers Towers was the formal name given to a pair of parallel, major torchtowers — civic owned guard towers that stood along the city of Waterdeep's Trollwall.Every tower connected to the Trollwall falls under the designation of "torchtower."The sourcebook City System states that all torchtowers with names are "major towers".

Location
These towers stood in the North Ward, in the center of its border with the Sea Ward. They stood on either side of the High Road, where it carried on into the Field Ward.

Structure
The Upper Towers both stood four stories tall. They were each connected to the Trollwall that bounded the city and separated the North and Sea Wards from the Field Ward. They acted as one of the gates between these wards.
Interior
Being torchtowers, the Upper Towers both had all the features that were common among them. This included a debriefing room, a small armory, holding cells, storage rooms for the holding of evidence or confiscated items, some bedchambers, and some garderobes. Additionally, being major torchtowers, the Upper Towers both had six small holding cells that could hold up to ten people, which were used in times of trouble.

Activities
Like all other torchtowers, these towers both had three beacon-fires that were constantly kept on standby.

Inhabitants
The Upper Towers were manned by members of the City Watch.

## Upland Rise

The Upland Rise was a forested hill that marked the northeastern edge of the city of Neverwinter.

Description
The Upland Rise historically served as a natural park for the people of Neverwinter. Mists from the Neverwinter River would gather around the base of the hill during the night.

History
In the decades following the eruption of Mount Hotenow, Dagult Neverember sent loggers and engineers to harvest the trees on the Upland Rise for lumber to rebuild the Neverwinter docks. By the late 1470s DR, the hill had been completely deforested and stripped of its natural beauty, leaving it a wasteland dotted with stumps.

In the, the Red Wizard Dhafiyand set a trap for members of the Nashers by luring them to the Upland Rise at night. There, he attacked alongside ash zombies and stole the Crown of Neverwinter from them.

## Uponga Naodha

Uponga Naodha was a captain of the guard of Port Nyanzaru in Chult during the late 15 century DR.

History
During the events of the death curse, Captain Naodha expressed interest to the Master of the Market about outfitting his subordinates in breastplate armor from the Sword Coast. While he understood that the Chultan heat made such armor impractical, he had also grown frustrated with how vulnerable the guards were when faced with the monsters of the jungle. His solution was to commission a foreign smith to craft titansteel armor for him.
