## Udhask

Udhask was a deep gnome ghost in Blingdenstone in the late 15 century DR.

History
When the drow attacked Blingdenstone in 1371 DR, he tried to reach for his loot when he died of a heart attack. For unknown reasons, he rose as a ghost. Thereafter, every time someone approached his remains he relived his death in a loop, attacking and scaring people.

If someone recovered his remains and put it in the catacombs, he would find peace.

## Udi

Udi was a wandering wu jen in Bawa in 1357 DR.

Activities
Udi wandered the wilderness of Bawa, trying to help the local tribes. She acted as healer and mediator between different tribes. She did not care about the Bavanese laws, customs, and property however.

For this, Udi was greatly respected among the Bawani people and no chief refused her counsel, but she was declared an outlaw by the Bavanese, risking her freedom every time she went into town.

## 'Ud

The 'ud  was a stringed Zakharan instrument similar to both the lute and guitar.

Because of its rich tone, the  'ud was often referred to as amir al-tarab, which translated as "prince of enchantment" in Midani.

Description
An 'ud consisted of a short fretless neck attached to a widened pear-shaped body. This instrument typically had five double strings, though 'uds from the Free Cities of Zakhara had an extra string. The strings were plucked with a small pick made from bone or from the shaft of an eagle or roc feather. The body was made from wood, but it was not uncommon to see 'uds from the Crowded Sea region to have bodies made from a tortoise shell.

History
The 'ud was a popular instrument among the wandering rawun of the Land of Fate.

## 'Ud of the marids

The 'Ud of the Marids was a beautiful magical 'ud created by marid luthiers to be presented to the new high chieftain of the jann of the High Desert on his ascension day.

Each new jann chieftain received a full set of magical instruments known collectively as the instruments of the jann; therefore, multiple 'uds of the marids existed throughout Zakhara.

Description
The entire body of this instrument was pure white and covered with intricate pearl carvings. The base, or soundbox, was actually made from a single giant pearl taken from one of the giant clams of the Crowded Sea. Since collecting the giant pearl was a dangerous task, the marids wisely had lesser beings perform this task.

Powers
This 'ud allowed the user to cast a strong charm person spell when the instrument was played. Bards and rawunin could use this power twice per day, but all others could only access this magic once per day.

Only bards and rawunin could "raise the waters" when playing the 'Ud of the Marids, summoning a powerful water elemental to do their bidding. This power could only be used once per week and the 'ud needed to be near a sufficient supply of water in order for the magic to function. The elemental remained under control so long as the 'ud was played but instantly returned to their home plane if the music was interrupted or ceased altogether.

## Udderfish venom

Udderfish venom was a poison extracted from the fangs or spines of the udderfish. It was harvested by pirates from the Nelanther Isles and sold to merchants in Skullport and Waterdeep.

Description
This substance was a thick, whitish liquid that tasted vaguely sweet. It could be mixed with water or alcohol and still retain its potency. A single dose typically cost 400 gp.

Effects
A victim that imbibed this poison (and did not manage to resist it) was not likely to notice the effects at first, perhaps passing it off as a headache or mild body aches, but soon they felt as if they had taken a real beating or been stepped on by a giant.

Notable Users
Elsk Thavarask, the proprietor of the Missing Minotaur tavern in Everlund, circa the Year of Wild Magic, 1372 DR, was known to use this substance to quiet down extremely rowdy customers. He would also sell it to people he trusted.

## Udadrow

Udadrow, or as they were almost-exclusively known, drow, were the most common variety of drow by far. Apart from extremely rare offshoots of drow (like the Lorendrow and Aevendrow who dwelt in secretive locations), all drow were Udadrow.

Description
These dark elves were those, or the descendants of those, who originally became influenced by the evil deity, Lolth.

Society
Udadrow society was oppressive and xenophobic. Some dark elves were able to escape tyrannical drow society, and escaped to the surface of Toril.

History
Due to the authoritative influence of the Spider Queen, almost all drow followed her ways to the Underdark. Two offshoot groups, Lorendrow and Aevendrow, did not. As they descended to the depths of the Underdark, Udadrow society become more and more corrupted by Lolth.

## Uddron Blass

Uddron Blass was an illithid cultist of the Absolute who wished to take down Baldur's Gate.

Description

This curious illthid wore some sort of mask and hood. Uddron appeared to be far more muscular than a typical mind flayer, and revealed a well-built torso under a spiked mantle. Uddron's pupil-less eyes had a soft white-pink glow.

Possessions
Uddron wielded a gold-silver two-pronged dagger.

Activities
Uddron sent lackeys to do their work whilst the mind flayer made preparations on behalf of the cult of the Absolute. However, Blass would step in should his underlings not be able to handle certain circumstances.

Relationships
The Master seemed to keep a pet displacer beast, who closely followed their orders. Uddorn also had a mindwitness who aimed to bring "enlightenment" to their servants and insanity to others who stood in the cult's way.

History
Uddron's Cultists

Prior to or during 1492 DR, Uddron Blass had infiltrated Baldur's Gate. Many other cultists, some of which were formerly members of churches, referred to Blass as their master. Under Uddron's command, these cultists gathered in the Knowledge-Hold, a grand library containing vast amounts of knowledge. The Heroes of Baldur's Gate caught onto the strange hooded cultists, and interrupted their ritual. A fight broke out between the heroes and the cult members. Father Alby, a former Kelemvorite, attempted to convince Nerys that the master, Uddron, had foreseen future events, and wished to show her their abilities. The heroes, however, managed to escape.

Uddron Steps In

Shandie, with Minsc (and Boo!) and Nerys in tow, approached a man known as Fetcher to acquire information about Master Uddron. He, however, did not know more than they did about the hidden master. At that point, Uddron's mindwitness appeared, along with a group of cultists, but they were slain after a battle. The other two heroes, Krydle and Delina, were attacked by another group of cultists, but Uddron Blass stepped in and blasted them with great psionic energy.

Captured Heroes

Master Uddron successfully captured Krydle, Delina, and Coran, and had them chained up underneath the High Hall. Uddron revealed that a Flaming Fist captain belonged to them. Here, Blass tapped into Krydle's mind, turning him against his father, and showing him visions of nautiloids pouring out a void. In a burst of wild magic energy, Uddron and Delina disappeared from the room, leaving the two adventurers behind with the displacer beast. As the beast was taken down, Coran and Krydle escaped.

The Mindbreaker
Krydle's and Uddron's minds were interlocked due to a mindflayer tadpole inside the former, and the remaining heroes made their way to a secret chamber under the streets. Here, Uddron believed he could bring Baldur's Gate to its knees by summoning the Mindbreaker. As the adventurers rushed in, Krydle stabbed Blass with a poisoned dagger, slaying the illithid. As this happened, Delina closed the portal on the Mindbreaker, causing it to be destroyed.

## Udzgul Thairharrow

Udzgul Thairharrow was a farmer in the village of Maskyr's Eye in the Vast in the late 13 or early 14 century DR.

Personality
He was considered to be half-witted.

History
When the folk of Maskyr's Vale came to investigate the collapse of old Maskyr's Tower, Udzgul claimed to have spotted a silvery doorway appearing for an instant in the air high over the ruins at sunset, but no-one believed him because they thought him a half-wit. Some others reported the same thing later, but they too were dismissed. Despite this, Khonduil Ammargath, who'd been in the tower when it fell, felt Udzgul was likely to have been right in what he saw.It is unknown when these events occur, but given that Khonduil was still alive as of the late 1350s DR, it is presumed to be within his lifetime and thus somewhere in the late 13 early 14 centuries DR.

Udzgul was deceased by the late 1350s DR.

## Udlara's tent

Udlara's tent was an enchanted tent belonging to Udlara Thaerintauhn.

Description
On the outside, the tent was dinghy and small.

Powers
The tent was somewhat similar in power to Daern's instant fortress, though far weaker. It was impossible to cut without powerful enchanted weapons, impervious to normal weapons or even those of bare enchantment. It was small, but opened into a much larger space; with a command word, it could be made to fold and shrink itself, alongside all its contents, though it could not activate this while there were living beings inside. Finally, no insect could enter it.

## Udlara Thaerintauhn

Udlara Thaerintauhn was a bedine camel breeder.

Udlara was not above taking advantage of outlanders, if they didn't show themselves to be respectful of bedine culture.

## Udlara's Camel Emporium

Udlara's Camel Emporium was a camel-selling and rental business owned by Udlara Thaerintauhn in the 14 century DR.

Location
Udlara's Emporium was across the bazaar in the Bitter Well oasis.

Structure
The tent appeared rather worn down, but it was actually an enchanted tent, called Udlara's tent.

Interior
The interior was actually clean, spacious, and well-lit.

Services
Udlara sold camels and camel-related services. Depending on their opinion of the buyer, the asking price for a camel could go from 45 to 80 gp.

Defenses
The magical properties of the tent made it difficult to damage without using magical weapons; even those had to be stronger than the bare minimum in order to deal damage.

History
Udlara's Camel Emporium had made arrangements with Cormyr to supply some of their adventurers with camels and guides, but Udlara renegotiated with the agents as they arrived.

## Udere

Udere was a drow assassin who abandoned the Underdark and worked for the infamous Dom as a mercenary in Cormyr during the mid-1480s DR.

Description
Udere had long white hair tied back, and wore a suit of plate armor while carrying two scimitars.

Relationships

For reasons not known to outsiders, Udere was fully devoted to the success of Dom's gang.

History

Originating from the Underdark like Pelloth, Udere took up work as a mercenary on the surface and joined Dom's elite team. The final mission took them to Greenweald Lane, where Dom was hired by the Shadovar of Thultanthar to waylay Bensen Virandson as he transported a fragment of a sought-after Nether Scroll to a Harper Lodge. He lead a group of hired mercenaries in an expert ambush that struck near the lodge, but was beaten back and slain nonetheless.
