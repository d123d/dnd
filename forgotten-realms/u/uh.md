## Uhndek

Uhndek was the lizardfolk leader of the Khoroshen tribe, based in the marshland of the Evermoors in the 1370s DR.

Relations
Uhndek had a crocodile companion named Dross.

Description
Uhndek was short and stocky.

Possessions

Uhndek owned a suit of well-made crocodile-hide armor and carried a spear, Semuanya's Fang, which had been adorned with human finger bones and carved runes. He also owned a crocodile-tooth necklace.

History
In 1374 DR, Uhndek and the tribe were hired by First Speaker Tessarin Alaurun in defense of Nesmé, but Uhndek betrayed the city and made an agreement with the trolls to work together in attacking Nesmé; the lizardfolk would gain the city and the trolls the surrounding moors. The meeting to agree to this plan was interrupted by Alaurun's aide, Chaver Tegarech, who was captured and taken to mines below Baeniff Tor.

## Uhlsrysn Xorlarrin

Uhlsrysn Xorlarrin was a member of House Xorlarrin and a master of Sorcere in 1479 DR.

Personality
The drow had a prickly pride, always feeling himself inferior to his brother and he was easily provoked.

Abilities
Uhlsrysn was a talented transmuter, known especially for his transmutations of goblin slaves into terrible monsters.

History
Uhlsrysn was the secondboy of House Xorlarrin, but because the elderboy was a more talented wizard, Uhlsrysn was chosen to remain as an instructor at Sorcere.

Appendix

 Notes
Page 63 of Menzoberranzan: City of Intrigue states that Uhlsrysn is secondboy of House Xorlarrin, but previously states (on page 53) that Ravel Xorlarrin holds that position. Given that source's description of Ravel and Uhlsrysn's entry stating that "his elder brother is a far more accomplished wizard," it is most likely referring to Ravel as the elder brother and not Brack'thal Xorlarrin, who lost much of his arcane ability during the Spellplague. Given this information, Uhlsrysn is most likely thirdboy of House Xorlarrin, rather than second.

## Uhmbrick

Uhmbrick was a mercenary ranger circa 1363 DR.

History
Uhmbrick led a company of 50 fellow ranger mercenaries who were often based in The Way Inn. In 1363 DR, they became involved in the Second Dragonspear War.

## Uhkerek

A uhkerek was a type of sled used on the Great Glacier.

Description
The uhkerek, a transportation sled akin to the jakerek, boasted a larger size and employed two caribou as pulling animals. Its platform took on a box-like shape, with sides standing at approximately  in height. The bottom of the platform was lined with seal skins, generously coated in blubber to minimize friction. Typically utilized for transporting supplies, the uhkerek also accommodated up to three passengers when necessary.
