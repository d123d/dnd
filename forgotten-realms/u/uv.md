## Uvuudaum

Uvuudaums were malevolent creatures from an alien plane.

Description
Uvuudaum had a torso, with two largely normal humanoid arms where expected on it, but their bizarre lower body consisted of six arms that splayed around in a rather spider-like fashion. They were known to wear loose clothing over the lower body. Most horrifying, however, was the tail-like appendage that a uvuudaum had in place of a head, on the end of which was an iron-hard spike.

Personality
These were strange and malevolent creatures. They took delight in revealing the existence of their horrifying, madness-inducing homeplace to more structured realities.

Abilities
Lacking eyes, uvuudaums instead could somehow detect the locations of creatures through some strange sense. Lacking a mouth, they could somehow telepathically communicate with any language-using creature within  of them.

Uvuudaums instilled madness and confusion in anyone within some  of them. Additionally, when an uvuudaum actually attacked someone with its head-spike, they drained away some vital sense and stability of the mind, and healed themself in the draining.

Uvuudaum were highly resistant to being damaged by electricity, and healed quite fast from most injuries except those caused by fire, severe cold, or holy weapons.

Additionally, uvuudaums were spellcasters, or at least able to naturally (if such a word could be applied to them) replicate the effects of many different spells, including confusion, dimension door, greater dispelling, displacement, haste, invisibility, lightning bolt, magic missile, mage armor, polymorph self, shield, stoneskin, true strike, and web all at will; chain lightning, cone of cold, disintegrate, dominate person, plane shift, prismatic spray, scrying, teleport without error, and wall of force thrice per day each; and contingent resurrection, nailed to the sky, time duplicate, and time stop once per day each.

Combat

Uvuudaums were madness-inducing just by existing, but any enemy who managed to hold off insanity long enough to fight them found their many spell-like talents and head-spike an even worse danger. If they had the opportunity to prepare for combat, uvuuduams typically made sure to utilize their haste, displacement, and stoneskin talents.

Society
Uvuudaums came from a distant realm that was so frighteningly remote it was considered beyond the planes, a space where the rules of normal dimensions meant nothing. The uvuudaum were participant and part of the madness there, where all the native creatures were alien and inchoate, and unspeakable entities whispered awful things to those who listened. Some said that uvuudaums were lords in that far realm.

Uvuudaums were sometimes encountered in groups of up to six individuals; such a gathering was called a madness.

History
In, the Purple Claws mercenary company accidentally entered the lair of an uvuudaum during an expedition to Throrgar in the Underdark. Every member of the company was killed by the uvuudaum's mooncalf minions.

## Uvaeren's Legacy

Uvaeren's Legacy, also known as the Legacy Library, was a unique repository of knowledge found in Myth Drannor that offered tremendous amount of lore to knowledge-seekers, albeit in unusual ways that seldom involved the reading of books or other documents.

Location
Uvaeren's Legacy was situated on the western side of the Street of Sorrows in the Dlabraddath district of the city.

Structure
The grand stone library comprised four stories, and four different sides, with wide-set stairs leading up to an entrance on each side.

Interior
The first floor of the library held an open-air space lined with statues of knowledgeable sages and scholars, in the form of the various races that called Myth Drannor Home. Rooms found throughout this floor  included the Hall of Scents, the Lorespirits' Room, the Hall of Crystal, and the Memory Chamber.

The top three floors of the library's Legacy Tower composed the Memory Chamber, a massive domed chamber whose walls were embedded with the same empathic crystals found in the Hall of Crystal. Within these tel'kiira-like crystals were thousands of years of collected emotions, nightmares, aspirations, and accumulated knowledge. Within the chamber was a seat that resembled spelljamming helm in its function, allowing elves to access the collected information of the crystals, or instilling madness into the minds of other races.

Services
Knowledge could be ingested in ways that were not possible in other libraries or houses of knowledge. Bottled fragrances in the Hall of Scents could be used to bring up memories and help identify certain pungent plants and substances; singing along with the library's statues would reveal new lyrics and notes; patterns of twinkling flashes of light could reveal hidden lore during meditations sessions; and empathic crystals that evoked feelings that uttered specific command words when standing before a mural or sculpture, or while accessing the Memory Chamber.

Defenses
The four entrances of the library were guarded by stone guardians that occasionally offered information about where to access information within the library.

The Memory Chamber was always watched over by two members of the Akh'Faer and at least one amrathor.

## Uvaeren

Uvaeren was an ancient elven realm built during The Founding Time in the forest of Arcorar.

Geography
Uvaeren was built in a forest clearing in what would later become Mistledale, due southwest of Oakengrove Abbey.

Government
Like the other realms of Arcorar, Uvaeren was ruled by a Coronal. In Uvaeren, the Coronal was an elected official, though once elected, they ruled for life.

History
During the Third Crown War, nine clans of elves (five sun elf, and four moon elf), all of whom were enemies of House Vyshaan, fled Aryvandaar in an effort to create a new, more peaceful elven realm. Initially, they settled the lands on the western shore of Lake Miir, and named their new realm Ilodhar. Ilodhar suffered centuries of attacks from goblins, ogres, and orcs, and in -8210 DR, all except three of the sun elf clans decided to abandon Ilodhar and try to settle somewhere else. One of the sun elf clans and two of the moon elf clans joined the elves of Rystall Woods, where they founded the city of Yrlaancel in -8130 DR, while the remaining sun elf clan and two moon elf clans settled southwest of Jhyrennstar to found Uvaeren. The Rivleam clan were chosen to lead the people living there.

Uvaeren became famous for its libraries and other collections of mundane and magical lore, developing several innovative methods to store knowledge, including, as many elves believe, the first tel'kiira.

Coronal Kiyuigh, Uvaeren's third Coronal, was more of a warrior than a scholar, and after his predecessor died while hunting in -7400 DR, Kiyuigh began leading sorties into the mountains to slay orcs and giants living there. He managed to lead one hundred such attacks until he was overcome and killed in -6900 DR. Eltaor, the next Coronal, returned Uvaeren to a land of scholarship. After Eltaor was killed by a palimpsest in -6300 DR, a young elf named Intevar was elected Coronal. Intevar was credited as having managed to store the collective knowledge of the elves in his personal library, unfortunately, this attracted the greedy, and Intevar was killed defending his library from seekers of the secrets of high magic in -5660 DR. Uvaeren's last Coronal was Intevar's young wife and apprentice, Lady Mnuvae.

Over three millennia after its founding, on Midsummer of -5000 DR, a meteor hit Uvaeren, obliterating it and killing a full three quarters of its citizens, including Coronal Mnuvae and almost the entirety of the city's nobility.

Uvaeren was never rebuilt and its survivors migrated to Jhyrennstar and Semberholme. Its remaining ruins would, millennia later in -1950 DR, house a horde of drow who had invaded western Cormanthyr and would be the site of Coronal Sakrattars Irithyl's death. Over two hundred years later in -1720 DR, some Netherese wizards took refuge in those same ruins, but all were dead by -1718 DR after setting off the magical defenses of one of Uvaeren's hidden libraries. On Nightal 30 of 0 DR, humans were escorted through the ruins of Uvaeren to witness the quamaniith high magic ritual that would raise the Standing Stone and seal the Dales Compact.

At some point, the dragon Dretchroyaster managed to acquire the Diamond Staff of Chomylla, an Uvaeranni artifact which was key to opening one of the library-vaults there. After discovering the staff's purpose, the dragon planned to use it as a focus to absorb all of the magic remaining in Uvaeren and turn himself into a being capable of challenging the gods themselves. Circa 1480 DR, the noted Turmishan sage Imani sponsored several groups of heroes to recover the staff, but although successful, it was stolen from him by an orc gang known as the Fangfists, this led to a four-way chase involving the Cult of the Dragon; a band of Zhentarim mercenaries; an agent of the Shadovar; and Imani's hired adventurers. Imani's employees won out in the end and the staff was returned to him with the knowledge contained within its keyed vault intact.

## Uva

Uva was a Black Gauntlet of Bane, who served the Cult of the Absolute in the late 15 century DR.

Description
Uva was a pale-skinned human woman who wore her decorated Banite Black Gauntlet armor that included a breastplate, pauldrons decorated with a hellish face, and a large and intimidating helmet, decorated with a pair of metal tusks and a big image of Bane's black hand. Uva was armed with an infernal mace.

History
In the, the Cult of Bane joined forces with the Cult of the Absolute, led by the Chosen of Bane, Lord Enver Gortash. Black Gauntlet Uva was a part of the Cult's operations that targeted the countless refugees that flooded Baldur's Gate. Her branch of the Cult assembled donations and toys rigged with blackpowder explosives in the Felogyr's Fireworks shop's third floor. Presumably, Black Gauntlet Uva perished during or shortly before the Cult attacked Baldur's Gate.

## Uvalkhur

Uvalkhur was a wizard of Sembia, and the once-rider of Malaeragoth.

Personality
He was a charming, expert guide with no head for intrigue. He didn't mind sharing his magic with his dragon steed.

Possessions
He had a manor in Northern Sembia, northwest of Saerb. He stocked it with much magic, which included a scrying mirror.

Activities
Uvalkhur was fond of riding the dragon Malaeragoth, and besides other activities, he gladly showed him many places.

Relationships
The son of a rich Sembian merchant, Uvalkhur was known to ride on Malaeragoth, a sapphire dragon.

History
Uvalkhur was a young man when he somehow secured the services of the dragon steed Malaeragoth; he also drew the enmity of the Cult of the Dragon. Eventually, when he was no longer young, he was slain in his estate, which was looted in turn by the surviving Cult members, and then by his steed. That destruction had taken place almost one hundred years before the ; the only surviving witness was the dwarf Badger Bates, who had been hired to build fountains and dig gardens at the time.

Legacy
His manor remained haunted into the 14 century DR by phantom wizards and overrun by leucrotta.
