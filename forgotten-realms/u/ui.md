## Uiss

Uiss was the leader of the Order of the Snake in Fochu Peninsula in Wa.

History
Around 1158 DR, the yuan-ti infiltrated the Order of the Snake.

In 1358 DR, the yuan-ti abomination Uiss was the leader of the clan. That year, the Snakes ordered a hunt against some people of the foreign adventuring party that accompanied Osari Minhiro, as the adventurers housed the souls of members of the Order of the Mouse.

At last, the adventurers confronted Uiss and his minions in their lair and defeated them.

## Uibilaqthraxx

Uibilaqthraxx, also called the True Tongue by beholders, was an ancient and disused language that influenced the modern Quevquel language of beholders.

History
Uibilaqthraxx was one of several older beholder languages that fell into disuse. Much of Quevquel was derived from Uibilaqthraxx directly or indirectly, with lengthy and ornate True Tongue words shortened into their much quicker Speech forms.

The name of the beholder cult Daeth Haeromm was transliterated directly from Uibilaqthraxx, with the meaning of "Wise Gatherers."

## Uibilaqthraxx dictionary

A list of words and phrases from the Uibilaqthraxx beholder language, an ancestor of the Quevquel language.

 daeth

## Uirkymbrand

Uirkymbrand was a Chosen of Mystra at some point long before the 8 century DR.

Personality
The man was self-aware about being very overbearing and not moralistic, instead being quite enthused about doing objectionable things unto others.

Description
In a vision revealed to Elminster Aumar by the goddess Mystra in 767 DR, Uirkymbrand was a bald man in red robes.

Possessions
Uirkymbrand had a crystal orb they used to scry, almost certainly a crystal ball, and he mentioned a "spell-seizing scepter".

History
One of the Chosen of Mystra, Elminster Aumar, received a vision from the goddess in the . In the vision, the previous Chosen, Uirkymbrand, saw the death of a fellow Chosen, Elthaeris, as cause for rejoicing, and began considering things he would be doing to weak people.
