## Ukolo

Ukolo was an Azuposi settlement in the Pasocada Basin in Maztica.

Activities
Despite its small size (its population was about 300), it was nonetheless important, for it was the center of Azuposi turquoise mining. Raw turquoise was exported from Ukolo to other, larger settlements were it was carved into jewelry and such.

## Ukulsid

Ukulsid was a dread warrior gnoll and the military commander and governor of the fortified town of Surcross in Thay as of .

Activities
As the military commander of the massive garrison in Surcross, Ukulsid was in charge of the forces stationed there to protect Thay's border with Thesk.

As the governor of Surcross, Ukulsid met with his subjects and meted out justice from the Hall of Petition within the town's central citadel, known as the Tombstone. However, few Surcross inhabitants ever came to petition him owing to the fact that he was prone to kill any who annoyed him, and to then have their corpse reanimated to serve the town's garrison.

Relationships
Ukulsid received his orders from Szass Tam himself. He permitted Valindra Shadowmantle to use his city as her headquarters only because this was the will of his master.

History
During the Shadovar–Thay War, Ukulsid was in charge of Surcross as it came under assault from the flying Netherese citadel of Kolthunral.

## Ukthet

Ukthet was a dragonborn member of the Inquest, and one of the elders that once ruled over the Abeiran city of Xorvintroth. After their deaths, their consciousnesses were preserved by magical means, while aspects of their personas lingered in the Astral Plane as a spectral echoes.

Description
Like their fellow elders, Ukthet wore a set of robes with astral and arcane imagery embroidered upon it along with a large metallic medallion set upon a necklace.

While in the Astral Plane, Ukthet and the other Inquest elders wore crowns of viridian crystals that protected them from detection from astral dreadnoughts.

History
At some point in life, Uthket died and their consciousness was transferred to a brain in a jar. While researching the forces of the Far Realm, the consciousnesses of the Inquest elders became trapped in the Astral Plane. The dragonborn arranged for their dead bodies and still-living brains to be transported elsewhere in their city.

After some time the entity known as the Emissary of the Nine Eyes, showed them a green planet that would be an ideal new home for their people. The Inquest set to work on the bridge of stars, a means by which they could travel to that planet and be free from constant warfare.

Their work would remain incomplete however. Some time during or around the, the living brains of Ukthet, Lirith, and Krektesh were discovered within the remains of Xorvintroth. They were quite startled and bewildered after being stirred from such an prolonged stasis.

## Ukujik

An ukujik, also referred to as a cutting house, was a structure used by Iulutiun.

Description
An ukujik was a dome with a diameter of  that served the purpose of animal butchering. Within the dome, a snow partition separated it into two halves, rising approximately  from the floor. One half contains two pits that were  deep, known as the ijukujik, while the other half without pits is called the akukujik. Each section had its own doorway for access.

In the akukujik, hunters carried out the task of game butchering, while they discarded organs and blubber over the partition into the ijukujik. The organs were placed in one pit, while the blubber was deposited in the other pit. The surplus in the ijukujik pits was available for villagers to freely take for themselves.

## Uktar (The Guild)

Uktar was a member of The Guild and its elected bursar in the late 15 century DR.

Description
Uktar was a human man who named himself after the month of Uktar, also known as the Rotting. He hid his face behind an ornate porcelain mask with only red vein-like marks stretching from his face, betraying his scars or an affliction of some kind. He had a fair skin tone and black hair gathered in a tight ponytail. He preferred to wear emerald-green robes with the Guild's telltale colors.

Activities
As the Guild's bursar, Uktar managed the organization's financial affairs and offered a bounty for unique keys that unlocked vaults of certain individuals within the Counting House's vaults. These sought after keys belonged to Cazador Szarr, Lord Gortash, Rakath Glitterbeard, Allandra Grey, Lady Jannath, Lorroakan, Ulder Ravengard, Viconia DeVir, and even Nine-Fingers herself.

History
Uktar gained the position and the Guildmaster's year sometime in or before the . He kept an eye on the illicit activities in the Chionthar Valley, including documenting the rise to power of a petty weapons dealer, Enver Gortash, him pushing out the Knights of the Shield and the Zhentarim from Baldur's Gate and eventually, becoming a lord. In contrast to Nine-Fingers, Uktar appeared to be cold and calculating. He advised Keene to be strict and punish those unable to pay for protection in order to show no signs of weakness. When the city of Baldur's Gate was infiltrated by the Cult of the Absolute, the Guild found itself losing territory to the new faction, especially with the dreaded Stone Lord working as the organization's muscle. Uktar thought that, at the very least, those who could not pay for protection should lose the said protection even if it were an orphanage, while Nine-Fingers claimed that there was nothing to demand out of orphans and that withdrawing protection meant giving up more territory to the Stone Lord. But Uktar did not realize that Nine-Fingers was not benevolent and instead ordered her men to seize the orphanage building and arm children old enough to fight the Cult and protect the building claimed by the Guild.

## Ukuma

Ukuma was a giant walrus who dwelt in the fortress of Grimskalle on the island of Solstice in the Frozenfar in the late 15 century DR.

Personality
Ukuma was granted intelligence and speech through a druidic awaken spell. It was not aggressive and did not attack unless provoked, instead spending its days rolling around on the ice and making small talk with anyone who approached. Those who offered fresh fish or other tasty morsels could easily earn its favor (and some valuable information about Grimskalle's layout).

History
Originally awakened and brought to Grimskalle by a group of frost druids, Ukuma was left in the central chamber of the lower level to serve as a guardian as of the winter of the . However, Ukuma had no real interest in guarding anything.
