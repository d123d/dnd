## Yngukulub

Yngukulub the Devourer was an aboleth ally of Gar Shatterkeel at the Fane of the Eye under Tyar-Besil during the Elemental Evil crisis.

Personality
Yngukulub obeyed Gar because it believed that in the new era of Olhydra the aboleth would rule the world.

History
In 1491 DR, Yngukulub stayed at the Fane, served by a retinue of deranged kuo-toa, until some adventurers infiltrated the temple and killed it.

## Yntar Shieldblazon

Yntar Shieldblazon was a bard.

History
Around 1370 DR, Yntar was a local member of the Heralds of Faerûn, holding the title of Flailchains. He was an authority on the history of Westgate. Once the role of Flailchains had been a public one, but after the death of the last king, Verovan, it became an independent agent but with strong ties to the rulers of the city.

## Ynaerv's Mask

Ynaerv's Mask was an artifact known within the ancient Imaskar Empire. It was once thought to be one of the Imaskarcana.In its initial appearance, it was an Imaskarcana; one of its sister artifacts amongst the Faces of Madness is referred to as a False Imaskarcana on The Grand History of the Realms, retconning it as no longer an Imaskarcana, since the official Imaskarcana have been revealed as predating them.

History
An alliance of Imaskari archwizards known as the Cabal of Madness created a number of masks known as the Faces of Madness, each themed around a school of magic, over decades after striking a bargain with horrors from beyond their world. Ynaerv's Mask was one of them. Each of those major artifacts were created with a curse that only the creator knew how to avoid. Before they were able to implement their plans, however, the Mulan rebelled with the aid of their gods, ultimately destroying Imaskar as a nation. In the resulting tumult, the masks were scattered.

Appearance
Ynaerv's Mask was a hideous mask built from the skull of an elven paladin, bearing the antlers of her mount. Small copper chains ran around the back of the mask to secure it; it covered the top of the wearer's face when worn.

Powers
Ynaerv's Mask granted an overwhelmingly powerful resistance to all spells of the Necromancy school. It allowed the user to cast such spells with an amazing level of penetration of innate resistances. The wearer could also employ control undead thrice a day, or more, depending on their force of personality. They also got the ability to cast, thrice a day, their choice of vampiric touch, animate dead, create greater undead, create undead and soul bind.

However, every time the mask was donned, a wearer ran the risk of being slain, and then raised as a curst were their fortitude to fail them.
