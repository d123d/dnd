## Feats

### Allied Defense

- **PREABILITY:** 1,CATEGORY=FEAT,Combat Expertise

Whenever you are using Combat Expertise to gain a bonus to Armor Class, any adjacent ally gains the same bonus.

### Cover Your Tracks

- **PREABILITY:** 1,CATEGORY=FEAT,Track
- **BONUS:** SKILL|Hide|2

Add +5 to the DC to follow your tracks, or +10 if you move at half speed and hide your trail. You gain a +2 bonus to Hide checks.

### Heat Tolerance

- **ASPECT:** SaveBonus|+10 Fortitude bonus against nonlethal heat damage

You gain a +10 bonus on Fortitude saves against nonlethal heat deamage.

### Hold the Line

- **PREABILITY:** 1,CATEGORY=FEAT,Combat Reflexes
- **PREATT:** 2

You can make an attack of opportunity against an opponent who charges you when it enters an area you threaten. Your attack of opportunity happens immediately before the charge attack is resolved. Your attacks of opportunity in a round are still limited to the number allowed by Combat Reflexes.

### Initiate of Loviatar

- **PREDEITY:** 1,Loviatar
- **PRECLASS:** 1,Cleric=5

The first time you take damage in combat, you gain a +1 moral bonus on attack rolls and saves against fear effects for 1 minute per Cleric level.

### Natural Scavenger

- **PRESKILL:** 1,Survival=5

You can move at your normal overland speed while using Survival to hunt or forage for food. You gain a +4 competence bonus on Survival checks made for this purpose.

### Resist Disease

- **ASPECT:** SaveBonus|+4 Fortitude bonus against disease

Gain a +4 bonus on Fortitude saving throws against disease.

### Selective Spell

- **PREABILITY:** 1,CATEGORY=FEAT,TYPE.Metamagic
- **ADDSPELLLEVEL:** 1

You can modify an area spell so that it does not affect one designated creature within the area.

### Hyena Tribe Hunter

### Lion Tribe Warrior

- **PREMULT:** 1,[!PREABILITY:1,CATEGORY=FEAT,TYPE=Regional],[PREABILITY:1,CATEGORY=FEAT,Lion Tribe Warrior]
- **PREMULT:** 1,[PREMULT:2,[PRERACE:1,Human],[PREREGION:Shaar]]
- **PRETEXT:** Member of the Lion Tribe

You can make a full attack with a single light weapon as part of a charge action. If you have light weapons in both hands, you can instead strike with each weapon once, using the normal rules for fighting with two weapons.

### Nomadic Trekker

- **PREMULT:** 1,[!PREABILITY:1,CATEGORY=FEAT,TYPE=Regional],[PREABILITY:1,CATEGORY=FEAT,Nomadic Trekker]
- **PREMULT:** 1,[PREMULT:2,[PRERACE:1,Human],[PREREGION:Shaar]],[PREMULT:2,[PRERACE:1,Wemic],[PREREGION:Shaar]]

You can move overland across trackless plains at normal speed. You gain a +4 bonus on Constitution checks required for forced marches across plains. Up to one ally per character level can also gain these benefits while traveling with you.

### Rhinoceros Tribe Charge

- **PREMULT:** 1,[!PREABILITY:1,CATEGORY=FEAT,TYPE=Regional],[PREABILITY:1,CATEGORY=FEAT,Rhinoceros Tribe Charge]
- **PREMULT:** 1,[PREMULT:2,[PRERACE:1,Human],[PREREGION:Shaar]]
- **PRETEXT:** Member of the Rhinoceros Tribe

When you charge, if your melee attack hits, it deals an extra 2d6 points of damage. This feat works only when you make a charge, even when you're mounted. If you have the ability to make multiple attacks on a charge, you can only apply the extra damage to only one of those attacks in a round.

### Tall Mouther Hunter

- **PREMULT:** 1,[!PREABILITY:1,CATEGORY=FEAT,TYPE=Regional],[PREABILITY:1,CATEGORY=FEAT,Tall Mouther Hunter]
- **PREMULT:** 1,[PREMULT:2,[PRERACE:1,Halfling],[PREREGION:Luiren]]

You gain a +2 competence bonus on damage rolls against aberrations. You also gain the benefit of the Improved Critical feat for the weapon your are using in any such attack. This benefit does not stack with any other effect that expands your weapon's critical threat range. These benefits apply on melee attack and ranged attacks up to 30 feet.

### Woodwise

- **PREMULT:** 1,[!PREABILITY:1,CATEGORY=FEAT,TYPE=Regional],[PREABILITY:1,CATEGORY=FEAT,Woodwise]
- **PREMULT:** 1,[PREMULT:2,[PRERACE:1,Elf],[PREREGION:Misty Vale]],[PREMULT:2,[PRERACE:1,Elf],[PREREGION:Sildeyuir]],[PREMULT:2,[PRERACE:1,Elf],[PREREGION:Forest of Lethyr]]

You ignore the hampered movement penalties from naturally occurring light or heavy undergrowth. (You are still hampered by magically enhanced brush and plants.) When fighting in wooded areas, you gain a +1 dodge bonus to Armor Class.
