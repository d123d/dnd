## Special Abilities

### Weapon and Armor Proficiency


Nar Demonbinders gain no proficiency with any weapon or armor.

### Spells per Day/Spells Known


Nar Demonbinders prepare and cast spells as sorcerer. He must learn and choose his spells from the Nar Demonbinder spell list. The character's caster level is equal to his Nar Demonbinder level plus his levels in one other spellcasting class of his choice. To cast a spell, the Nar Demonbinder must have a CHA score of at least 10 + the spell's level. Nar Demonbinder bonus spells are based on CHA, and saving throws against these spells have a DC of 10 + spell level + CHA modifier.

### Fiendish Familiar


A Nar Demonbinder can call a fiendish familiar, either a imp or a quasit, just as if he had, and met the requirements for, the Improved Familiar feat.

### Inimical Casting


A Nar Demonbinder knows how to employ spells that deflect the attacks of outsiders, regardless of alignment. When a Nar Demonbinder casts one of the aligned spells (such as Dispel Good) he may choose the adjust the alignment affect at casting time (making it a Dispel Evil instead).

### Iron Sign


On reaching 2nd level, a Nar Demonbinder learns to fashion a special token known as an iron sign. Creating the token requires 3 days and 1000gp in magical materials. The Nar Demonbinder can use it one of two ways: Calling Diagram, or Demon Trap (see p27 for further details).

### Master Summoner

- **BONUS:** VAR|HasMasterSummoner|1

at 3rd level, a Nar Demonbinder gains a +4 bonus on opposed CHA checks to trap or compel creatures through any Planar Binding spell.

### Brazen Sign


On reaching 4th level, a Nar Demonbinder learns to fashion a brazen sign, this requires 5 days and 2000gp worth of materials. (See p27 for further details).

### Infernal Certitude

- **BONUS:** VAR|HasInfernalCertitude|1

Due to his long study of demons, devils, and such creatures, a 5th level Nar Demonbinder gains a +4 bonus on spell penetration checks against any outsider.

### Silver Sign


On reaching 6th level, a Nar Demonbinder learns to fashion a silver sign, this requires 7 days and the expenditure of 5000gp worth of materials. (See p27 for further details).

### Baleful Energy


On attaining 7th level, when a Nar Demonbinder casts a spell that details damage, one-half of that damage dealt is derived directly from infernal power and is therefore not reduced by resistance to the energy type of the damaging spell.

### Sword Focus


A Raumathari Battlemage may preform a special rite to make a single sword into a focus for his spells. The focus sword can then replace any material component of less than 50gp value in the casting of the battlemage's spells. While casting through his focus sword a Raumathari Battlemage gains a +1 caster level. The rite to create a sword focus requires three days and 1000gp in material and a masterwork weapon. Magical swords may be attuned. A battlemage may attune only a single sword at a time.

### Channel Spell


As a free action, %1 time(s) per day, a Raumathari Battlemage can cast a spell through his melee weapon (usually his sword focus), of no higher level than %2, as he attacks with it. He gains an attack bonus equal to the level of the channeled spell for that attack only. The channeled spell counts against the battlemage's normal daily limit.|RaumathariBattlemageChannelUsage|RaumathariBattlemageMaxChannelLevel

### Battle Spell


As a free action, %1 time(s) per day, a Raumathari Battlemage can apply a single metamagic feat to a spell he casts without affecting its casting time or the slot it occupies. At 2nd level he may apply Silent Spell, at 4th level he can apply Still Spell, at 6th level can apply Empower Spell, at 8th level he can apply Quicken Spell, and at 10th level he can apply Maximize Spell.|RaumathariBattlemageBattleSpells

### Runescarring


You can scribe up to 7 scars onto your body (2 each on Chest, Upper Arms, Back of Hands and 1 on the face), each imbued with a spell like ability. Scribing a runescar takes 1 hour and costs (5GP * Spell Lvl * Caster Lvl), and also costs XP equal to 1/25th the GP spent. Each scar deals 1d6 damage per spell level when scribed. It takes a standard action to touch the rune to activate it.

### Scribe Runescar


At 1st level, a runescarred berserker learns how to carve a runescar into her own skin. A runescar is a means of storing a spell, much like a scroll. The berserker knows only a limited number of spells with which to imbue runescars, selected from the runescarred berserker spell list below. To learn a runescar spell, the berserker must have a Wisdom score of at least 10 + the spell level. A runescar berserker can only cast spells by crafting them as runescars; she has no other spellcasting capability and cannot use spell completion or spell trigger magic items based on spells from this list. Scribing a runescar takes 1 hour, the expenditure of materials equal to 5 gp x spell level x caster level, and the expenditure of XP equal to 1/25th the gp spent. The character may set the caster level of the runescar at anywhere from the minimum class level necessary for her to know the rune to her own runescarred berserker class level. She may scribe a runescar only on her own body. The act of scribing a runescar is painful and deals 1d6 points of damage per spell level at the end of the scribing time. (The berserker is free to heal this damage by any normal means afterward-the runescar remains until activated.) A runescarred berserker can bear no more than seven runescars at any one time: two on the chest, two on the upper arms, two on the backs of the hands, and one on the face. Runescars are dormant until activated and cannot be dispelled (although they can be suppressed). Runescars are considered divine spells, although a berserker does not actively prepare or cast them. The save DC, if necessary, is 10 + spell level + the berserker's Wisdom score. To activate a runescar, the character must take a standard action and touch the rune. All runescar spells target only the runescarred berserker or an item in her possession, even if the spell scribed could normally be bestowed on another subject. After activation, the runescar's magic is discharged, and the rune fades to nothing but a faint outline. Typically, a berserker's skin is crisscrossed with dozens of old, discharged runescars.

At 2nd level, she gains one additional rage per day; at 5th level, she gains a second additional rage; and at 9th level, she gains a third additional rage. ### Damage Reduction

- **BONUS:** VAR|BarbarianDRLVL|1
- **BONUS:** VAR|BarbarianDRLVL|1
- **BONUS:** VAR|BarbarianDRLVL|1

Starting at 4th level, a runescarred berserker gains the ability to shrug off some amount of injury from each blow or attack. Subtract 1 from the damage the berserker takes each time she is dealt damage from a weapon or natural attack. At 7th level and 10th level, this damage reduction increases to 2 and then 3 points. This overlaps (does not stack with) damage reduction from any other class feature.

### Greater Rage


Starting at 8th level, a runescarred berserker's rage bonuses become +6 to Strength, +6 to Constitution, and a +3 morale bonus on Will saves. Her AC penalty remains -2.
